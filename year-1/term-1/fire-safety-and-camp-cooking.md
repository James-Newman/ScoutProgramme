# Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** fire and cooking skills are the last big practical building block before the weekend camp, tying together the safety and hygiene habits built in the previous two sessions.
**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award requirement to prepare and light an open fire or set up a suitable stove, and use it to prepare, cook and serve a meal safely.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Good fire, bad fire (15 min)

Build (or photograph in advance) two example fire lays — one done properly (cleared ground, sensible size, water and a bucket of sand nearby, built away from tents and overhanging branches) and one done badly (built on grass, too big, too close to the tent line, no water on hand). Walk the group past both — or show photos if building two fires isn't practical — and get them to call out the differences before you confirm the rules: safe distance from tents, water/extinguishing method on hand at all times, one person in charge of the fire, how to put it out properly afterwards.

### Backwoods biscuits (40 min)

[Backwoods biscuits](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/backwoods-biscuits/) — patrols build and light a star fire (logs arranged like spokes, pushed inward as they burn, which is a naturally good fit for a shorter meeting slot since it burns at a controlled rate), then use it to cook the campfire biscuit dough wrapped around a stick. Assign one fire-lighter and one fire-watcher per patrol at all times, rotating through the patrol so everyone gets a turn at each role.

**Materials:** firewood, matches/firesteels, biscuit dough ingredients, sticks, water/fire bucket.

### Cook it, serve it safely (25 min)

Building directly on the fire just lit, patrols prepare and cook one more simple item (a sausage, a piece of bread, a twist of dough) and then serve it round the patrol properly — plates or clean hands only, no double-dipping, food covered while waiting. This is the actual "prepare, cook, and serve a meal safely" requirement in action, so watch for and correct any hygiene slip-ups (the ones from the previous session's hand-washing game) as they cook.

**Materials:** simple cooking ingredients, plates/tongs.

### Campfire entertainment taster (15 min)

Teach the room one simple camp song or call-and-response chant, then give each patrol five minutes to prepare a 30-second skit or joke to perform at the weekend camp's campfire. This also counts towards the Outdoor Challenge Award's optional-extras list.

### Clear up and recap (5 min)

Douse and check the fire is properly out, tidy the cooking area, and a one-line recap of the safe-distance and water-on-hand rules from earlier.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs an outdoor space cleared for fire lighting, water/fire extinguisher on hand, and a completed fire risk assessment per group policy.
- The campfire entertainment taster also counts towards the Outdoor Challenge Award's optional-extras list alongside the rucksack packing from [Camp Kit & Personal Equipment](camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md) and the menu planning in [Weekend Camp Preparation](weekend-camp-preparation.md).
