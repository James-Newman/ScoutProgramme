# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Camp](camp.md) and earlier sessions.

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term (see [Camp](camp.md)); this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive their Outdoor Challenge Award (Part 1) badge and any activity badges earned this term, against the requirement log finalised at [Camp](camp.md). Any Scout not quite ready can be picked up individually early next term rather than held back here — the Award is completed in full across Part 1 (this term) and Part 2 (Year 4, Term 1), so a gap now isn't final. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall, and let the room vote on a couple of the funnier outcomes. A mix of pure silliness and a nod back to the term's skills works well:

- **Tent-pitch blindfold** — one Scout pitches a small tent solo while blindfolded, patrol shouting instructions from a distance.
- **Mystery kit box** — identify five camp items by touch alone, hands through a curtained box.
- **Tallest tower** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of tent pegs and string in three minutes.
- **Silent charades relay** — a patrol charades relay using only camp/term vocabulary (tent, hygiene, campfire, and so on).
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style — the format does the entertaining, so it doesn't need performance skill from leaders, just a scoreboard and straight-faced delivery of daft rules.

**Materials:** varies by challenge — a small tent, a curtained box of camp items, tent pegs and string, a colander and water, paper and pens.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Term 2: Global Citizens & Conservation.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format (score out of five, deadpan judging) matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape (short badge presentation, then Taskmaster challenges) is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session, the same way [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md) is reused as every opener.
