# Session 3 — Tent Pitching: Theory & Indoor Practice

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** tent pitching is the term's headline campcraft skill; this session builds the understanding needed before Scouts pitch for real outdoors next week.
**Badge focus:** builds towards the Outdoor Challenge Award requirement to pitch and strike a tent with others — the practical sign-off happens in Session 4.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Tent anatomy talk — poles, pegs, guy lines, groundsheet, flysheet |
| 0:25–1:05 | 40 min | [Learn how to pitch a tent](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/teamwork-tents/) — explore the parts, then team up to pitch indoors |
| 1:05–1:25 | 20 min | Patrol relay: correctly identify and assemble poles/pegs against the clock |
| 1:25–1:40 | 15 min | Discussion: choosing ground and orientation against the wind, ahead of next week's outdoor session |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Review and questions |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- One tent per small group of four to six, as recommended by the [Pitch a patrol tent](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/pitch-a-patrol-tent/) activity guidance — they don't need to match.
- Leave plenty of floor space between groups so poles can be handled safely indoors.
