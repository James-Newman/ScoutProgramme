# Badge Review & Sign-Off

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

**Fits the term because:** closes out the term by recognising what's been achieved and formally signing off the term's badge.

**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award (Part 1: Campcraft) sign-off and presentation, against the requirements covered this term:

> "With others, pitch and strike your tent."
>
> "Lead, or help to lead, a group of Scouts to set up a well-organised site. It should include sleeping tents, food and equipment stores, a fire or stove, kitchen and eating area."
>
> "Find out why personal and campsite hygiene is important. What should you do to be hygienic?"
>
> "Find out what accidents and incidents can happen outdoors or during your camp. Show how you would deal with them."
>
> "Prepare and light an open fire or set up a suitable stove. Use it to prepare, cook and serve a meal safely."
>
> Three of the "complete at least four" optional extras: "Show how to pack a rucksack correctly, with appropriate kit for the camp or event." / "Plan a balanced menu for a short camp." / "Take part in a campfire or other entertainment."

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Camp trail trivia (25 min)

A last group recap, played as a quickfire team quiz: split into patrols and fire questions pulled from across the term (tent parts, hygiene rules, first aid kit contents, fire safety), first patrol to buzz in or hands-up gets to answer, points on a simple scoreboard. No props needed beyond a question list and something to keep score on — a whiteboard is enough.

**Materials:** question list, whiteboard for scoring.

### Sign-off stations (40 min)

Split into small groups on a rotation: while most Scouts play supervised games (a quieter continuation of the trivia, or a repeat of an earlier favourite activity), a leader pulls each Scout individually for a short sign-off conversation against the Outdoor Challenge Award requirements checklist, confirming what's been demonstrated across the term and flagging anything to pick up early next term rather than holding the whole group up.

**Materials:** requirements checklist per Scout.

### Camp Olympics final (20 min)

A celebratory rerun of the group's favourite game or two from across the term (tent-peg horseshoes, camp charades, or similar), run as a "grand final" with silly prizes rather than anything competitive-stakes — this is the fun payoff for the term, not more assessment.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

A short, proper ceremony: roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive their Outdoor Challenge Award (Part 1) badge and any activity badges earned this term, patrol applause.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Term 2 teaser (5 min)

A short, mysterious announcement of Term 2's theme — Global Citizens & Conservation — pitched as a trailer rather than an agenda item, to send everyone off curious about what's next.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Any Scout not yet ready to sign off can be picked up individually early in Term 2 rather than held back from the presentation — the Outdoor Challenge Award is completed in full across Part 1 (this term) and Part 2 (Year 4, Term 1), so a gap here isn't final.
- Confirm badge stock and certificates are ordered ahead of this session.
