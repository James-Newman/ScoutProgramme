# Session 7 — Outdoor Safety & Risk Awareness

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** outdoor safety is the third of the term's stated core goals, and sits alongside tent pitching and hygiene as the foundation for everything Scouts do outdoors this term and beyond.
**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award requirement to identify potential outdoor and camping accidents or incidents and demonstrate appropriate responses.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | What is a risk assessment, and why check the weather before heading out |
| 0:20–0:45 | 25 min | [First aid kit Kim's game](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/first-aid-kit-catch-up/) |
| 0:45–1:20 | 35 min | [Emergency scenario bingo](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/emergency-scenario-bingo/) — identifying incidents and the right response |
| 1:20–1:40 | 20 min | [Weather wear](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/weather-wear/) — dressing appropriately for the conditions |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Recap and personal safety checklist for camp |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- The First Aid Kit activity is explicitly designed to run in the weeks leading up to a camp or night away — this slot, three weeks before the weekend camp, is a good fit.
- If time is short, [Practice calling 999 for first aid scenarios](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/practice-calling-999-for-first-aid-scenarios/) is a good substitute for the scenario bingo — same requirement, different format.
