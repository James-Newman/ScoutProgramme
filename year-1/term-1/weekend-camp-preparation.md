# Weekend Camp Preparation

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

**Fits the term because:** this is where everything the term has built — kit, pitching, site layout, hygiene, safety, cooking — comes together into a concrete plan for the optional weekend camp.

**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award optional extra — menu planning. It's one of the "complete at least four" tasks, and this session also rehearses the pitching, site layout, and kit skills from earlier sessions that the camp itself will draw on.

> "Plan a balanced menu for a short camp."

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Mission briefing (15 min)

Frame the whole session as a "mission briefing" rather than admin — dates and location on the wall map, then patrol roles (patrol leader, cook, quartermaster, fire officer) drawn out of a hat rather than assigned, so it feels like a lottery rather than a chore list. Each Scout who draws a role gets a small printed role card explaining what it means at camp.

**Materials:** wall map, role cards.

### Plan your camp menu (30 min)

[Plan your camp menu](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/plan-your-camp-menu/) — patrols draft a menu for the weekend, check it against allergies and dietary requirements gathered back in [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md), then pitch their plan to the room. The room votes for favourite dishes from each patrol's plan to build a single master menu, so every patrol sees at least one of their ideas make the final cut.

**Materials:** menu planning sheets.

### Map and string route walk-through (20 min)

Pin up a map of the hike route on the wall and trace it with a piece of string or wool, marking key points (start, a landmark, the turnaround or end point) with pins. Get a couple of Scouts to physically walk the string route with a finger while narrating what they'd expect to see, rather than just talking through it yourself — it's a small thing but keeps it hands-on rather than a briefing.

**Materials:** route map, string/wool, pins.

### Final kit check (25 min)

Hand back the patrol kit checklists from [Camp Kit & Personal Equipment](camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md) and run it as a scavenger-hunt-style check: call out an item, patrols race to confirm (or produce, if it's something they'd bring tonight) who's responsible for bringing it. Anything missing gets assigned to someone on the spot.

### Questions and logistics (10 min)

Open floor for questions, hand out and collect consent forms, confirm pickup/drop-off details.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- The weekend camp itself is scheduled separately by leaders and isn't tied to a specific session number in this term (see the term [overview](overview.md)) — this session should be timed to land shortly before it, whenever it falls, even if that means moving its place in the running order.
- Check allergies and dietary requirements gathered in [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) before finalising the menu.
