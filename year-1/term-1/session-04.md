# Session 4 — Tent Pitching: Outdoor Practical

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** this is where the term's core campcraft skill gets proven for real, outdoors, ahead of the weekend camp.
**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award requirement — "with others, pitch and strike your tent." This session is where that requirement is practically demonstrated and logged.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Recap indoor learning; outdoor safety brief (ground hazards, tool safety around pegs and mallets) |
| 0:20–0:55 | 35 min | [Pitch a patrol tent](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/pitch-a-patrol-tent/) — full outdoor pitch in patrols |
| 0:55–1:20 | 25 min | [Learn to pitch a mess tent](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/learn-to-pitch-a-mess-tent/) — group task on the larger shared tent |
| 1:20–1:40 | 20 min | Timed strike (take-down) and pack-away relay |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Debrief — what went well, what was tricky; leaders log requirement progress per Scout |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- Needs outdoor space (field, garden, or campsite) and good weather contingency — if postponed, swap with Session 5 rather than cutting it, since it's the requirement sign-off session.
- Have spare tents rigged nearby in case of breakages so no patrol loses practice time.
