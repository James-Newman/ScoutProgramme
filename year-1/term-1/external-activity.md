# External Activity: Scout Activity Centre Visit

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

**Fits the term because:** gives Scouts a proper campsite and instructor support to go deeper on this term's fire and site-craft skills than a meeting-hall garden allows.

**Badge focus:** deepens the Outdoor Challenge Award requirements already covered in [Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep](fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md) and [Site Layout & Design](site-layout-design.md), and can bank one more of the "complete at least four" optional extras depending on what the centre offers (backwoods cooking fits this term well).

> "Cook a backwoods meal with the group."

This session replaces the normal hall meeting with a booked visit to a local Scout Activity Centre or similar external, instructor-led venue. Timings depend entirely on the venue and travel — treat the running order below as a shape to fit around what's actually booked, not a fixed script.

## Running order (example, half-day)

| When | Block |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival | Welcome from centre staff, site safety briefing |
| Session 1 | Instructor-led activity — e.g. backwoods cooking, pioneering, or an extended fire-lighting session on a real fire circle |
| Break | Food, toilets |
| Session 2 | Second instructor-led activity, or free/patrol-led practice time on the centre's facilities |
| Departure | Pack up, thanks to centre staff, head count |

## Session detail

### Booking and choosing an activity

Contact a local Scout Activity Centre (search [scoutadventures.org.uk](https://www.scoutadventures.org.uk/) or the district/county activity centre list) two to three months ahead — a half-day slot for one troop is often booked up quickly. Ask what's included in an instructor-led session; backwoods cooking and pioneering both fit this term's theme directly, and either counts towards the optional-extras list.

### On the day

Leaders take a supporting role here — the value of this session is the change of scenery, proper facilities (a real fire circle, more space, purpose-built equipment), and hearing the same messages (fire safety, hygiene, teamwork) from a different voice.

## Notes

- If no activity centre is within reach, a visit from an external instructor to the normal meeting place (e.g. a local Scout Active Support unit running a bushcraft taster) is a reasonable substitute — the point is a change of format and expertise, not necessarily a change of venue.
- Costs, transport, and any additional consent forms need sorting well ahead of the term starting — this is the one session in the term that depends on an external, bookable resource.
