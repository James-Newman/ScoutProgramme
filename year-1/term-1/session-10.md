# Session 10 — Practical Skills Recap

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** revisits every core skill from the term — pitching, hygiene, first aid awareness, kit — as a single circuit, closing any gaps before the badge sign-offs in Session 12.
**Badge focus:** no new requirement — this session consolidates the Outdoor Challenge Award requirements already covered in Sessions 4 through 8.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Recap framing — what the circuit covers and why |
| 0:20–1:20 | 60 min | Patrol relay circuit: timed tent-pitch station, hygiene quiz station, first aid kit ID station, kit-packing station |
| 1:20–1:35 | 15 min | [Keep your kit fit](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/keep-your-kit-fit/) — kit maintenance check-in |
| 1:35–1:50 | 15 min | Team reflection — what still needs practice before sign-off |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- Whether this lands before or after the weekend camp depends on when the camp itself is scheduled — either way it works as a recap, so no changes are needed if the camp date shifts.
- Use the reflection at the end to flag individual Scouts who need extra one-to-one time in Session 11 or 12.
