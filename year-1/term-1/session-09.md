# Session 9 — Weekend Camp Preparation

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** this is where everything the term has built — kit, pitching, site layout, hygiene, safety, cooking — comes together into a concrete plan for the optional weekend camp.
**Badge focus:** contributes to the Outdoor Challenge Award's optional-extras list (menu planning), and rehearses the pitching, site layout, and kit skills from earlier sessions that the camp itself will draw on.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Camp overview — dates, logistics, patrol roles (patrol leader, cook, quartermaster) |
| 0:25–0:55 | 30 min | [Plan your camp menu](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/plan-your-camp-menu/) |
| 0:55–1:15 | 20 min | Walk-through of the accompanying short hike route |
| 1:15–1:40 | 25 min | Final kit check and rucksack repack, revisiting Session 2 |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Questions, consent forms, and other logistics reminders |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- The weekend camp itself is scheduled separately by leaders and isn't tied to a specific session number in this term (see the term [overview](overview.md)) — this session should be timed to land shortly before it, whenever it falls, even if that means swapping its place in the running order.
- Check allergies and dietary requirements gathered in Session 6 before finalising the menu.
