# Session 1 — Term Kickoff & Safety Briefing

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** sets up patrols, the term's badge goals, and the outdoor safety expectations that every later campcraft session depends on.
**Badge focus:** no specific Outdoor Challenge Award requirement — this session lays the safety groundwork the rest of the term builds on, and introduces the requirements Scouts will be working towards.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Patrols reformed for the year; term overview and the badges on offer |
| 0:25–0:40 | 15 min | Icebreaker: [Try the human knot game](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/try-the-human-knot-game/) |
| 0:40–1:00 | 20 min | Outdoor safety induction — always tell a leader, the buddy system, what to do if lost or hurt |
| 1:00–1:20 | 20 min | Outdoor Challenge Award explained; requirements checklist posted in the meeting place; patrols set a term goal |
| 1:20–1:50 | 30 min | Campsite-themed patrol games (tent-peg beanbag relay, camp charades) |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- The Outdoor Challenge Award requirements referenced across this term's sessions are drawn from the [Scouts Outdoor Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/awards/outdoor/) page — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since the Scout Association updates requirement wording from time to time.
- Keep the requirements checklist visible in the meeting place for the rest of the term so Scouts can see their own progress.
