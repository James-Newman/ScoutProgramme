# Practical Skills Recap

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

**Fits the term because:** revisits every core skill from the term — pitching, hygiene, first aid awareness, kit — as a single circuit, closing any gaps before the badge sign-offs at the end of term.

**Badge focus:** no new requirement — consolidates the requirements already covered in [Tent Pitching: Outdoor Practical](tent-pitching-outdoor-practical.md), [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md), [Outdoor Safety & Risk Awareness](outdoor-safety-and-risk-awareness.md), and [Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep](fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md).

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Circuit briefing |
| 0:20–1:20 | 60 min | Patrol mini-Olympics circuit |
| 1:20–1:35 | 15 min | Keep your kit fit |
| 1:35–1:50 | 15 min | Sticky-note wall |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Circuit briefing (10 min)

Frame the next hour as a "mini-Olympics" — four stations, patrols rotate through all four, points scored at each, medals (or just bragging rights) at the end. Explain the scoring briefly and get moving quickly; the framing does more for engagement than a long explanation would.

### Patrol mini-Olympics circuit (60 min)

Four stations, roughly 15 minutes each, patrols rotating through in sequence:

1. **Tent-pitch speed round** — pitch and strike against the clock, scored on both time and correctness.
2. **Hygiene quiz station** — a quickfire true/false or picture round drawing on the [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) content.
3. **First aid kit ID station** — a repeat of the Kim's game format from [Outdoor Safety & Risk Awareness](outdoor-safety-and-risk-awareness.md), same kit, different items missing.
4. **Kit-packing station** — race to correctly pack a rucksack, drawing on [Camp Kit & Personal Equipment](camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md).

Run it with a stopwatch and a scoreboard on the wall — the competitive framing is what makes an hour of "revision" feel like a session in its own right rather than a chore.

**Materials:** tents/pegs, hygiene quiz sheets, first aid kit and tray, rucksack and kit pile, scoreboard.

### Keep your kit fit (15 min)

[Keep your kit fit](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/keep-your-kit-fit/) — a check-in on maintaining kit inventories (size, brand, detachable parts, where it lives at home), a useful lower-energy activity after the circuit.

### Sticky-note wall (15 min)

Each Scout writes one thing they feel confident about and one thing they'd like more practice on, on two different coloured sticky notes, and adds them to a wall chart. Read a few out loud (with permission) to close — this feeds directly into what gets picked up in the camp debrief and consolidation session.

**Materials:** two colours of sticky notes, wall chart.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Whether this lands before or after the weekend camp depends on when the camp itself is scheduled (see [Weekend Camp Preparation](weekend-camp-preparation.md)) — either way it works as a recap, so no changes are needed if the camp date shifts.
- Use the sticky-note wall to flag individual Scouts who need extra one-to-one time in later sessions.
