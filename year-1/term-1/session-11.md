# Session 11 — Camp Debrief & Skills Consolidation

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** turns the weekend camp experience into learning, addressing gaps before the term's badge work is signed off.
**Badge focus:** no new requirement — closes out any Outdoor Challenge Award requirements from Sessions 2–8 that individual Scouts haven't yet demonstrated.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Circle time — how camp and the hike went, patrol feedback round |
| 0:30–1:10 | 40 min | Re-practice whatever came up as tricky in the feedback (tent pegs, fire lighting, packing, etc.) |
| 1:10–1:25 | 15 min | Photos and journal entries for the troop scrapbook (with consent) |
| 1:25–1:40 | 15 min | Leaders check in individually with any Scout who has requirement gaps |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Preview Session 12's badge sign-off |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- If the weekend camp hasn't happened yet by this point in the term, swap this session with Session 10 or delay it — it depends on the camp having taken place.
- The 40-minute re-practice block is deliberately unscripted — its content depends entirely on what the group found tricky, so plan the specific activity once the debrief has happened.
