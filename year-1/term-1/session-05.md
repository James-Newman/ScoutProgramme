# Session 5 — Campsite Selection & Site Layout

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** pitching a single tent is only half the skill — this session scales that up to laying out a whole patrol site, which is what Scouts will actually do at the weekend camp.
**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award requirement to lead, or help lead, a group to set up a well-organised site including sleeping tents, food and equipment stores, a fire or stove, kitchen, and eating area.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Discussion: what makes a good campsite, using [Camping standards](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/camping-standards/) guidance |
| 0:25–0:45 | 20 min | Photo-card sort: good pitch vs. bad pitch, spotting drainage, ground, and wind problems |
| 0:45–1:20 | 35 min | Patrol task: sketch a full site layout — sleeping tents, stores, fire/stove, kitchen, eating area |
| 1:20–1:40 | 20 min | Patrols present their layouts to each other; peer feedback |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Link to the weekend camp — walk through the real site plan if it's known yet |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- See [Plan a perfect Scout camp](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/programme-planning-pros/) for further site-planning ideas if patrols need a steer.
- Bring squared paper or a whiteboard per patrol for the layout sketches.
