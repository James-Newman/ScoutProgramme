# Night Hike

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Community & Global Citizenship

**Fits the term because:** every term includes one Night Hike regardless of theme — it's this term's Hikes Away opportunity, and (per the standing convention for every term's Night Hike) the home for the Outdoor Challenge Award's Countryside Code requirement, which accumulates passively regardless of the term's own focus badge. As a loose (non-required) tie-in, the route can pass local landmarks a patrol found out about in [Community Research](community-research.md), if that lines up conveniently.

**Badge focus:** none for World Challenge Award — but this session is the standing home for the passive Outdoor Challenge Award Countryside Code requirement (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)) and this term's optional Hikes Away (1) staged-progression opportunity. It's also the standing home for Navigator Staged Activity Badge's real navigated routes (see [`filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md`](../../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md)/[`navigator-stage-4.md`](../../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-4.md)) — but since neither of those has been slotted into a term yet, no Scout should be expected to have the map-reading knowledge for a genuine navigating turn this early. If any Scout already does (a keen self-starter, or prior experience), give them the lead for part of the route; otherwise treat this first hike the same as Camp's first outing — leader-led, with real navigating turns to follow once the Navigator filler sessions have actually run.

> "Understand the three points of the Countryside Code. Show what action you can take to follow the code."

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with an evening/early-night walk, typically 6:30–9:00pm depending on sunset. Treat the running order below as a shape to adjust to local daylight and route length, not a fixed script.

## Running order (example, ~2.5 hours)

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Muster & kit check |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Route & safety brief |
| 0:30–1:30 | 60 min | The hike out |
| 1:30–1:50 | 20 min | Halfway stop |
| 1:50–2:30 | 40 min | The hike back |
| 2:30–2:45 | 15 min | Debrief & collection |

## Session detail

### Muster & kit check (10 min)

Meet at the hall (or the walk's start point), register patrols, and run a quick kit check per Scout — torch with spare batteries, footwear and coat suitable for the forecast, whistle, water. Confirm buddy pairs.

### Route & safety brief (20 min)

Walk the route on a map with the whole group before setting off — start point, a landmark or two, the halfway stop, the return leg. Since this term doesn't have a dedicated outdoor-safety session to draw on, cover the essentials directly here: the buddy system (stick with your buddy, tell a leader if you need to step away), and what to do if separated from the group (stay where you are, stay calm, use a whistle — three short blasts — to signal for help).

Since the route crosses real countryside, this is also the natural place for the Countryside Code: leave gates as you find them, stick to paths, take litter home, keep dogs (if any come along) under control, be considerate of other people out at night. Ask the group what they'd do in a couple of quick scenarios (a gate left open, litter on the path) rather than just reading the code out. Official leaflets/posters are free to download from [gov.uk/countryside-code](https://www.gov.uk/countryside-code).

**Materials:** route map, torches, whistles.

### The hike out (60 min)

Walk the outbound leg in patrols, leaders spread front, middle, and back.

### Halfway stop (20 min)

A short break at a pre-scouted spot — hot chocolate from a flask, a quiet torch-lit game ([Kim's game](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/play-kims-game/) with a single torch beam works well in the dark), a head-count.

### The hike back (40 min)

Return leg, ideally a different route or direction so it doesn't feel like a straight there-and-back. Rotate who's leading at the front of each patrol.

### Debrief & collection (15 min)

Head-count, a quick "what went well" round while parents arrive, and a reminder that this counts towards Hikes Away progression.

## Notes

- Needs a pre-walked, risk-assessed route and a completed risk assessment for night activities per group policy — see [Scouts example risk assessments](https://www.scouts.org.uk/volunteers/staying-safe-and-safeguarding/risk-assessments/example-risk-assessments/).
- Adult:young person ratios, hi-vis, and torch requirements should follow the group's own safety policy for night activities.
- Counts towards this term's Hikes Away (1) staged progression (see [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml)).
- Leaders should note engagement with the Countryside Code scenarios as the evidence for that Outdoor Challenge Award requirement (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)).
- Leaders should also note whether any Scout got a genuine navigating turn this hike, as the evidence for Navigator Staged Activity Badge's real-route requirement — not expected this early in the programme, but worth recording if it happens.
