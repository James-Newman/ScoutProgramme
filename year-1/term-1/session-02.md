# Session 2 — Camp Kit & Personal Equipment

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** before Scouts can pitch a tent or cook on camp, they need to know what to bring and how to carry it — the practical starting point for the term's campcraft focus.
**Badge focus:** contributes to the Outdoor Challenge Award's optional-extras list (rucksack packing), one of the four extras Scouts need to complete across their time working on the award.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Talk-through of a personal camp kit list, and why each item matters |
| 0:25–0:55 | 30 min | [Practice packing an expedition rucksack](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/practice-packing-an-expedition-rucksack/) |
| 0:55–1:20 | 25 min | [Everything you need, nothing you don't](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/everything-you-need-nothing-you-don-t/) |
| 1:20–1:40 | 20 min | Kit inspection and labelling — naming kit, checking it's autumn-camp-ready |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Patrols draw up their own kit checklist for the weekend camp |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- Bring a selection of rucksacks and camp kit (borrowed group kit is fine) for the packing practice.
- The patrol kit checklists produced here feed into Session 9's final kit check before the optional weekend camp.
