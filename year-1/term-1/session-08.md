# Session 8 — Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** fire and cooking skills are the last big practical building block before the weekend camp, tying together the safety and hygiene habits built in Sessions 6 and 7.
**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award requirement to prepare and light an open fire or set up a suitable stove, and use it to prepare, cook and serve a meal safely.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Activity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome — notices, patrol flags, opening |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Fire safety talk — open fire vs. stove, safe distances, putting a fire out properly |
| 0:25–1:05 | 40 min | [Backwoods biscuits](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/backwoods-biscuits/) — building a star fire and lighting it |
| 1:05–1:30 | 25 min | Cooking safely on the fire — preparing, cooking, and serving a simple item |
| 1:30–1:45 | 15 min | Campfire entertainment taster — songs and skits for the weekend camp evening |
| 1:45–1:50 | 5 min | Clear up and fire safety recap |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close — notices, thought for the week, home |

## Notes

- Needs an outdoor space cleared for fire lighting, water/fire extinguisher on hand, and a completed fire risk assessment per group policy.
- The campfire entertainment taster also counts towards the Outdoor Challenge Award's optional-extras list alongside the rucksack packing from Session 2 and the menu planning in Session 9.
