# Year 1, Term 1 (Autumn) — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 1, term 1) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: tent pitching, basic camp hygiene, and outdoor safety.
Badges earned: **Outdoor Challenge Award (Part 1: Campcraft)**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (2), Hikes Away (1).

This is the itemised 12-session breakdown for the term's weekly meetings, each running the standard 2-hour evening (10 min welcome, 1 hour 40 core content, 10 min close). Each session's plan links to real activities from [scouts.org.uk/activities](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/) and states which [Outdoor Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/awards/outdoor/) requirement, if any, it targets — check the live award page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

The optional weekend camp that carries the Nights Away and Hikes Away progression is scheduled separately by leaders during or around the term and isn't tied to a specific session below, so it can move without making this breakdown wrong. [Weekend Camp Preparation](weekend-camp-preparation.md) and [Camp Debrief & Skills Consolidation](camp-debrief-and-skills-consolidation.md) are best run either side of it, whenever it falls.

The remaining Outdoor Challenge Award requirements are picked up later in the programme, principally in Year 4, Term 1 (Part 2: Wilderness & Consolidation):

> "Take an active part in at least eight nights away as a Scout. Four of the nights should be camping."
>
> "Understand the three points of the Countryside Code. Show what action you can take to follow the code."
>
> "Using knots that you have learned, build a simple pioneering project, object or camp gadget."
>
> "Explore the environment of your camp and make sure you know where everything is. Respect the environment you are in and, at the end of the camp, leave the site as you found it."
>
> "Show how to use an axe, saw or knife safely. You can choose any or all of these tools."
