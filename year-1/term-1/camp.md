# Camp

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Community & Global Citizenship

**Fits the term because:** every term includes one Camp regardless of theme — it's this term's Nights Away opportunity, and (per the standing convention for every term's Camp) the home for two Outdoor Challenge Award requirements that accumulate passively regardless of the term's own focus badge. Since this is the very first term, patrols haven't had any dedicated tent-pitching/campcraft session yet (that's covered by [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md), not yet slotted into any term), so this camp is more leader-supported than a later one would be.

**Badge focus:** none for World Challenge Award — but this session is the standing home for two passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirements (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)) and this term's Nights Away (2) staged-progression opportunity.

> "Take an active part in at least eight nights away as a Scout. Four of the nights should be camping."

> "Explore the environment of your camp and make sure you know where everything is. Respect the environment you are in and, at the end of the camp, leave the site as you found it."

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with a residential weekend (typically Friday evening to Sunday afternoon). Adjust the running order to the site and travel involved.

## Running order (example, Friday evening – Sunday)

| When | Block |
| --- | --- |
| Friday, arrival | Arrive, pitch camp with leader support, settle in |
| Friday evening | Informal games, campfire chat |
| Saturday | Patrol-led activities, games, free time |
| Saturday evening | Cook and serve dinner as a patrol; campfire with an international flavour |
| Sunday morning | Strike camp, patrol chores, pack away |
| Sunday, before departure | Debrief circle |

## Session detail

### Friday: arrival and settle-in

Since tent pitching hasn't been formally taught yet, leaders take a more hands-on role helping patrols pitch — this is a good early exposure to the skill ahead of whichever future session covers it properly, not a test of it. Once camp is up, walk each patrol round the whole site — where the toilets are, the site boundary, the nearest water point, anything to avoid — so they genuinely know where everything is before dark. That's the "know where everything is" half of the passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirement above.

### Saturday: camp in full swing

Patrol-led activities, games, chores rota — a fairly standard camp day. If it fits naturally, a short activity connecting back to the term's theme works well here (a "flags of the world" patrol quiz, or cooking a dish inspired by a country a patrol researched in [International Issues & Connections](international-issues-and-connections.md)), but this is a nice-to-have, not a requirement.

### Saturday evening: cook, serve, campfire

Patrols cook and serve their evening meal, then a campfire to finish the day. Since the term's had an international/global flavour, songs or short pieces from other countries' Scouting traditions are a fun, low-effort tie-in — the [World Scout Movement](https://www.scout.org/) has plenty to draw on.

### Sunday: strike camp and debrief

Patrols strike and pack away camp (with leader support as needed), then walk their pitch before leaving — litter picked, ground checked, nothing left that wasn't there Friday. That's the "leave the site as you found it" half of the passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirement, worth every patrol doing themselves rather than one group tidying up after everyone. Finish with a short debrief circle before parents arrive: what went well, what was hard, one thing each patrol would do differently next time.

## Notes

- Needs a completed camp risk assessment and site booking well ahead of the term — see [Scouts example risk assessments](https://www.scouts.org.uk/volunteers/staying-safe-and-safeguarding/risk-assessments/example-risk-assessments/).
- Counts towards this term's Nights Away (2) staged progression (see [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml)), and — combined with every other term's Camp — towards the Outdoor Challenge Award's Nights Away requirement (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)).
- Full campcraft skill-building (tent pitching, site layout, fire safety) doesn't have a dedicated term — it's covered by standalone sessions in [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md), slotted in wherever a future term has room. Keep expectations for this first camp modest and leader-supported until some of those have happened.
