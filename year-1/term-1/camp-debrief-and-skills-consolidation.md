# Camp Debrief & Skills Consolidation

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills

**Fits the term because:** turns the weekend camp experience into learning, addressing gaps before the term's badge work is signed off.

**Badge focus:** no new requirement — closes out any of this term's Outdoor Challenge Award requirements that individual Scouts haven't yet demonstrated.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Rose, thorn, bud |
| 0:30–1:10 | 40 min | Fix the tricky bit |
| 1:10–1:25 | 15 min | Scrapbook corner |
| 1:25–1:40 | 15 min | One-to-one check-ins |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Sign-off session preview |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Rose, thorn, bud (20 min)

Run patrol feedback as "rose, thorn, bud": each Scout names one thing that went well (rose), one thing that was hard or went wrong (thorn), and one thing they're looking forward to trying next (bud). Go round patrols rather than the whole circle at once so it doesn't drag, and have a leader jot down the "thorns" — they're the input for the next block.

### Fix the tricky bit (40 min)

This block is deliberately unscripted, because its content depends entirely on what came out of the debrief and the sticky-note wall from [Practical Skills Recap](practical-skills-recap.md): set up two or three short practical stations targeting whatever came up most (tent pegs not holding, fire lighting taking too long, rucksacks packed wrong) and let patrols rotate through the ones relevant to them. Plan the specific stations once the debrief has happened, not in advance.

### Scrapbook corner (15 min)

Patrols put together a page of camp photos (with consent), tickets, or written highlights for a troop scrapbook or noticeboard — a lower-energy, creative way to consolidate the weekend while the practical stations wind down for other patrols.

**Materials:** printed camp photos, scrapbook/noticeboard, glue/pens.

### One-to-one check-ins (15 min)

While most of the group is on the scrapbook or a quiet game, a leader pulls individual Scouts aside briefly to talk through any Outdoor Challenge Award requirement gaps flagged during the term — this is a conversation, not a test, and sets them up to be ready for the sign-off session.

### Sign-off session preview (10 min)

Tease what's coming: the requirements checklist will get one final group recap, then individual sign-offs and the badge presentation. Build a bit of anticipation rather than just stating the agenda.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- If the weekend camp hasn't happened yet by this point in the term, swap this session with [Practical Skills Recap](practical-skills-recap.md) or delay it — it depends on the camp having taken place.
