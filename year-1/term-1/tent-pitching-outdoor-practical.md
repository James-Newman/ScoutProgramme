# Tent Pitching: Outdoor Practical

**Term:** Year 1, Term 1 — Campcraft & Outdoor Skills
**Fits the term because:** this is where the term's core campcraft skill gets proven for real, outdoors, ahead of the weekend camp.
**Badge focus:** Outdoor Challenge Award requirement — "with others, pitch and strike your tent." This session is where that requirement is practically demonstrated and logged.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Recap and tool safety brief |
| 0:20–0:55 | 35 min | Pitch a patrol tent |
| 0:55–1:20 | 25 min | Learn to pitch a mess tent |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Recap and tool safety brief (10 min)

Quick verbal recap of last week's naming and would-you-camp-here rounds — cold-call a few Scouts rather than reviewing it yourself. Then a short, concrete tool safety brief for mallets and pegs: point end down and away from feet when carrying, one Scout swings at a time with everyone else's hands clear, mallet handed over (not thrown) between Scouts. Demonstrate the "safety triangle" — everyone else stands back at arm's-length-plus while a peg is driven in.

### Pitch a patrol tent (35 min)

[Pitch a patrol tent](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/pitch-a-patrol-tent/) — full outdoor pitch in patrols, on real ground, applying last week's ground-and-wind lessons to choose their spot. Commentate on it like a sports event as they go ("Beaver patrol going for the classic four-corner start, Fox patrol trying something ambitious with the guy lines") — a bit of theatre keeps the energy up over what's otherwise a fairly technical 35 minutes. This is the practical evidence for the Outdoor Challenge Award's pitch-and-strike requirement, so note which Scouts actively pitched (not just stood nearby) as you go round.

**Materials:** one tent per patrol, mallets, ground sheet.

### Learn to pitch a mess tent (25 min)

[Learn to pitch a mess tent](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/learn-to-pitch-a-mess-tent/) — a group task on the larger shared tent, which needs more coordination than a patrol tent. Assign explicit roles before starting (pole team, peg team, guy-line team, caller) and rotate them halfway through so everyone gets a go at the "caller" role of directing the team.

**Materials:** mess tent, mallets.

### Timed strike relay (20 min)

Reverse the process: patrols race to strike (take down) and correctly pack away their tent from this session, competing on both speed and whether it's packed correctly (a badly rolled tent that won't fit its bag doesn't count as done). Award points for both.

### Debrief and sign-off log (10 min)

Quick huddle per patrol: what went well, what was fiddly. Leaders note against each Scout's name whether they've demonstrated pitch-and-strike to a standard you're happy to log towards the Award.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs outdoor space (field, garden, or campsite) and good weather contingency — if postponed, swap with [Campsite Selection & Site Layout](campsite-selection-and-site-layout.md) rather than cutting it, since it's the requirement sign-off session.
- Have spare tents rigged nearby in case of breakages so no patrol loses practice time.
