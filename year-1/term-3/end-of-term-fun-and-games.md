# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 1, Term 3 — Fire & Cooking

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Menu Planning, Shopping & Storage](menu-planning-shopping-and-storage.md), [Camp](camp.md), [Naturalist](../../filler-sessions/naturalist.md), [Air Spotter](../../filler-sessions/air-spotter.md), and [Writer](../../filler-sessions/writer.md).

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive every badge they've completed in full this term: **Chef Activity Badge** (against the two-course meal cooked and served at Camp), **Naturalist Activity Badge**, **Air Spotter Activity Badge**, and **Writer Activity Badge** (for anyone who completed those filler sessions). Any Scout not quite ready can be picked up individually early next term. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **[Blindfold taste test](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/crowdie-or-cranachan/)** — guess the ingredient, blindfolded.
- **Fastest sandwich relay** — race to assemble a simple sandwich correctly, one ingredient at a time, relay-style.
- **Menu pitch** — 60 seconds to pitch the most ridiculous imaginary two-course menu to the room.
- **[Tallest tower](https://www.icebreakerspot.com/activities/newspaper-tower-challenge)** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style.

**Materials:** simple food items for the taste test, sandwich ingredients, paper and tape, a colander and water, pens.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Year 2, Term 1: Creative Challenge.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session.
