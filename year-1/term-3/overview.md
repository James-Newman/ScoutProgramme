# Year 1, Term 3 (Summer) — Fire & Cooking

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 1, term 3) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: menu planning, shopping and food storage, cooking and serving a two-course meal, and safe clear-up and waste management.
Badges earned: **Chef Activity Badge**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Time on the Water.

Every term's 12 weeks follow the same shape: one [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md), one [Night Hike](night-hike.md), one [Camp](camp.md), one [External Activity](external-activity.md), and one [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) session — five fixed slots every term, regardless of theme. That leaves seven weeks for the term's own badge content.

**This term needs only 2 of those 7 core-content weeks for Chef Activity Badge itself, since the badge's fifth requirement resolves at Camp.** [Menu Planning, Shopping & Storage](menu-planning-shopping-and-storage.md) combines three requirements in one session (planning a two-course menu, shopping/transport, and food storage/hygiene), and [Camp](camp.md) itself is where the real substance happens — cooking and serving the planned menu, then clearing up, exactly as the badge's own wording allows ("prepared on an open fire on camp").

**That leaves 5 of the term's 7 core-content weeks free**, and this term uses them well beyond just filling time. Three go to real, targeted Outdoor Challenge Award progress that sets Camp up properly: [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) (which also doubles as a passive nudge to confirm dietary needs ahead of menu planning), [Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md) (teaches the fire and knife skills Camp's cooking actually needs — and is itself a strong passive match for Chef's cooking requirement, per the note in its own file), and [Site Layout & Design](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/site-layout-design.md) (building on [Campsite Selection](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/campsite-selection.md) from Year 1, Term 2, and best run just before an actual camp — exactly the timing here). The remaining two weeks carry [Naturalist](../../filler-sessions/naturalist.md) and [Air Spotter](../../filler-sessions/air-spotter.md) — complete, standalone badges in their own right, chosen simply because there was room and they hadn't been used yet — plus [Writer](../../filler-sessions/writer.md) fills what would otherwise be a sixth free week, since Chef's own light footprint (2 sessions + Camp) leaves genuine spare capacity this term.

## Requirement coverage

All 5 Chef Activity Badge requirements are covered this term:

> "Plan a menu for a weekend event for between four and six people. Include the quantities you'll need and any dietary needs of the group, and think about how your choices can help to minimise food waste." — [Menu Planning, Shopping & Storage](menu-planning-shopping-and-storage.md)
>
> "Show how and where to shop for the food and the best way to transport it." — [Menu Planning, Shopping & Storage](menu-planning-shopping-and-storage.md)
>
> "Show how to store food properly and hygienically to prevent food poisoning and keep it fresh for longer." — [Menu Planning, Shopping & Storage](menu-planning-shopping-and-storage.md)
>
> "Cook and serve at least one meal from your menu, including at least two courses." — [Camp](camp.md)
>
> "Demonstrate how to clear up and manage leftovers safely and responsibly." — [Camp](camp.md)

Source: [Scouts Chef Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/chef/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

## Passive completion — other badges this term's content touches

## Advance preparation

Pulled from each session's own Notes — everything a leader needs to organise ahead of running this term, not just on the day.

### Bookings & external arrangements

- **[External Activity](external-activity.md)** — book well ahead, since popular slots get booked up. A water-based option fits this term's optional Time on the Water progression well.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** site booking — well ahead of the term.

### Risk assessments

- **[Night Hike](night-hike.md)** — a pre-walked, risk-assessed route, plus a completed risk assessment for night activities.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — a completed camp risk assessment.
- **[Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md)** — a fire risk assessment and a knife-use risk assessment covering ratios and supervision; check the group's own minimum age/leader-ratio policy for knife use.
- **[Naturalist](../../filler-sessions/naturalist.md)** — confirm access to a suitable green space; weather-dependent with no clean indoor substitute.

### Content to source or prepare carefully

- **[Menu Planning, Shopping & Storage](menu-planning-shopping-and-storage.md)** — cross-reference each patrol's dietary needs/allergies against consent-form records for accuracy; [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) is a good moment to confirm these too.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — each patrol needs to actually do their own shopping ahead of Camp, based on what they planned — confirm this happens, it isn't automatic.

### Equipment & materials to source

See the [material inventory](#material-inventory) below for the full list — the standouts needing advance sourcing are a UV lamp and fluorescent lotion/powder (Camp Hygiene & Food Safety), fixed-blade knives and firewood (Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep), and an aircraft picture pack (Air Spotter, reused from Year 1 Term 1 if already built).

### Stock to order

Four badges complete in full this term — order stock for all four from the [Scout Store](https://shop.scouts.org.uk/) ahead of [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md):

- **Chef Activity Badge** — this term's own focus badge.
- **Naturalist Activity Badge**, **Air Spotter Activity Badge**, **Writer Activity Badge** — completed via their filler sessions.

**Not this term:** Outdoor Challenge Award makes real progress (hygiene, fire/knife safety, site layout, Nights Away, environment) but remains passive/accumulated — not a badge to stock yet.
