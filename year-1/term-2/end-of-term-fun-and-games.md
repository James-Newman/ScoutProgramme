# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 1, Term 2 — First Aid & Emergency Response

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md), [Bleeding, Burns & Choking](bleeding-burns-and-choking.md), [Heat, Cold & Breathing](heat-cold-and-breathing.md), [Emergency Aid Scenario Practice](emergency-aid-scenario-practice.md), and [Communicator](../../filler-sessions/communicator-activity-badge.md).

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive every badge they've completed in full this term: **Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3)** (against the evidence built up across the term's four Emergency Aid sessions) and **Communicator Activity Badge** (for anyone who completed that filler session). Any Scout not quite ready can be picked up individually early next term rather than held back here. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **Bandage relay** — race to correctly bandage a "wound" (a balloon taped to an arm) using a roll of bandage, judged on speed and whether it stays on.
- **999 charades** — mime an emergency scenario, patrol guesses what's happened.
- **Silent charades relay** — patrol charades relay, miming everyday objects.
- **[Tallest tower](https://www.icebreakerspot.com/activities/newspaper-tower-challenge)** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style — the format does the entertaining, so it doesn't need performance skill from leaders.

**Materials:** bandages, balloons, tape, paper, a colander and water, pens.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Term 3: Fire & Cooking.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format (score out of five, deadpan judging) matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape (short badge presentation, then Taskmaster challenges) is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session.
