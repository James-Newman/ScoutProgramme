# Year 1, Term 2 (Spring) — First Aid & Emergency Response

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 1, term 2) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: calling 999, and how to help someone who is unconscious, unconscious and not breathing, bleeding, choking, or having a burn, heat exhaustion, hypothermia, or an asthma attack.
Badges earned: **Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3)**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Hikes Away (+1).

Every term's 12 weeks follow the same shape: one [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md), one [Night Hike](night-hike.md), one [Camp](camp.md), one [External Activity](external-activity.md), and one [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) session — five fixed slots every term, regardless of theme (Spring/Summer terms don't get Little O or a Shooting Session — those are Autumn only). That leaves seven weeks for the term's own badge content.

**This term needs 4 of those 7 core-content weeks for Emergency Aid Stage 3 itself.** Stage 3 is cumulative with Stages 1 and 2, so a Scout entering fresh needs all ten topics covered: getting help/calling 999, reassuring someone at the scene, unconscious, unconscious and not breathing, bleeding, a burn, choking, heat exhaustion, hypothermia, and an asthma attack. Three teaching sessions — [Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md), [Bleeding, Burns & Choking](bleeding-burns-and-choking.md), and [Heat, Cold & Breathing](heat-cold-and-breathing.md) — cover roughly three or four topics each, comfortably exceeding the badge's own "two to three hours of training" minimum with room for genuine skill practice. A fourth session, [Emergency Aid Scenario Practice](emergency-aid-scenario-practice.md), consolidates everything via a scenario-card circuit before sign-off, rather than assessing Scouts cold.

That leaves **3 of the term's 7 core-content weeks free.** Rather than force in three complete-in-one-sitting filler badges the way Year 1, Term 1 originally considered, this term puts two of those weeks towards genuine progress on the passive Outdoor Challenge Award — [Tent Pitching](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/tent-pitching.md) and [Campsite Selection](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/campsite-selection.md) — since Year 1, Term 1's Camp flagged that campcraft skills hadn't been taught yet; this is that gap being closed, and it means this term's own Camp no longer needs to be as leader-supported. The third free week carries [Communicator Activity Badge](../../filler-sessions/communicator-activity-badge.md) (Option 3: Mobile and Internet Communication) — a loose thematic fit, since clear communication under pressure is exactly what calling 999 also demands.

## Requirement coverage

All of Emergency Aid Stage 3's cumulative requirements are covered this term:

> "Explain to your leader or another adult about how to call 999." — [Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md)
>
> "the importance of getting help" / "reassuring someone at the scene of an emergency" (inherited from Stages 1–2) — [Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md)
>
> "Explain how you help someone who: is unconscious; is unconscious and not breathing" — [Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md)
>
> "Explain how you help someone who: is bleeding ... has a burn ... is choking" — [Bleeding, Burns & Choking](bleeding-burns-and-choking.md)
>
> "Explain how you help someone who: has heat exhaustion; has hypothermia; is having an asthma attack" — [Heat, Cold & Breathing](heat-cold-and-breathing.md)

Source: [Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge, Stage 3](https://www.scouts.org.uk/staged-badges/emergency-aid/stage-3) (and [Stage 1](https://www.scouts.org.uk/staged-badges/emergency-aid/stage-1)/[Stage 2](https://www.scouts.org.uk/staged-badges/emergency-aid/stage-2) for the inherited cumulative requirements) — check the live pages before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

Worth checking before running full sessions: "If you hold a first aid award covering this or a similar syllabus from a recognised first aid provider (such as the British Red Cross or St John Ambulance) you can automatically get this badge" — some Scouts may already qualify this way.

## Advance preparation

Pulled from each session's own Notes — everything a leader needs to organise ahead of running this term, not just on the day.

### Bookings & external arrangements

- **[External Activity](external-activity.md)** — book a venue/provider well ahead, since popular slots get booked up. Pick something different from Year 1, Term 1's booking so it counts as a genuinely new activity towards Adventure Challenge Award.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** site booking — well ahead of the term.
- **First aid training resources** — for [Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md), [Bleeding, Burns & Choking](bleeding-burns-and-choking.md), and [Heat, Cold & Breathing](heat-cold-and-breathing.md): a training manikin and a demonstration/empty asthma inhaler are both worth borrowing from a local first aid provider, St John Ambulance, or a pharmacy/GP surgery well ahead of time if the group doesn't already own them.

### Risk assessments

- **[Night Hike](night-hike.md)** — a pre-walked, risk-assessed route, plus a completed risk assessment for night activities per group policy.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — a completed camp risk assessment.
- **[Tent Pitching](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/tent-pitching.md)** and **[Campsite Selection](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/campsite-selection.md)** — both need suitable outdoor space and a weather contingency.

### Content to source or prepare carefully

- **[Getting Help & Unconscious](getting-help-and-unconscious.md)**, **[Bleeding, Burns & Choking](bleeding-burns-and-choking.md)**, **[Heat, Cold & Breathing](heat-cold-and-breathing.md)** — all four Emergency Aid sessions **must be delivered or directly supervised by an adult with relevant first aid knowledge**, an explicit badge requirement, not just good practice. Confirm who that is well ahead of the term.
- **[Communicator](../../filler-sessions/communicator-activity-badge.md)** — a small fallback pack of photos/videos from a recent Scouting activity, for any Scout without their own to share.

### Equipment & materials to source

See the [material inventory](#material-inventory) below for the full, quantified list — the standouts needing advance sourcing are a first aid training manikin, a demonstration inhaler, one tent per patrol plus a mess tent (Tent Pitching/Camp), and laminated site photo cards (Campsite Selection).

### Stock to order

Two badges complete in full this term — order stock for both from the [Scout Store](https://shop.scouts.org.uk/) ahead of [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md):

- **Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3)** — this term's own focus badge.
- **Communicator Activity Badge** — completed via the filler session.

**Not this term:** Outdoor Challenge Award is only partially progressed (pitch-and-strike signed off; Campsite Selection is prep, not a sign-off) — it remains passive/accumulated, not a badge to stock yet.
