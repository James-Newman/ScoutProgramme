# Heat, Cold & Breathing

**Term:** Year 1, Term 2 — First Aid & Emergency Response

**Fits the term because:** the third of the term's Emergency Aid sessions — completes Stage 3's cumulative topic list with two temperature-related emergencies and one breathing-related one.

**Badge focus:** Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3) requirements — helping someone who has heat exhaustion, has hypothermia, or is having an asthma attack.

> "Explain how you help someone who: ... has heat exhaustion; has hypothermia; ... is having an asthma attack"

**This must be taught by an adult with relevant first aid knowledge** — an explicit Stage 3 requirement, not just good practice.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### Heat exhaustion (30 min)

Teach recognising heat exhaustion (headache, dizziness, sweating heavily, feeling sick) and the response — move to a cool place, remove excess clothing, cool the skin, give water, lie down with legs raised. Small-group scenario cards work well here: a card describes symptoms, the patrol works out and demonstrates the response.

**Materials:** scenario cards describing heat-exhaustion symptoms.

### Hypothermia (30 min)

Teach recognising hypothermia (shivering, confusion, pale/cold skin) and the response — move somewhere warm/sheltered, replace wet clothing, warm gradually with blankets, give a warm (not hot) drink if fully conscious. Discuss why this is especially relevant to camps and hikes this programme already runs.

**Materials:** a blanket or space blanket for a quick demonstration.

### Asthma attack (40 min)

Teach recognising an asthma attack and helping someone use their reliever inhaler — sitting them upright, staying calm, helping them use the inhaler as prescribed, calling 999 if it doesn't improve or they don't have their inhaler. Practise with a demonstration (empty/dummy) inhaler so Scouts have actually handled one, not just heard it described.

**Materials:** a demonstration/empty inhaler and spacer, if available (many pharmacies or GP surgeries can provide an empty demo device).

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.
