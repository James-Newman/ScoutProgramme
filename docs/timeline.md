# 3-Year Scout Programme Timeline (Terms 1–7 planned; Year 3 Terms 2–3 pending)

Rendered view of [`data/terms.yaml`](../data/terms.yaml). This document outlines the 3-year, 9-term continuous cyclical programme plan for a UK Scout Troop (ages 10 to 14). Designed around an inclusive, attendance- and participation-based approach, Scouts achieve their core badge requirements during regular weekly meetings. Optional weekend camps, night hikes, and paddleboarding sessions provide opportunity-based staged progression.

Only the terms that have actually been replanned are listed below. Year 3, Terms 2 and 3 aren't listed yet — their old content was a pre-redesign leftover that had never been revisited and contained known conflicts, so it's removed rather than shown as stale data; they'll reappear here once genuinely replanned.

Each term consists of a 12-week framework with a 6- to 7-week core badge content block, ensuring completion within a single term even during shortened school terms.

**Notes:**

- Four Challenge Awards are never a dedicated focus-badge term: Outdoor, Adventure, and Teamwork Challenge Award are earned passively across many terms (see `filler-sessions/README.md` and `filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`), and Team Leader Challenge Award is instead assessed termly by leaders. Expedition Challenge Award is also passive, covered by the standing summer Camp and/or the autumn Grimsdyke Challenge Hike. Personal Challenge Award is passive too, discussed with Scouts and leaders each term rather than in a dedicated session. See `data/awards.yaml` for how each is actually covered.
- **Master at Arms Activity Badge (Air Rifle / Pistol Shooting)** is also passive — see [`filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md`](../filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md) — covered mostly by the group's recurring shooting sessions during terms.
- The **Chief Scout's Gold Award** (the top award) isn't tied to a specific term — it's presented to each Scout individually as they complete all required Challenge Awards and personal milestones, whenever that happens to fall. See `data/awards.yaml`.
- The programme was originally a 4-year, 12-term plan; Year 4 (Terms 10–12) was dropped when it moved to 3 years. Year 4's badges (Survival Skills Activity Badge; a Global Issues Cycle 2/Refresher + Photographer Activity Badge; Expedition Challenge Award Cycle 2/Senior Roles + Campers Activity Badge) are dropped from the programme entirely, not re-slotted — see `data/awards.yaml`.
- **Naming correction:** the catalogue previously listed a "Global Issues Challenge Award," which isn't a real Scout Association badge — there are only 9 real Challenge Awards for the Scouts section. It was most likely always meant to be the real **Global Issues Activity Badge**, now correctly named and earned in Year 2, Term 3 above.
- Year 3, Terms 2 and 3 used to appear here too, but that content was a pre-redesign leftover that had never been revisited — Term 1 duplicated Chef Activity Badge and Emergency Aid Stage 3/4 from Year 1, and Term 2 still listed Teamwork Challenge Award as a dedicated term badge despite that award being made permanently passive. Only Term 1 has been re-added so far (Skills Challenge Award, above); Terms 2 and 3 are still pending replan. Of the badges that only had a home in that old data, Personal Challenge Award and Master at Arms Activity Badge are now covered passively (see above); Communicator Activity Badge is still flagged "pending Year 3 replan" in `data/awards.yaml`.
- Year 1, Term 3's previous content ("Pioneering & Outdoor Survival" — Pioneer Activity Badge + Paddle Sports Activity Badge) has now been re-slotted into Year 2, Term 2 and made passive (Paddle Sports) respectively.
- Year 2's previous content (Navigator Staged Activity Badge Stage 3/4 at Term 1; Scientist/Artist Activity Badge at Term 2; Hiker Activity Badge at Term 3) was displaced by the rebuild above and hasn't been re-slotted anywhere yet — Team Leader Challenge Award and Expedition Challenge Award from that same displacement are now resolved to passive (see above).
