# 3-Year Scout Programme Timeline (All 9 Terms Planned)

Rendered view of [`data/terms.yaml`](../data/terms.yaml). This document outlines the 3-year, 9-term continuous cyclical programme plan for a UK Scout Troop (ages 10 to 14). Designed around an inclusive, attendance- and participation-based approach, Scouts achieve their core badge requirements during regular weekly meetings. Optional weekend camps, night hikes, and paddleboarding sessions provide opportunity-based staged progression.

Every term now has a theme and a focus badge, though only Year 1, Term 1 is built out session-by-session so far — the rest are framework only (see the Status note in `README.md`).

Each term consists of a 12-week framework with a 6- to 7-week core badge content block, ensuring completion within a single term even during shortened school terms.

**Notes:**

- Four Challenge Awards are never a dedicated focus-badge term: Outdoor, Adventure, and Teamwork Challenge Award are earned passively across many terms (see `filler-sessions/README.md` and `filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`), and Team Leader Challenge Award is instead assessed termly by leaders. Expedition Challenge Award is also passive, covered by the standing summer Camp and/or the autumn Grimsdyke Challenge Hike. Personal Challenge Award is passive too, discussed with Scouts and leaders each term rather than in a dedicated session. See `data/awards.yaml` for how each is actually covered.
- **Master at Arms Activity Badge (Air Rifle / Pistol Shooting)** and **Navigator Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3/4)** are also passive — see [`filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md`](../filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md) and [`filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md`](../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md)/[`navigator-stage-4.md`](../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-4.md) — covered mostly by the group's recurring shooting sessions and standing Night Hikes respectively.
- **Orienteer Activity Badge** is passive too, delivered in full by the standing Little O session every Autumn term (see `little-o-orienteering.md`) — not accumulated across years, unlike most of the badges above.
- The **Chief Scout's Gold Award** (the top award) isn't tied to a specific term — it's presented to each Scout individually as they complete all required Challenge Awards and personal milestones, whenever that happens to fall. All 9 Challenge Awards now have a genuine home with no outstanding gaps (Expedition Challenge Award's individual report is written independently by each Scout in their own time after the hike) — the Gold Award is fully achievable as documented. See `data/awards.yaml`.
- The programme was originally a 4-year, 12-term plan; Year 4 (Terms 10–12) was dropped when it moved to 3 years. Year 4's badges (Survival Skills Activity Badge; a Global Issues Cycle 2/Refresher + Photographer Activity Badge; Expedition Challenge Award Cycle 2/Senior Roles + Campers Activity Badge) are dropped from the programme entirely, not re-slotted — see `data/awards.yaml`.
- **Naming correction:** the catalogue previously listed a "Global Issues Challenge Award," which isn't a real Scout Association badge — there are only 9 real Challenge Awards for the Scouts section. It was most likely always meant to be the real **Global Issues Activity Badge**, now correctly named and earned in Year 2, Term 3 above.
- Year 3's three terms used to be entirely missing here — pre-redesign leftovers that had never been revisited, with known conflicts (Term 1 duplicated Chef Activity Badge and Emergency Aid Stage 3/4 from Year 1; Term 2 still listed Teamwork Challenge Award as a dedicated term badge despite that award being made permanently passive). All three have now been re-added with real, verified badges (above). Of the badges that only had a home in that old data, Personal Challenge Award and Master at Arms Activity Badge are now covered passively (see above); Communicator Activity Badge is now an opportunistic filler session instead (see `filler-sessions/README.md`) — a Scout only earns it if a leader slots that session into a free week, unlike the guaranteed-passive badges above.
- Year 1, Term 3's previous content ("Pioneering & Outdoor Survival" — Pioneer Activity Badge + Paddle Sports Activity Badge) has now been re-slotted into Year 2, Term 2 and made passive (Paddle Sports) respectively.
- Year 2's previous content (Navigator Staged Activity Badge Stage 3/4 from Term 1, now resolved to passive above; Scientist/Artist Activity Badge from Term 2, now resolved into Year 3, Term 2 above; Hiker Activity Badge from Term 3) was displaced by the rebuild above. Team Leader Challenge Award and Expedition Challenge Award from that same displacement are now resolved to passive (see above).
- **Naming correction, and dropped:** "Hiker Activity Badge" was never a real Scout Association badge either — there are 79 real Activity Badges for the Scouts section, and "Hiker" isn't one of them. The closest match, Hill Walker Activity Badge, requires 5 one-day journeys of 14km+ in hilly country with 3 led by a qualified permit holder — well beyond the group's regular Night Hikes and Hikes Away. Rather than force-fit it or invent hillwalking logistics the group doesn't have, it's dropped from the programme entirely, the same treatment as the Year 4 badges. This was the last remaining displaced/TBD badge — every badge in the catalogue now either has a genuine home or has been deliberately dropped.
