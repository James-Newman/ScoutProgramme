# Summary of Total Awards Received Over 3 Years

Rendered view of [`data/awards.yaml`](../data/awards.yaml). By consistently showing up and participating in regular weekly meetings over their 9-term journey, a Scout will achieve a comprehensive set of Scouting awards.

## 1. The Top Award

- **Chief Scout's Gold Award** — Presented to each Scout individually as they complete all required Challenge Awards and personal milestones, whenever that happens to fall, rather than at a single end-of-programme ceremony.

## 2. Ten Challenge Awards (In-Meeting Core)

- **World Challenge Award** — Earned in Year 1, Term 1. All 7 requirements are covered across just 4 of the term's 7 core-content sessions — see [`year-1/term-1/overview.md`](../year-1/term-1/overview.md) for how they combine.
- **Outdoor Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively across many terms via [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/`](../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md) and the standing Camp/Night Hike/Welcome/External Activity guidance in `AGENTS.md`.
- **Global Issues Challenge Award** — Displaced from Year 1, Term 2 and not yet re-slotted. Its Cycle 2 refresher was previously planned for Year 4, Term 2; dropped along with the rest of Year 4.
- **Adventure Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via every term's standing External Activity slot plus [`filler-sessions/adventure-challenge-award.md`](../filler-sessions/adventure-challenge-award.md).
- **Team Leader Challenge Award** — Displaced from Year 2, Term 1 and not yet re-slotted.
- **Creative Challenge Award** — Earned in Year 2, Term 1 (moved here from Year 2, Term 2).
- **Expedition Challenge Award** — Displaced from Year 2, Term 3 and not yet re-slotted. Its Cycle 2/senior-roles refresher was previously planned for Year 4, Term 3; dropped along with the rest of Year 4.
- **Skills Challenge Award** — TBD, pending Year 3 replan (previously Year 3, Term 1; removed along with the rest of Year 3's stale pre-redesign data).
- **Teamwork Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via every term's standing Welcome Session plus [`filler-sessions/teamwork-challenge-award.md`](../filler-sessions/teamwork-challenge-award.md).
- **Personal Challenge Award** — TBD, pending Year 3 replan (previously Year 3, Term 3; removed along with the rest of Year 3's stale pre-redesign data).

## 3. Activity Badges (Completed Within Specific Terms)

- **Environmental Conservation Activity Badge** (displaced from Year 1, Term 2; not yet re-slotted)
- **Pioneer Activity Badge** (Year 2, Term 2 — resolves the earlier displacement from Year 1, Term 3)
- **Scientist Activity Badge** *or* **Artist Activity Badge** (displaced from Year 2, Term 2; not yet re-slotted)
- **Hiker Activity Badge** (displaced from Year 2, Term 3; not yet re-slotted)
- **Chef Activity Badge** (Year 1, Term 3)
- **Communicator Activity Badge** (TBD, pending Year 3 replan — previously Year 3, Term 2)
- **Master at Arms Activity Badge (Air Rifle / Pistol Shooting)** (TBD, pending Year 3 replan — previously Year 3, Term 3)

## 4. Staged Activity Badges (Optional / Opportunity-Based Accumulation)

- **Nights Away Staged Activity Badge** — Attained automatically by Scouts who choose to attend the optional weekend camps (+2 nights per term).
- **Hikes Away Staged Activity Badge** — Attained automatically by Scouts who choose to attend the optional term night hikes and lightweight expeditions.
- **Time on the Water Staged Activity Badge** — Accumulated by Scouts who choose to attend the summer paddleboarding sessions.
- **Navigator Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3/4)** — Displaced from Year 2, Term 1 and not yet re-slotted.
- **Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge (Stage 1)** — Earned in Year 2, Term 3. Resolves the earlier displacement from Year 1, Term 3, previously mis-recorded as a flat "Paddle Sports Activity Badge" — that badge doesn't exist, it's staged.
- **Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3)** — Earned in Year 1, Term 2.

**Notes:**

- The programme was originally a 4-year, 12-term plan. Year 4 (Terms 10–12) was dropped when it moved to 3 years, and its badges — Survival Skills Activity Badge, Photographer Activity Badge, and Campers Activity Badge — were dropped from this catalogue entirely, not re-slotted. See `data/awards.yaml` and `data/terms.yaml`.
- Year 3's old content was a pre-redesign leftover that had never been revisited and contained known conflicts (it duplicated Chef Activity Badge and Emergency Aid Stage 3/4 from Year 1, and still listed Teamwork Challenge Award as a dedicated term badge despite that award being made permanently passive). It's been removed from `data/terms.yaml` entirely rather than kept as stale data — hence the four badges above marked "pending Year 3 replan." Year 3 still exists conceptually; it just hasn't been replanned yet, unlike Year 4 which was dropped outright.
