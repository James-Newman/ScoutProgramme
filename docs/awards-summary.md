# Summary of Total Awards Received Over 3 Years

Rendered view of [`data/awards.yaml`](../data/awards.yaml). By consistently showing up and participating in regular weekly meetings over their 9-term journey, a Scout will achieve a comprehensive set of Scouting awards.

## 1. The Top Award

- **Chief Scout's Gold Award** — Presented to each Scout individually as they complete all required Challenge Awards and personal milestones, whenever that happens to fall, rather than at a single end-of-programme ceremony. All 9 Challenge Awards now have a genuine home with no outstanding gaps (see section 2) — this is fully achievable as documented.

## 2. Nine Challenge Awards (In-Meeting Core)

- **World Challenge Award** — Earned in Year 1, Term 1. All 7 requirements are covered across just 4 of the term's 7 core-content sessions — see [`year-1/term-1/overview.md`](../year-1/term-1/overview.md) for how they combine.
- **Outdoor Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively across many terms via [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/`](../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md) and the standing Camp/Night Hike/Welcome/External Activity guidance in `AGENTS.md`.
- **Adventure Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via every term's standing External Activity slot plus [`filler-sessions/adventure-challenge-award.md`](../filler-sessions/adventure-challenge-award.md).
- **Team Leader Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; assessed termly by leaders instead (leading a team at camp/an all-day event, helping a new Scout settle in, helping another Scout develop a skill, representing views at a Patrol Leaders' Council). Previously displaced from Year 2, Term 1.
- **Creative Challenge Award** — Earned in Year 2, Term 1 (moved here from Year 2, Term 2).
- **Expedition Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; covered by the standing summer Camp and/or the autumn Grimsdyke Challenge Hike. The individual report is written independently by each Scout in their own time after the hike, within the three-week window — no dedicated session needed. Previously displaced from Year 2, Term 3; its Cycle 2/senior-roles refresher was separately planned for Year 4, Term 3 and dropped along with the rest of Year 4.
- **Skills Challenge Award** — Earned in Year 3, Term 1 (fits back into its original slot from the old pre-redesign plan, now verified against the real requirements).
- **Teamwork Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via every term's standing Welcome Session plus [`filler-sessions/teamwork-challenge-award.md`](../filler-sessions/teamwork-challenge-award.md).
- **Personal Challenge Award** — Never a dedicated term; discussed with Scouts and leaders each term instead, since agreeing/following through on two personal challenges is easy to let slip without a regular reminder. Previously pending Year 3 replan.

**Naming correction:** this list previously included a "Global Issues Challenge Award," which isn't a real Scout Association badge — there are only 9 real Challenge Awards for the Scouts section (all listed above). It was most likely always meant to be the real **Global Issues Activity Badge** — see section 3 below.

## 3. Activity Badges (Completed Within Specific Terms)

- **Environmental Conservation Activity Badge** (Year 3, Term 3 — resolves the oldest open displacement, from Year 1, Term 2)
- **Pioneer Activity Badge** (Year 2, Term 2 — resolves the earlier displacement from Year 1, Term 3)
- **Scientist Activity Badge** *or* **Artist Activity Badge** (Year 3, Term 2 — resolves the earlier displacement from Year 2, Term 2; which option each Scout/the term uses isn't decided yet)
- **Chef Activity Badge** (Year 1, Term 3)
- **Global Issues Activity Badge** (Year 2, Term 3 — corrects the "Global Issues Challenge Award" naming error above, resolving the earlier displacement from Year 1, Term 2)
- **Master at Arms Activity Badge (Air Rifle / Pistol Shooting)** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via the group's recurring shooting sessions plus [`filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md`](../filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md) for the safety-rules requirement. Previously pending Year 3 replan.

## 4. Staged Activity Badges (Optional / Opportunity-Based Accumulation)

- **Nights Away Staged Activity Badge** — Attained automatically by Scouts who choose to attend the optional weekend camps (+2 nights per term).
- **Hikes Away Staged Activity Badge** — Attained automatically by Scouts who choose to attend the optional term night hikes and lightweight expeditions.
- **Time on the Water Staged Activity Badge** — Accumulated by Scouts who choose to attend the summer paddleboarding sessions.
- **Navigator Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3/4)** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via the group's standing Night Hikes/Hikes Away for the real navigating routes, plus [`filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md`](../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md) and [`filler-sessions/navigator-stage-4.md`](../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-4.md) for the map/compass knowledge. Previously displaced from Year 2, Term 1.
- **Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge (Stage 1)** — Never a dedicated term; earned passively via every summer term's standing Time on the Water/External Activity slot plus [`filler-sessions/paddle-sports-staged-activity-badge.md`](../filler-sessions/paddle-sports-staged-activity-badge.md). Previously a dedicated Year 2, Term 3 badge (which itself had resolved an earlier displacement from Year 1, Term 3, after finding "Paddle Sports Activity Badge" doesn't exist as a flat badge — it's staged) — moved to passive because the group already runs paddle sports content every summer regardless.
- **Emergency Aid Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3)** — Earned in Year 1, Term 2.

**Notes:**

- The programme was originally a 4-year, 12-term plan. Year 4 (Terms 10–12) was dropped when it moved to 3 years, and its badges — Survival Skills Activity Badge, Photographer Activity Badge, and Campers Activity Badge — were dropped from this catalogue entirely, not re-slotted. See `data/awards.yaml` and `data/terms.yaml`.
- Year 3's old content was a pre-redesign leftover that had never been revisited and contained known conflicts (it duplicated Chef Activity Badge and Emergency Aid Stage 3/4 from Year 1, and still listed Teamwork Challenge Award as a dedicated term badge despite that award being made permanently passive). It was removed from `data/terms.yaml` entirely rather than kept as stale data. All three terms have since been re-added with real, verified badges: Skills Challenge Award (Term 1), Scientist/Artist Activity Badge (Term 2), and Environmental Conservation Activity Badge (Term 3) — see section 2/3. Personal Challenge Award and Master at Arms Activity Badge, which also only had a home in that old data, are now covered passively instead (see sections 2 and 3).
- Year 2, Term 3 ("On the Water") was also removed from `data/terms.yaml` separately from the above, after its badge (Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge, Stage 1) moved to passive — see section 4. It's since been re-slotted with Global Issues Activity Badge (see section 3), since there was no real 9th/10th Challenge Award left over to fill it once Team Leader, Expedition, Personal, and Master at Arms all moved to passive.
- **Communicator Activity Badge** no longer appears in section 3 above — it's fully completable in one sitting (Option 3, Mobile and Internet Communication), so it's now an opportunistic filler session (`filler-sessions/communicator-activity-badge.md`) rather than a guaranteed catalogue badge, the same way Writer/Entertainer/etc. are handled. A Scout only earns it if a leader slots that session into a free week — unlike the passive badges above, it isn't structurally guaranteed. See `filler-sessions/README.md`.
- **Hiker Activity Badge** also no longer appears in section 3 above — it was never a real Scout Association badge, like "Global Issues Challenge Award" above. There are 79 real Activity Badges for the Scouts section, and "Hiker" isn't one of them; the closest match, Hill Walker Activity Badge, needs 5 one-day journeys of 14km+ in hilly country with 3 led by a qualified permit holder — well beyond the group's regular Night Hikes and Hikes Away. Rather than force-fit it in, it's dropped from the catalogue entirely, the same treatment as the Year 4 badges. This was the last remaining displaced/TBD badge — every badge now either has a genuine home or has been deliberately dropped.
