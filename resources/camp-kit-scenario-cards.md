# Everything You Need, Nothing You Don't — Scenario & Item List

Used in: [Camp Kit & Personal Equipment](../year-1/term-2/camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md) ("Everything you need, nothing you don't", 25 min), adapting the [official activity](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/everything-you-need-nothing-you-don-t/), which doesn't publish its own printable item list.

## Scenario (read aloud)

"It's an autumn weekend camp. Two nights, one of them likely to be wet. You're walking from the car park to the pitch, so whatever you pack, you're carrying."

## Item list

Read each item aloud (or hold up a card); patrols hold up green ("pack it") or red ("leave it"). Suggested running order mixes obvious calls with the debatable ones, so the energy doesn't dip.

| # | Item | Suggested answer | Why (for discussion) |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Waterproof jacket | Pack it | Forecast says rain |
| 2 | Hairdryer | Leave it | No power at camp, heavy, pointless |
| 3 | Torch + spare batteries | Pack it | Dark by 6pm in autumn |
| 4 | Games console | Leave it | Camp's about being outdoors, and there's no power |
| 5 | Spare socks (2 pairs) | Pack it | Wet feet ruin a weekend fast |
| 6 | Glass jar of jam | Leave it | Breaks, heavy — decant into a plastic tub if you must bring it |
| 7 | Sleeping bag rated for the season | Pack it | Non-negotiable |
| 8 | Umbrella | Leave it | Useless in wind, better off with a proper waterproof |
| 9 | Flip-flops | Debatable | Fine for the shower block, useless everywhere else — not as a main shoe |
| 10 | First aid kit (personal) | Pack it | Small, light, always useful |
| 11 | Full deck of playing cards | Pack it | Light, good for downtime, no debate |
| 12 | Six changes of clothes for two nights | Debatable | Overpacking — three is plenty, laundry isn't happening at camp |
| 13 | Sun cream | Pack it | Even in autumn, even if cloudy |
| 14 | A brick-sized paperback | Debatable | Nice to have, but weigh it against pack space |
| 15 | Wash kit (soap, flannel, toothbrush, towel) | Pack it | Non-negotiable |
| 16 | A whole loaf of bread, unwrapped | Leave it | Squashes, goes stale, no protection from weather/animals |
| 17 | Head torch | Pack it | Hands-free beats a handheld torch every time |
| 18 | A second, unnecessary tent | Leave it | Already covered by patrol/mess tents |
| 19 | Reusable water bottle | Pack it | Non-negotiable |
| 20 | A single trainer with no pair | Leave it | Obviously useless, good for a laugh mid-activity |

## Scoring

One point per correct call for the "pack it"/"leave it" items; for the three "debatable" items, award the point to whichever patrol gives the best-argued case, regardless of which side they land on.
