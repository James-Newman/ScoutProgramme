# Kit List Bingo — Cards & Clue Sheet

Used in: [Camp Kit & Personal Equipment](../year-1/term-1/camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md) ("Kit list bingo", 15 min).

Item pool drawn from the official [Scouts Camp Kit List](https://shop.scouts.org.uk/journal/2024/05/17/scouts-camp-kit-list). Sixteen items, cut down to nine per card so cards differ across the room. Print one card per Scout (or share between two).

## Clue sheet (read aloud, in any order)

| # | Clue | Answer |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | You'll wish you had this if you need the loo at 2am. | Torch |
| 2 | Keeps your feet dry when the last pair got soaked. | Spare socks |
| 3 | Zip it up before the rain starts, not after. | Waterproof jacket |
| 4 | Nobody wants wet legs on a hike. | Waterproof trousers |
| 5 | You sleep in it, not on it. | Sleeping bag |
| 6 | Stops the cold ground stealing your body heat all night. | Roll mat |
| 7 | Fill it up before you set off. | Water bottle |
| 8 | Eat your dinner off this. | Mess tin / plate |
| 9 | You'll need this to actually eat the dinner. | Spork / cutlery |
| 10 | Soap, flannel, toothbrush, towel — all in one bag. | Wash kit |
| 11 | Nobody thinks about this until they're sunburnt. | Sun cream |
| 12 | Keeps your head warm at night, and warm heads mean warm bodies. | Hat |
| 13 | Cold fingers can't tie knots. | Gloves |
| 14 | Plasters, bandages, the works. | First aid kit |
| 15 | For after the loo, before food, and any time your hands feel grim. | Hand sanitiser |
| 16 | Put it on when the sun goes down and the temperature drops. | Warm layer / fleece |

## Bingo cards

Each card is a 3×3 grid. Cut along the lines, or print one per page.

Print extra copies of whichever card by duplicating one of the four above — with a group of up to 6 patrols (roughly 24–36 Scouts), expect to print 6–9 copies of each card.
