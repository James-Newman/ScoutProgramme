# Food & Drink Sorting Cards

Used in: [Healthy Body, Healthy Choices](../year-3/term-1/healthy-body-healthy-choices.md) ("Balanced diet & hydration", 25 min), adapting the [Eatwell Guide](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/food-guidelines-and-food-labels/the-eatwell-guide/). Print and cut into individual cards; patrols sort the food items into the correct Eatwell Guide group, then sort the drinks into "hydrates well" or "hydrates poorly."

| :-- | :-- |

| Baked beans | Beans, pulses, fish, eggs, meat |
| Eggs | Beans, pulses, fish, eggs, meat |

| Crisps | Foods high in fat, salt, or sugar |
| Chocolate bar | Foods high in fat, salt, or sugar |
| Fizzy drink | Foods high in fat, salt, or sugar |

## Drinks to sort by how well they hydrate

| Drink | Hydrates well | Hydrates poorly |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Water | Yes | |
| Squash (diluted) | Yes | |
| Milk | Yes | |
| Fizzy cola | | Yes — high sugar and caffeine |
| Energy drink | | Yes — high sugar and caffeine |
| Strong black coffee | | Yes — caffeine has a mild diuretic effect |

## Notes

- Add or swap items to suit what's actually in the group's cupboard for the session — the format matters more than the specific sixteen items listed.
- The "hydrates poorly" drinks aren't forbidden, just worth knowing about — the point is understanding the difference, not banning anything outright.
