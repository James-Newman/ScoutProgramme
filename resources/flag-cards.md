# Flag Cards

Used in: [End of Term Fun & Games](../year-1/term-1/end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) ("Mystery flag relay"). Print and cut into individual cards — one flag per card, name on the back or on a separate matching card for a memory-match variant. A mix of well-known and less obvious flags keeps the relay from being too easy.

## Notes

- If printing in black and white, swap in a source with distinct flag outlines rather than relying on colour alone (many flags look similar in greyscale).
- Countries a patrol researched in [International Issues & Connections](../year-1/term-1/international-issues-and-connections.md) are worth including if they're not already on this list — a nice small callback to earlier in the term.
