## Campfire Foil Parcels: Sausage, Bacon, Potato & Onion

A hearty, hobo-style parcel that each Scout builds themselves and cooks in the embers of an open fire.

### Ingredients (for a group of 4)

- 8 pork sausages
- 8 rashers streaky bacon
- 3 large baking potatoes, scrubbed and cut into 1.5 cm cubes
- 1 onion, peeled and sliced into thin wedges (2 for fuller parcels)
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil or butter, plus a little more oil for the foil
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp mixed dried herbs (optional)
- 8 sheets heavy-duty foil, about 40 cm square (two layers per parcel)

### Steps

1. **Fire first.** Light the fire well ahead of cooking and let it burn down to glowing orange embers with no big flames. Cooking in embers gives even heat and avoids burnt parcels. A leader supervises, with a bucket of water and a bucket of sand beside the fire.
2. **Prep.** Wash your hands. Scrub and cube the potatoes into small, even pieces (about 1.5 cm) so they cook through. Slice the onions into wedges. Halve the bacon rashers if it makes them easier to wrap. Keep the sausages whole.
3. **Season the veg.** In a bowl, toss the potatoes and onions with the oil or butter, salt, pepper and herbs until lightly coated. Use separate boards and utensils for raw meat and veg, and wash your hands again after handling raw sausages and bacon.
4. **Build.** Lay two sheets of foil on top of each other and rub a little oil on the middle. Pile a quarter of the potato and onion mix in the centre. Lay 2 sausages on top and wrap 2 rashers of bacon around or over them.
5. **Seal.** Bring the long edges of the foil together and fold them over twice, then fold and crimp both ends so the parcel is fully sealed with a little air space inside. Label it with your name using a marker or a twist of foil.
6. **Cook.** Use tongs to place the parcels on the hot embers, not in the flames. Cook for **20–25 minutes**, turning once halfway with the tongs. Allow up to 35 minutes if the embers are fading.
7. **Check.** A leader takes the parcel out with tongs, lets it rest for 2 minutes, then opens it away from faces, because hot steam escapes. The potatoes should be soft when pierced with a fork, and the sausages must be cooked all the way through, with no pink inside and the juices running clear. If not, reseal and return to the embers for 5–10 minutes.
8. **Rest and eat.** Let it cool for a few minutes, then eat straight from the foil, taking care with hot steam and fat. Put the used foil in the bin, and make sure the fire is fully out afterwards.

### Safety

- A leader is with the fire at all times.
- Long tongs and heat-proof gloves for anything on or near the fire.
- Keep parcels small so the potato cooks through.
- Check for allergies (such as gluten in some sausages) before cooking.

### Variations

- **Veggie:** swap in veggie sausages and skip the bacon.
- **Extra flavour:** a knob of butter, some grated cheese, or a splash of ketchup or BBQ sauce before sealing.
