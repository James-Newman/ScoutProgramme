# Camp Hygiene True or False — Statement List

Used in: [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](../year-1/term-1/camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) ("True or false floor debate", 25 min). Statements are drawn from [Scouts food hygiene guidance](https://www.scouts.org.uk/volunteers/staying-safe-and-safeguarding/safety/safe-camping-residentials-and-outdoor-skills/guidance-for-food-safety/food-hygiene/).

Mark one side of the room "true," the other "false." Read each statement; Scouts move to the side they think is correct before the answer is revealed.

| # | Statement | Answer | Why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | It's fine to store raw meat next to cooked food in the same cool box. | False | Raw and ready-to-eat food must be kept separate to avoid cross-contamination. |
| 2 | You should wash your hands before handling food, even if they look clean. | True | Germs you can't see are still there. |
| 3 | Leftover food can sit out all afternoon and still be safe to eat that evening. | False | Food left at room temperature for hours becomes a food poisoning risk. |
| 4 | Chopping boards and knives should be cleaned between raw and cooked food, not just at the end of cooking. | True | Prevents cross-contamination mid-prep. |
| 5 | Hand sanitiser is just as good as washing with soap and water when hands are visibly dirty. | False | Sanitiser doesn't remove dirt/grease the way soap and water does. |
| 6 | You should wash your hands after using the toilet, before you do anything else. | True | Always, every time. |
| 7 | It's OK to taste food with the same spoon you're stirring with, more than once. | False | Double-dipping spreads germs back into the food. |
| 8 | Food should be covered while it's waiting to be served. | True | Keeps insects and dirt off. |
| 9 | If a patrol member feels unwell, they should still help cook that day's meal. | False | Anyone feeling unwell (especially with sickness/diarrhoea) shouldn't handle food. |
| 10 | Washing up water should be hot and have washing-up liquid in it, not just cold water. | True | Hot soapy water actually removes grease and germs. |
| 11 | It's fine to use the same cloth to wipe the table and then wipe up after raw chicken. | False | Cross-contamination risk — use separate cloths or wash thoroughly between uses. |
| 12 | Rubbish should be bagged and stored away from the cooking and eating area. | True | Keeps pests away and stops contamination. |
| 13 | Personal hygiene (hand-washing, clean clothes) matters just as much at camp as it does at home. | True | Camp doesn't lower the standard, if anything it raises the risk. |
