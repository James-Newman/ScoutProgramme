# Toilets, Rubbish & Waste — Scenario Cards

Used in: [Camp Hygiene & Food Safety](../year-1/term-1/camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) ("Toilets, rubbish, and waste at camp", 20 min). Six cards, one per patrol.

Each card poses a situation; the patrol works out and presents their answer.

**Card 1 — No bin**
"There's no bin at your pitch, and the nearest one is a ten-minute walk away. What do you do with your rubbish over the weekend?"
*Discussion points: bag it and store it away from the tents/kitchen; don't let it pile up in the open; take it to the bin at a sensible time, not let it wait until Sunday.*

**Card 2 — Night-time loo**
"It's 2am, dark, and someone needs the toilet. What's the process?"
*Discussion points: wake your tent buddy or tell someone, take a torch, use the buddy system, know the route in daylight before it gets dark.*

**Card 3 — Food scraps after dinner**
"Dinner's finished and there are food scraps left on plates. What happens to them?"
*Discussion points: scrape into a rubbish bag, don't tip onto the ground (attracts animals/pests), wash plates properly afterwards.*

**Card 4 — Recycling**
"Your patrol has cans, plastic bottles, and food packaging after two days at camp. Does it all go in one bag?"
*Discussion points: separate recyclables if the site supports it, otherwise bag everything for disposal off-site, don't burn plastic in the fire.*

**Card 5 — Spilled washing-up water**
"Someone's about to tip a bowl of greasy washing-up water straight onto the grass by the tents. Is that OK?"
*Discussion points: use a designated grey-water disposal point if the site has one, don't tip it near tents or water sources, strain food bits out first.*

**Card 6 — Leaving on Sunday**
"You're packing up to leave. What needs to happen to the pitch before you go?"
*Discussion points: all rubbish taken with you or binned, nothing left behind (tent pegs, guy lines, litter), ground checked and left as it was found — this is also the basis of the "leave no trace" requirement covered properly at [Camp](../year-1/term-1/camp.md).*
