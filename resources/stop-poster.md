## What to do if you're lost

# S — STOP
Stay where you are. Don't wander further.

# T — THINK
What do you know? What do you need?

# O — OBSERVE
Look around for a landmark or a way back.

# P — PLAN
Decide what to do, then do it.

---

*Blow your whistle in bursts of six if you need help finding your way back.*
