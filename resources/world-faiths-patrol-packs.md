## Research pack: Christianity

Christianity is the largest religion in the world and is followed by billions of people across many different countries. Although Christians can be found on every continent, the religion began nearly 2,000 years ago in the Middle East. Over time, the faith spread throughout the Roman Empire and then across Europe, Africa, Asia and the Americas.

Christians follow the teachings of Jesus Christ. Jesus lived in the region now known as Israel and Palestine. Christians believe that his teachings showed people how to live lives based on love, forgiveness, kindness and service to others. Many of the stories about Jesus describe him helping people, healing the sick and encouraging people to care for those who are struggling.

The Christian holy book is known as the Bible. It contains many different books written over hundreds of years. Christians usually divide it into two main sections called the Old Testament and the New Testament. The New Testament contains accounts of Jesus' life and teachings, as well as letters written by some of the earliest Christian leaders.

Christians worship in churches, although church buildings can look very different. Some churches are small village buildings with only a few dozen worshippers. Others are enormous cathedrals that can hold thousands of people. Inside a church, you may find features such as stained-glass windows, an altar, candles, crosses and musical instruments used during worship.

Many Christians gather for worship on Sundays. A church service often includes singing, prayer, readings from the Bible and a talk from a minister, priest or pastor. However, not all Christians worship in exactly the same way. Different Christian groups sometimes have different traditions.

Two important Christian festivals are Christmas and Easter. Christmas celebrates the birth of Jesus and is observed by Christians around the world. Easter celebrates Jesus' death and resurrection. Many Christians consider Easter the most important event in the Christian calendar because it represents hope and new life.

Throughout history, Christian communities have often been involved in helping others. Many schools, hospitals, food banks and charities were originally founded by Christian groups. Organisations such as The Salvation Army continue to provide support for people facing difficulties.

Some Christians pray every day, while others may pray at particular times or in church services. Prayer can be silent or spoken aloud. Christians may pray for family members, friends, people in need or to give thanks for good things in their lives.

The Bible has been translated into more languages than any other book. Today Christianity includes many different groups, including Anglicans, Catholics, Orthodox Christians and Protestants. Although these groups sometimes worship differently, they share core beliefs about Jesus and the Bible.

### Did you know?

- The world's largest church building can hold tens of thousands of people.
- The Christian fish symbol was used secretly by some early Christians.
- Many Christmas traditions, such as decorations and special meals, developed over hundreds of years.
- Christianity is the largest religion in the United Kingdom.
- Some churches have bells that have been ringing for hundreds of years.

## Research pack: Islam

Islam is one of the world's largest religions and has followers throughout every continent. It began more than 1,400 years ago in Arabia and spread through trade, scholarship and travel to many different parts of the world. Today there are Muslim communities in countries as varied as Indonesia, Egypt, Turkey, the United Kingdom and Canada.

Muslims believe in one God, known in Arabic as Allah. Muslims believe that God revealed guidance to the Prophet Muhammad and that his teachings continue to guide Muslims today. Muhammad was born in Makkah and is regarded by Muslims as the final prophet in a long line of prophets which includes figures such as Ibrahim (Abraham), Musa (Moses) and Isa (Jesus).

The holy book of Islam is called the Qur'an. Muslims believe it contains God's words and treat it with great respect. Copies of the Qur'an are often stored carefully and may be placed on a stand while being read. Many Muslims learn passages of the Qur'an by heart and recite them during worship.

Many Muslims worship in mosques. Before entering the prayer hall, worshippers often remove their shoes. Mosques may look very different depending on where they are built. Some have domes and minarets, while others are simple community buildings. The design is less important than the purpose: providing a place for prayer, learning and community.

One important part of Islam is the Five Pillars. These are five key practices that help Muslims live out their faith:

- Declaration of faith
- Prayer
- Charity
- Fasting
- Pilgrimage

Muslims are encouraged to pray regularly throughout the day. These prayers provide opportunities to pause, reflect and remember their faith, even during busy lives.

Ramadan is a special month during which many Muslims fast during daylight hours. Fasting is seen as a way of developing self-discipline, appreciation for what people have, and understanding for those who have less. Many Muslims look forward to breaking the fast together with family members and friends.

At the end of Ramadan, Muslims celebrate Eid al-Fitr. Another important festival is Eid al-Adha. These festivals often involve prayer, family gatherings, special meals, gifts and acts of charity.

For many Muslims, faith influences daily life through prayer, honest behaviour, helping those in need and being part of a community. Charity is often viewed as an important responsibility rather than simply a choice.

Throughout history, Islamic scholars made important contributions in mathematics, astronomy, medicine and engineering. During the Middle Ages, some of the world's leading centres of learning were located in Muslim cities such as Baghdad and Cordoba.

### Did you know?

- Muslims pray facing Makkah.
- The Islamic calendar is based on the phases of the moon.
- Some mosques are famous for their architecture and artwork.
- Calligraphy is often an important feature of Islamic art.
- There are thousands of mosques in the United Kingdom today.

### Common misconception

Some people think all Muslims live in the Middle East. In reality, Muslims live in countries all around the world, and the largest Muslim populations are found in parts of Asia rather than the Middle East.

### Timeline

- 570 CE – Muhammad is born.
- 610 CE – Muslims believe Muhammad received the first revelations.
- 622 CE – Beginning of the Islamic calendar.
- Today – Islam is one of the world's largest religions.

### Quotation

"The best among you are those who are best to other people."

## Research pack: Hinduism

Hinduism is one of the world's oldest religions and developed over thousands of years in India. Unlike some religions, Hinduism does not have a single founder. Instead, its beliefs and traditions developed gradually over a very long period of time.

Because Hinduism developed over such a long period, there is considerable variety among Hindu communities. People may practise their faith in different ways while still considering themselves Hindu. This diversity is one reason Hinduism can sometimes be difficult to define in a single sentence.

Several holy texts are important within Hinduism. These include the Vedas and the Bhagavad Gita. These texts contain stories, teachings and guidance about life, responsibility and spiritual development.

Many Hindus worship in temples known as mandirs. Worship may also take place at home. Some families have shrines containing images, lamps and other objects used during prayer and reflection.

Important Hindu ideas include dharma, karma and reincarnation. Dharma relates to living responsibly and carrying out one's duties. Karma is the belief that actions have consequences. Reincarnation is the belief that life continues through a cycle of rebirth.

Hindus often believe that how a person behaves is important. Qualities such as honesty, self-discipline, kindness and respect are frequently encouraged. Different Hindu stories often explore the consequences of making wise or unwise choices.

One of the best-known Hindu festivals is Diwali, often called the Festival of Lights. Homes may be decorated with lamps and candles, symbolising light overcoming darkness and good overcoming evil. Families often gather together to celebrate.

Family is often very important within Hindu communities. Festivals frequently involve large family gatherings, food and celebrations. Respect for parents, grandparents and elders is often emphasised.

Faith may influence daily life through worship, celebration of festivals and efforts to live according to dharma. Some Hindus are vegetarian because of beliefs about respect for living things.

India contains thousands of Hindu temples, ranging from small local buildings to enormous temple complexes visited by millions of people each year.

### Did you know?

- Hinduism is followed by more than a billion people worldwide.
- Many Hindu families have shrines in their homes.
- Diwali is celebrated in many countries outside India.
- Some Hindu temples are famous for their detailed carvings.
- The river Ganges is considered sacred by many Hindus.

### Common misconception

Some people think all Hindus believe exactly the same things. In reality, Hinduism includes many traditions and practices, and beliefs can vary between families, regions and communities.

### Timeline

- More than 3,000 years ago – Early Hindu traditions develop.
- Ancient India – The Vedas are written down.
- Around 2,000 years ago – The Bhagavad Gita becomes an important Hindu text.
- Today – Hindu communities exist around the world.

### Quotation

"Truth alone triumphs."

## Research pack: Sikhism

Sikhism began around 500 years ago in the Punjab region of India. It was founded by Guru Nanak, who taught that all people are equal and should live honest lives while helping others.

Guru Nanak travelled widely and shared teachings about fairness, respect and service. He challenged the idea that people should be treated differently because of their wealth, status, gender or background. These teachings continue to influence Sikh communities around the world today.

The Sikh holy book is called the Guru Granth Sahib. Sikhs treat it with great respect and regard it as their spiritual guide. In a gurdwara, the Guru Granth Sahib is often placed in a position of honour.

Sikhs worship in a gurdwara. Visitors are welcomed regardless of religion or background. Before entering the main worship area, people usually remove their shoes and cover their heads as a sign of respect.

One of the most distinctive features of a gurdwara is the langar. This is a free meal prepared and shared by volunteers. Anyone can eat at a langar, regardless of religion, age, wealth or background. It reflects Sikh beliefs about equality and community.

Many Sikhs try to put their faith into action through service. Volunteering, helping neighbours and supporting community projects are often seen as important ways to live according to Sikh teachings.

Important Sikh values include equality, honesty, generosity, service and respect for others. These values can often be seen through community projects organised by Sikh groups around the world.

An important Sikh festival is Vaisakhi. It celebrates a significant event in Sikh history and is often marked through community gatherings, music, parades and celebrations.

Faith influences daily life through worship, service, community involvement and efforts to treat people fairly. Many Sikhs believe that actions are just as important as words.

Sikh communities can be found throughout the United Kingdom and around the world. Many gurdwaras organise food banks, volunteer projects and fundraising activities.

### Did you know?

- The Golden Temple in Amritsar is one of Sikhism's most important sites.
- Thousands of free meals are served through langar every day.
- Sikh communities often play a major role in local volunteering projects.
- The Sikh turban has religious and cultural significance.
- The word "Sikh" means "learner" or "student".

### Common misconception

Some people think the langar is simply a free meal. For Sikhs, it is also a powerful symbol of equality because everyone sits and eats together regardless of background.

### Timeline

- 1469 – Guru Nanak is born.
- Late 1400s and early 1500s – Guru Nanak travels and teaches.
- 1604 – The first version of the Sikh holy scripture is compiled.
- Today – Sikh communities exist around the world.

### Quotation

"Recognise all humankind as one."
