# Present Your Findings / Prepare Your Showcase

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Science & Art

**Fits the term because:** the last parallel-track session before both groups reunite — Science Scouts prepare how they'll explain their results, Art Scouts prepare how they'll present their body of work, both ahead of [Science & Art Showcase Night](science-and-art-showcase-night.md).

**Badge focus:** Science track — Scientist Activity Badge requirement, explaining experiment results to others. Art track — Artist Activity Badge requirement, showing a selection of recent work.

> **Scientist:** "...explain your results to others."

> **Artist:** "Show a selection of your own recent work."

## Running order (both tracks run in parallel)

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome (joint) |
| 0:10–1:40 | 90 min | Track content |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Track share-back |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close (joint) |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min, joint)

Notices, opening, split into Science and Art groups. Explain that next session is the joint Showcase Night, so today is preparation.

### Science track: Present Your Findings (90 min)

Each Scout puts together a short explanation of their experiment for [Science & Art Showcase Night](science-and-art-showcase-night.md) — what they tested, what they predicted, what actually happened across both runs, and why (the actual science behind the result). A simple poster or a few index cards of notes works well; the requirement is explaining results clearly to others, not a polished slideshow.

**Materials:** poster paper/card, pens, each Scout's own recorded findings from [Run Your Experiment](run-your-experiment-and-paint-a-landscape.md).

### Art track: Prepare Your Showcase (90 min)

Each Scout selects which pieces to show at [Science & Art Showcase Night](science-and-art-showcase-night.md) — the three required illustrations from this term plus any other recent work they're proud of — and mounts or arranges them for display (simple card mounts, a display board, or laid out on a table).

**Materials:** the three completed illustrations plus any other recent work, mounting card/backing paper, display boards or tables.

### Track share-back (10 min, within each track)

Each track does a quick dry run within their own group — Science Scouts give their explanation to a partner, Art Scouts show their arranged display to a partner — so nerves are settled before Showcase Night.

### Close (10 min, joint)

Both tracks reconvene, notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Encourage Scouts to bring in any older art pieces from home for the Art track's "selection of recent work," not just this term's three — the requirement doesn't limit it to work made this term.
- Leaders should note each Scout's readiness for Showcase Night, flagging anyone who needs a bit more support before presenting or exhibiting.
