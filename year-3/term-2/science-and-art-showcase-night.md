# Science & Art Showcase Night

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Science & Art

**Fits the term because:** the culmination of the term's two-track structure — Science and Art Scouts reunite to complete their final requirement in front of the whole Troop.

**Badge focus:** Science track — Scientist Activity Badge requirement, explaining experiment results to others; completes the badge. Art track — Artist Activity Badge requirement, showing a selection of recent work; completes the badge.

> **Scientist:** "...explain your results to others."

> **Artist:** "Show a selection of your own recent work."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Set up the exhibition |
| 0:20–1:10 | 50 min | Science presentations |
| 1:10–1:50 | 40 min | Art exhibition walk-round |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain that tonight both tracks complete their final requirement — Science Scouts present, Art Scouts exhibit — in front of the whole Troop.

### Set up the exhibition (10 min)

Art Scouts set up their mounted/arranged pieces around the room while Science Scouts do a final check of their presentation notes.

### Science presentations (50 min)

Each Science Scout gives their short explanation of their experiment to the room (or in smaller rotating groups if numbers are large) — what they tested, predicted, found, and why. Art Scouts watch and ask questions as the audience.

### Art exhibition walk-round (40 min)

Science Scouts (and any parents present, if this runs as an open evening) walk round the Art exhibition, with each Art Scout talking through their displayed work when someone stops at it.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home. This session completes both Scientist and Artist Activity Badge requirements for anyone who's finished the term's full track.

## Notes

- Consider inviting parents to this session as an informal open evening — it works well for both tracks and doesn't need extra content beyond what's already prepared.
- Leaders should use tonight's presentations and exhibition as the final sign-off evidence for both badges, checking each Scout completed every requirement of their chosen track across the term.
