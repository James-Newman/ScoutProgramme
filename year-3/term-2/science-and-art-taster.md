# Science & Art Taster

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Science & Art

**Fits the term because:** this term's badge is genuinely either/or — Scientist Activity Badge or Artist Activity Badge — so every Scout needs a real taste of both before committing to a track for the rest of the term.

**Badge focus:** none required yet — this session is a taster for both Scientist Activity Badge and Artist Activity Badge, setting up which track each Scout follows for the rest of the term.

This term runs two parallel tracks from here on: **Science** (working towards Scientist Activity Badge, Option 1) and **Art** (working towards Artist Activity Badge, Option 1). Every remaining core-content session in this term has a Science half and an Art half, run simultaneously in separate spaces with Scouts split by their chosen track.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain the term's two-track structure and that today gives everyone a taste of both before choosing.

### Science taste: quick experiments (40 min)

As a whole group, run two or three quick, visual science demonstrations connected to Scouting activities or hobbies — why a fire needs oxygen (the "fire triangle" demonstrated with a snuffed candle), why a compass points north (a simple magnetism demo), or why a pulley system reduces effort (a simple block-and-tackle demo). Let Scouts have a go themselves where safe to do so, not just watch.

**Materials:** candles/jars for the fire-triangle demo, a compass and magnets, a simple pulley/rope set-up.

### Art taste: quick sketches (40 min)

As a whole group, run a short, low-pressure sketching exercise — five minutes each on a quick scene, a still-life object, and a rough landscape sketch (mirroring the three subjects Artist Activity Badge itself asks for later, but at taster pace, not full pieces).

**Materials:** paper, pencils, an object to sketch, a view or photo of a landscape.

### Choose your track (20 min)

Each Scout picks Science or Art for the rest of the term, based on today's taste of both. Track sizes don't need to be even. Explain that the rest of the term will run Science and Art content in parallel each week, and both tracks meet back together for the term's closing [Science & Art Showcase Night](science-and-art-showcase-night.md).

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs two leaders (or a leader and a confident helper) able to run the Science and Art tastes simultaneously, or run them as a whole-group sequence if only one adult is available to lead content.
- Leaders should note each Scout's chosen track, since every remaining core-content session this term needs it to know which group a Scout joins.
