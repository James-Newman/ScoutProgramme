## Badge requirement(s)

Sources: [Scouts Scientist Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/scientist/) and [Scouts Artist Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/artist/) — check the live pages before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association. Each badge offers two options; a Scout only needs to complete one badge, via one option.

**Scientist, Option 1:**

> "Explore and discuss the science behind two Scouting activities or hobbies."

> Complete one of: plan and complete your own experiment exploring the science behind one Scouting activity or hobby, record findings, and explain results to others; or plan and run an activity, demonstration, or presentation to help others understand the science behind a Scouting activity or hobby.

**Scientist, Option 2:**

> Plan and complete three science experiments or activities (with adult approval); for each, change something about the experiment and try again at least once, predicting outcomes beforehand, and "show that you understand the science behind your experiment or activity."

> Identify how one experiment links to the real world and explain it to others.

**Artist, Option 1 (Artist):**

> "Paint, draw or illustrate each of these: a scene from a story, a person or object, a landscape."

> "Show a selection of your own recent work."

**Artist, Option 2 (Arts Enthusiast):**

> "Choose a favourite art form or artist to take an active interest in." Describe two connected visits, show good knowledge of an aspect of the interest, and list major events/exhibitions/venues connected with it, explaining why they matter.
