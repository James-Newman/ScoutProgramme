# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Scouting Science

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Science Showcase Night](science-showcase-night.md) and the sessions leading up to it.

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened last session; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive every badge they've completed in full this term: **Scientist Activity Badge** (this term's own focus badge, via the term's three experiments and [Science Showcase Night](science-showcase-night.md)) and **Entertainer Activity Badge**, via [`filler-sessions/entertainer.md`](../../filler-sessions/entertainer.md). This is a Spring term, so there's no Shooting Session or Little O this time round — Master at Arms and Orienteer simply aren't in play this term. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates for Scientist and Entertainer Activity Badge.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **Mad scientist mixture** — guess the outcome of a safe, visual kitchen-cupboard reaction (baking soda and vinegar, for instance) before it happens.
- **One-minute masterpiece** — draw a given object in under a minute, judged on recognisability.
- **Tallest tower** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style.

**Materials:** baking soda, vinegar, a tray; paper and pens, tape, a colander and water.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Term 3: Environmental Conservation.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session.
