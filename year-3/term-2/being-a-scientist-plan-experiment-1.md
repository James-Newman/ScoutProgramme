# Being a Scientist: Plan Experiment 1

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Scouting Science

**Fits the term because:** launches the term's three-experiment structure, giving Scouts the process and safety grounding they'll reuse for [Experiment 2](experiment-2-water-filtration.md) and [Experiment 3](experiment-3-insulation-test.md).

**Badge focus:** Scientist Activity Badge requirement — planning the first of three science experiments, checked with an adult first.

> "Plan and complete three science experiments or activities. Check your plan with an adult first, then for each experiment: 'Change something about the experiment or activity and try it again, at least once. Predict what you think will happen and find out if you were right.' 'Show that you understand the science behind your experiment or activity.'"

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain the term's shape: three experiments across the term, each changed and re-tried, then one explained to the whole Troop for its real-world link at [Science Showcase Night](science-showcase-night.md).

### What does being a scientist actually involve? (15 min)

Cover the basic process this term will use for every experiment: plan it, predict the outcome, get it checked by an adult, run it, change one thing, run it again, and see if the prediction held up. Emphasise that a "wrong" prediction is still a good result — it's still real science.

### Introducing Experiment 1: fire-lighting speed (20 min)

Set up the first experiment: which fire-lighting method (or which tinder) lights fastest? Patrols brainstorm what they could test — dry versus damp tinder, cotton wool versus dry grass, flint-and-steel versus matches — and pick one comparison each.

### Plan & predict (45 min)

Each patrol fills in an experiment plan: what they're testing, what they predict will happen and why, what they'll change for the second run, and how they'll measure/time the result (a stopwatch works well here). Push for a genuine prediction with reasoning, not a guess.

**Materials:** [experiment-plan template](../../resources/experiment-plan-template.md), one per patrol.

### Adult sign-off (20 min)

A leader reviews each patrol's plan for safety (fire-lighting materials, supervision, extinguishing method) and signs it off, ready for [Experiment 1: Run, Change & Retry](experiment-1-run-change-and-retry.md) next session. Flag anything that needs gathering in the week between sessions.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs a fire safety brief and appropriate supervision/extinguishing equipment on hand even for this planning session, since patrols may want to test small samples of tinder while planning.
- Leaders should note each patrol's signed-off plan and prediction, ready to check against actual results next session.
