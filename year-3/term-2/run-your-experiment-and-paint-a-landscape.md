# Run Your Experiment / Paint a Landscape

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Science & Art

**Fits the term because:** the third parallel-track session — Science Scouts carry out the experiment they planned last session; Art Scouts complete their third and final required illustration.

**Badge focus:** Science track — Scientist Activity Badge requirement, completing the planned experiment and recording findings. Art track — Artist Activity Badge requirement, the last of three required illustrations.

> **Scientist:** "Plan and complete your own experiment exploring the science behind one Scouting activity or hobby, record your findings, and explain your results to others."

> **Artist:** "Paint, draw or illustrate each of these: a scene from a story, a person or object, a landscape."

## Running order (both tracks run in parallel)

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome (joint) |
| 0:10–1:40 | 90 min | Track content |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Track share-back |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close (joint) |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min, joint)

Notices, opening, split into Science and Art groups.

### Science track: Run Your Experiment (90 min)

Each Scout runs the experiment they planned in [Plan Your Experiment](plan-your-experiment-and-draw-a-person-or-object.md), using the materials gathered since. Record actual results against the prediction made last session, and — per the requirement's own wording — change one variable and try again at least once (a different fabric, a different knot, a different fire-lighting technique) to see if the result changes.

**Materials:** whatever each Scout's [experiment plan](../../resources/experiment-plan-template.md) calls for — gathered in advance based on last session's sign-off.

### Art track: Paint a Landscape (90 min)

Each Scout paints or draws a landscape — a local view from memory, a reference photo, or looking out of a window — completing the third and final subject Artist Activity Badge asks for.

**Materials:** paper, pencils, paints/coloured pencils/markers, reference photos of landscapes if painting from memory isn't practical.

### Track share-back (10 min, within each track)

Science Scouts note down their actual result against their prediction; Art Scouts hold up their finished landscape.

### Close (10 min, joint)

Both tracks reconvene, notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- The "change something and try again" step is part of the requirement's own wording — make sure each Scout genuinely repeats their experiment with one changed variable, not just once.
- Leaders should note each Science Scout's recorded findings (prediction vs. actual, across both runs) and each Art Scout's finished landscape, as evidence for their respective requirements — Art Scouts now have all three required pieces, ready for [Present Your Findings / Prepare Your Showcase](present-your-findings-and-prepare-your-showcase.md).
