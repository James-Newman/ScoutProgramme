# Science Showcase Night

**Term:** Year 3, Term 2 — Scouting Science

**Fits the term because:** the culmination of the term's three experiments — Scouts present their real-world link explanation to the whole Troop, completing the badge.

**Badge focus:** Scientist Activity Badge requirement — explaining the real-world link to others; completes the badge.

> "Find out how one of your experiments or activities links to the real world. Then, explain it to others."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Set up |
| 0:20–1:40 | 80 min | Patrol presentations |
| 1:40–1:55 | 15 min | Round of questions |
| 1:55–2:00 | 5 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain that tonight each patrol presents their real-world link from [Real-World Link Research](real-world-link-research.md), completing the badge for the term.

### Set up (10 min)

Patrols lay out any props, posters, or leftover experiment materials (a wrapped test cup, a mini filter, fire-lighting kit) they'd like to show alongside their explanation.

### Patrol presentations (80 min)

Each patrol presents their chosen experiment, its result, and the real-world link they researched — a genuine explanation, not just a summary of the experiment itself. Keep it informal; consider inviting parents as an open evening.

### Round of questions (15 min)

After each patrol presents (or at the end, if time's short), the room asks one or two questions — this is a good, low-pressure way to check the explanation genuinely lands with an audience, not just the patrol itself.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home. This completes Scientist Activity Badge for anyone who's finished all three experiments and tonight's explanation.

## Notes

- Consider inviting parents to this session as an informal open evening — a good, low-effort way to make the badge completion feel like a genuine occasion.
- Leaders should use tonight's presentations as the final sign-off evidence for the real-world-link requirement, checking each Scout has also completed all three experiments from earlier in the term.
