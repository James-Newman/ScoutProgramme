# Year 3, Term 3 (Summer) — Environmental Conservation

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 3, term 3) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: researching an environmental issue important to the local community, taking part in an activity or project that improves local conservation, and running a campaign to raise awareness of an environmental issue.
Badges earned: **Environmental Conservation Activity Badge**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Time on the Water.

This term resolves the oldest open TBD flag in `data/terms.yaml`: Environmental Conservation Activity Badge was displaced from Year 1, Term 2 when that term became the Emergency Aid term, and hadn't been re-slotted anywhere until now — the last remaining piece of that term's original "Global Citizens & Conservation" theme (the other half, Global Issues, was resolved into Year 2, Term 3). This is also the final term of the full 3-year programme.

Every term's 12 weeks follow the same shape: one [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md), one [Night Hike](night-hike.md), one [Camp](camp.md), one [External Activity](external-activity.md), and one [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) session — five fixed slots every term, regardless of theme. This is a Summer term, so there's no Shooting Session or Little O this time round — those only recur in each year's Autumn term. That leaves seven weeks for the term's own badge content.

**This term needs all 7 of those core-content weeks for Environmental Conservation Activity Badge itself.** [Environmental Issue Research](environmental-issue-research.md) covers the first requirement and sets up a term-long focus issue. The second requirement — "take part in an activity or project that improves local conservation" — runs across three substantial sessions rather than being folded into Camp: [Conservation Project: Site Visit & Habitat Task](conservation-project-site-visit-and-habitat-task.md) (a genuine off-site placement with a local conservation partner), [Recycling & Energy Audit](recycling-and-energy-audit.md) (turning the lens on the Troop's own environmental footprint), and [Conservation Project: Wrap-Up & Litter-Pick](conservation-project-wrap-up-and-litter-pick.md) (a final local action plus reflection on the whole project). The third requirement — a genuine awareness campaign — runs across [Campaign: Choose Your Target & Plan](campaign-choose-your-target-and-plan.md) and [Campaign: Take Action](campaign-take-action.md), which needs a real target (an MP, a local agency, or a community group) and a real, sent action, not a mock exercise. **The term's one remaining free week carries [Local Knowledge](../../filler-sessions/local-knowledge.md)** — a complete, standalone badge in its own right, chosen for how naturally its "know your local area" premise pairs with this term's local-environment focus, and because it's the eighth and last of this plan's one-sitting filler badges to be used, completing the full set across the 3-year programme.

**Passive-completion check:** this term's conservation project is a separate, deeper undertaking from Year 1, Term 1's [Community & Environmental Action Day](../../year-1/term-1/community-and-environmental-action-day.md) (which satisfied World Challenge Award's environmental project requirement) — not a repeat of the same placement, though revisiting the same category of local partner is a reasonable, practical choice if that placement went well two years earlier.

## Requirement coverage

All 3 Environmental Conservation Activity Badge requirements are covered this term:

> "Find out about an environmental issue that is important to your local community." Potential topics include recycling and conservation, energy efficiency, local land use, pollution, coastal erosion, or tourism impact. — [Environmental Issue Research](environmental-issue-research.md)
>
> "Take part in an activity or project that improves local conservation." — [Conservation Project: Site Visit & Habitat Task](conservation-project-site-visit-and-habitat-task.md), [Recycling & Energy Audit](recycling-and-energy-audit.md), [Conservation Project: Wrap-Up & Litter-Pick](conservation-project-wrap-up-and-litter-pick.md)
>
> "Get involved in a campaign to make others aware of an environmental issue." — [Campaign: Choose Your Target & Plan](campaign-choose-your-target-and-plan.md), [Campaign: Take Action](campaign-take-action.md)

Source: [Scouts Environmental Conservation Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/environmental-conservation/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

## Advance preparation

Pulled from each session's own Notes — everything a leader needs to organise ahead of running this term, not just on the day.

### Bookings & external arrangements

- **[External Activity](external-activity.md)** — book well ahead, since popular slots get booked up. A water-based option fits this term's optional Time on the Water progression well.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** site booking — well ahead of the term.
- **[Conservation Project: Site Visit & Habitat Task](conservation-project-site-visit-and-habitat-task.md)** — contact a local nature reserve, wildlife trust, or council environmental team well ahead of the term, the same lead time as an External Activity booking.
- **[Local Knowledge](../../filler-sessions/local-knowledge.md)** — needs printed local street maps or map-based apps/tablets prepared in advance, covering the correct radius around the meeting place.

### Risk assessments

- **[Night Hike](night-hike.md)** — a pre-walked, risk-assessed route, plus a completed risk assessment for night activities.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — a completed camp risk assessment.
- **[Conservation Project: Site Visit & Habitat Task](conservation-project-site-visit-and-habitat-task.md)** — a placement risk assessment and enough leader/adult supervision for the specific task the host organisation sets.
- **[Conservation Project: Wrap-Up & Litter-Pick](conservation-project-wrap-up-and-litter-pick.md)** — coordinate with the local council or landowner if litter-picking somewhere other than genuinely public land.

### Content to source or prepare carefully

- **[Campaign: Take Action](campaign-take-action.md)** — a real letter to an MP or agency should go out for real, checked by a leader first; finding the correct contact details takes real research, so start it in [Campaign: Choose Your Target & Plan](campaign-choose-your-target-and-plan.md) with enough time.
- **[Recycling & Energy Audit](recycling-and-energy-audit.md)** — whatever change the Troop agrees to adopt needs a leader to actually own following through on it (ordering proper recycling bins, putting up signage).

### Equipment & materials to source

See the [material inventory](#material-inventory) below for the full list — the standouts needing advance sourcing are litter-picking kit (pickers, gloves, high-vis, sacks) and local street maps for Local Knowledge.

### Stock to order

Two badges complete in full this term — order stock for both from the [Scout Store](https://shop.scouts.org.uk/) ahead of [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md):

- **Environmental Conservation Activity Badge** — this term's own focus badge.
- **Local Knowledge Activity Badge** — completed via its filler session.

**Check individually, not a term-wide stock item:** Master at Arms Activity Badge — by this final term, review each Scout's full three-year Shooting Session tally (Year 1 Term 1, Year 2 Term 1, Year 3 Term 1) and sign off/order for anyone who's reached the six-session minimum and had a supervised contest. This is a Summer term, so Orienteer/Little O aren't in play this term specifically.
