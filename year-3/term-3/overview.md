## Badge requirement(s)

Source: [Scouts Environmental Conservation Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/environmental-conservation/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

> "Find out about an environmental issue that is important to your local community." Potential topics include recycling and conservation, energy efficiency, local land use, pollution, coastal erosion, or tourism impact.

> "Take part in an activity or project that improves local conservation." This can happen during a conservation camp or spread across at least five weekly sessions, covering areas like recycling, energy reduction, habitat protection, pollution, erosion, or flooding.

> "Get involved in a campaign to make others aware of an environmental issue." Examples include writing to MPs and local agencies or engaging with community groups.
