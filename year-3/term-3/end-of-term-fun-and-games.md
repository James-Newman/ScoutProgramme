# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 3, Term 3 — Environmental Conservation

**Fits the term because:** closes the term — and the whole 3-year programme — with a celebration rather than more assessment. The badge work is already done and logged from [Environmental Issue Research](environmental-issue-research.md), [Conservation Project: Site Visit & Habitat Task](conservation-project-site-visit-and-habitat-task.md), [Recycling & Energy Audit](recycling-and-energy-audit.md), [Campaign: Choose Your Target & Plan](campaign-choose-your-target-and-plan.md), [Campaign: Take Action](campaign-take-action.md), and [Conservation Project: Wrap-Up & Litter-Pick](conservation-project-wrap-up-and-litter-pick.md).

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges. As the final session of the 3-year programme, it's worth leaving a little extra room for reflection alongside the usual celebration.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:25–1:45 | 80 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:45–2:00 | 15 min | Three years in review & close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (15 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive every badge they've completed in full this term: **Environmental Conservation Activity Badge** (this term's own focus badge) and **Local Knowledge Activity Badge** (via [Local Knowledge](../../filler-sessions/local-knowledge.md)). This is a Summer term, so there's no Shooting Session or Little O this time round — Master at Arms and Orienteer simply aren't in play this term. Master at Arms Activity Badge itself should now be reviewed against the full record of Shooting Sessions across all three years (Year 1 Term 1, Year 2 Term 1, and Year 3 Term 1) — check whether each Scout has now reached the six-session minimum and had a supervised contest, and sign off anyone who's completed it. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates for Environmental Conservation, Local Knowledge, and (where individual records show it's due) Master at Arms.

### Taskmaster challenges (80 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **Recycling relay** — sort a mixed pile of items into the correct recycling/waste stream against the clock.
- **[Human sculpture](https://www.icebreakerspot.com/activities/human-sculptures)** — patrols freeze into a shape representing a given word, other patrols guess.
- **[Tallest tower](https://www.icebreakerspot.com/activities/newspaper-tower-challenge)** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the whole three years.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style.

**Materials:** mixed recyclable/waste items for the sorting relay, paper and pens, tape, a colander and water.

### Three years in review & close (15 min)

Since this closes the full 3-year programme, take a bit longer than usual: a quick round where each Scout names one favourite session or badge from across the three years, followed by notices, thought for the week, and home.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format matters more than the specific tasks.
- This is the final term of the 3-year plan — check Online Scout Manager (or equivalent) for any Scout whose Master at Arms tally, Orienteer sign-off, or Outdoor Challenge Award/Adventure Challenge Award/Teamwork Challenge Award progress needs picking up individually now the structured programme has ended.
