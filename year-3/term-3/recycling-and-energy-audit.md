# Recycling & Energy Audit

**Term:** Year 3, Term 3 — Environmental Conservation

**Fits the term because:** the second piece of this term's conservation project, turning the lens on the Troop's own environmental footprint rather than an external site.

**Badge focus:** Environmental Conservation Activity Badge requirement — taking part in an activity or project that improves local conservation (part 2 of 3, alongside [Site Visit & Habitat Task](conservation-project-site-visit-and-habitat-task.md) and [Conservation Project Wrap-Up & Litter-Pick](conservation-project-wrap-up-and-litter-pick.md)).

> "Take part in an activity or project that improves local conservation." Areas this can cover include recycling, energy reduction, habitat protection, pollution, erosion, or flooding.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain today's task: patrols audit the Scout hall/meeting place itself for recycling and energy waste, then design real improvements.

### Briefing (10 min)

Explain what to look for: what's currently recycled versus binned, lighting left on unnecessarily, heating/insulation issues, single-use items that could be swapped for reusable ones.

### Audit the meeting place (40 min)

In patrols, walk round the meeting place noting what's currently recycled, what waste ends up in general bins that shouldn't, and any obvious energy waste (lights on in empty rooms, doors left open with heating on, and so on).

**Materials:** clipboards, an [audit checklist](../../resources/energy-and-recycling-audit-checklist.md), one per patrol.

### Design a recycling scheme (30 min)

Each patrol designs one concrete, genuinely actionable improvement — a proper recycling bin system with clear labelling, a "lights off" reminder scheme, a reusable-cup policy for meetings, or similar. Push for something the Troop could actually adopt, not just an idea on paper.

### Present & agree changes (20 min)

Each patrol presents their proposed improvement. As a group (with leader input on what's practical), agree at least one genuine change to actually implement from today onwards — a real, lasting improvement to how the Troop runs its meeting place.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Whatever change gets agreed needs a leader to actually own following through on it (ordering proper recycling bins, putting up signage) — note this before closing so it doesn't quietly drop.
- Leaders should note the audit findings and which genuine change the Troop adopts, as evidence for this requirement.
