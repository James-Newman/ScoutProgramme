# Campaign: Take Action

**Term:** Year 3, Term 3 — Environmental Conservation

**Fits the term because:** carries [Campaign: Choose Your Target & Plan](campaign-choose-your-target-and-plan.md) through into a genuine, sent, real-world action — completing the badge's awareness-campaign requirement.

**Badge focus:** Environmental Conservation Activity Badge requirement — getting involved in a campaign to raise awareness of an environmental issue (action half; completes this requirement).

> "Get involved in a campaign to make others aware of an environmental issue." Examples include writing to MPs and local agencies or engaging with community groups.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Finalise the message |
| 0:30–1:30 | 60 min | Take action |
| 1:30–1:50 | 20 min | Send it / set it up |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Recap each patrol's target and plan from last session.

### Finalise the message (20 min)

Each patrol reviews and finalises their draft letter/email or community-engagement materials from [Campaign: Choose Your Target & Plan](campaign-choose-your-target-and-plan.md) — checking it's clear, polite, and makes a specific ask.

### Take action (60 min)

Patrols carry out their planned action: writing and finalising a formal letter/email to an MP or agency, or preparing a poster/stall/information pack for a community group. If a patrol is running a community engagement rather than a letter, use this time to prepare materials for delivering it (a stall design, a short presentation, flyers).

**Materials:** paper and envelopes/printer for a formal letter, or poster/stall materials for community engagement.

### Send it / set it up (20 min)

Each patrol either sends their letter/email for real (checking with a leader first — this is a genuine action, not a mock exercise) or, for community engagement, confirms concrete next steps for actually delivering it (a date for a stall, a group to approach, a way to display posters publicly).

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- A real letter to an MP or agency should go out for real this session, checked by a leader first — "get involved in a campaign" needs a genuine action, not a drafted-but-unsent letter.
- If a reply from an MP or agency arrives after the term ends, that's fine and worth sharing with the group whenever it comes in — the requirement is the genuine attempt, not a guaranteed response.
- Leaders should note each patrol's completed action (a sent letter, a scheduled community engagement) as the final evidence for this requirement.
