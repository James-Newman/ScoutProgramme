# Conservation Project: Site Visit & Habitat Task

**Term:** Year 3, Term 3 — Environmental Conservation

**Fits the term because:** the first, most substantial piece of this term's conservation project — a genuine hands-on placement with a real local conservation partner, following on from [Environmental Issue Research](environmental-issue-research.md)'s choice of focus issue.

**Badge focus:** Environmental Conservation Activity Badge requirement — taking part in an activity or project that improves local conservation (part 1 of 3, alongside [Recycling & Energy Audit](recycling-and-energy-audit.md) and [Conservation Project Wrap-Up & Litter-Pick](conservation-project-wrap-up-and-litter-pick.md)).

> "Take part in an activity or project that improves local conservation." Areas this can cover include recycling, energy reduction, habitat protection, pollution, erosion, or flooding.

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with a half-day (or full-day) placement at a local conservation site, similar in shape to a booked External Activity.

## Running order (example, half-day)

| When | Block |
| --- | --- |
| Arrival | Introduction from the host organisation, safety briefing |
| Morning/early afternoon | Hands-on habitat task (litter-picking, planting, clearing invasive species, building/repairing wildlife habitat features — whatever the host needs) |
| Partway through | Host talks through the site's conservation challenges and why the task matters |
| Before departure | Group discussion: what's the site's biggest ongoing conservation challenge, and how does today's task help with it? |

## Session detail

### Choosing and booking a placement

Contact a local nature reserve, wildlife trust, or council environmental team well ahead of the term — a half-day slot for a group of Scouts needs the same lead time as an External Activity booking. Ask specifically what genuine hands-on task the group can help with; the requirement needs "take part in," not a passive tour. Where possible, pick a placement connected to the term's focus issue chosen in [Environmental Issue Research](environmental-issue-research.md).

### On the day

Carry out the hands-on habitat task the host organisation has set. Work in the badge's own prompt naturally into a pause during the placement: what conservation challenge does this task address, and why does it matter for this specific site?

### Wrap-up

Before everyone leaves, a quick round: one thing they did that genuinely helped, one thing that surprised them about the site's conservation needs.

## Notes

- Needs a placement risk assessment and enough leader/adult supervision for the specific task the host organisation sets — follow the group's own safety policy for off-site activities, in the same way as [Camp](camp.md) or [External Activity](external-activity.md).
- This is a separate, deeper placement from Year 1, Term 1's [Community & Environmental Action Day](../../year-1/term-1/community-and-environmental-action-day.md) (which satisfied World Challenge Award's environmental project requirement) — not a repeat of the same visit, though revisiting the same category of local partner if that placement went well is a reasonable, practical choice.
- Leaders should note the hands-on task completed and engagement with the discussion, as evidence for this requirement.
