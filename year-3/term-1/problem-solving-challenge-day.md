# Problem-Solving Challenge Day

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** delivers the badge's problem-solving requirement in one sitting, running three distinct problem-solving activities back to back.

**Badge focus:** Skills Challenge Award requirement — taking part in at least three activities that require a number of problem-solving skills.

> Take part in at least three activities that require a number of problem-solving skills.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain that today's three challenges each count towards this term's problem-solving requirement — the point is genuine problem-solving under a constraint (limited materials, limited time, or both), not just following instructions.

### Challenge 1: The raft build (35 min)

Patrols design and build a small "raft" from limited materials (a few sheets of newspaper, tape, and enough weight to test with — a tennis ball or similar) that must support the weight without sinking or collapsing when placed in a shallow tray of water. Time-boxed to 25 minutes of build time, then a test round.

**Materials:** newspaper, tape, a shallow tray of water, a tennis ball or similar test weight (per patrol).

### Challenge 2: The escape puzzle (35 min)

A simple lock-box or [escape-room-style](https://www.scouts.org.uk/activities/run-your-own-escape-room/) puzzle chain: patrols work through a sequence of 3–4 linked clues/puzzles (a cipher, a riddle, a physical puzzle) to "unlock" a box or envelope at the end. Off-the-shelf puzzle box kits or a home-made paper-clue chain both work.

**Materials:** a lockable box and a chain of clues/puzzles (bought puzzle-box kit, or home-made), one set per patrol.

### Challenge 3: The tower of spaghetti (30 min)

The classic [Marshmallow Challenge](https://marshmallowchallenge.com/Welcome.html) — patrols build the tallest free-standing tower from dry spaghetti and tape that can support a marshmallow (or similar small weight) on top, within a strict time limit.

**Materials:** dry spaghetti, tape, string, a marshmallow (or similar small weight) per patrol.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Any three genuinely problem-solving activities work here — swap in local favourites if these three don't fit what's available.
- Leaders should note each Scout's genuine participation across all three challenges as the evidence for this requirement.
