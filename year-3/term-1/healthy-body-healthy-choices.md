# Healthy Body, Healthy Choices

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** covers the badge's health-knowledge requirement in one sitting, pairing the risks of smoking/alcohol/drugs with the more everyday habits of diet, hydration, and sleep.

**Badge focus:** Skills Challenge Award requirement — researching the dangers of smoking, alcohol, and drugs, and understanding diet, hydration, and sleep.

> Research and explain the dangers and harmful effects of smoking, alcohol, and drugs, and understand the importance of diet in maintaining a healthy body, including a balanced diet, staying hydrated, and a regular sleep pattern.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### Smoking, alcohol & drugs research (40 min)

In small groups, each researches one of the three (smoking, alcohol, drugs) using age-appropriate, reputable sources — [NHS Live Well](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/) and [Talk to Frank](https://www.talktofrank.com/) are good starting points. Each group finds out the real health effects, short and long-term, and one or two facts that would genuinely surprise their peers.

**Materials:** internet access (phones/tablets/laptops), or pre-printed briefing notes as a fallback.

### Present back (20 min)

Each group briefly presents their findings to the room — keep it fact-led and matter-of-fact rather than preachy, letting the research itself make the point.

### Balanced diet & hydration (25 min)

Teach the basics of a balanced diet (the Eatwell Guide's food groups) and why staying hydrated matters for concentration, energy, and physical performance. A quick sorting game — patrols sort a pile of food/drink cards into food groups, then identify which drinks actually hydrate well versus poorly — makes this practical rather than a lecture.

**Materials:** [food and drink sorting cards](../../resources/food-and-drink-sorting-cards.md), an Eatwell Guide poster/printout.

### Sleep matters (20 min)

Cover why a regular sleep pattern matters for growing teenagers — concentration, mood, growth, and recovery from physical activity (a good tie-back to this term's Physical Activity requirement). Discuss practical habits: a consistent bedtime, screens off before bed, a wind-down routine.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Keep the tone factual and non-judgemental — the requirement is understanding the dangers/importance, not a lecture on behaviour.
- Leaders should note each group's research findings and engagement with the diet/hydration/sleep content, as the evidence for this requirement.
