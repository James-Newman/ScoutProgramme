# Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** by this point in the term, four to six weeks have passed since [Physical Activity Kickoff](physical-activity-kickoff.md), giving genuine progress records to review and sign off.

**Badge focus:** Skills Challenge Award requirement — completing the four-to-six-week physical activity record, showing improvement.

> Regularly take part in physical activities over a period of four to six weeks, keeping a record showing your improvement, understanding the importance of warm up and warm down exercises.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Warm up recap |
| 0:20–1:00 | 40 min | Review your progress record |
| 1:00–1:40 | 40 min | Final activity session |
| 1:40–1:55 | 15 min | Share your improvement |
| 1:55–2:00 | 5 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### Warm up recap (10 min)

Quick recap of why warm up and warm down matter, then lead the group through a proper warm up together — checking the habit's actually stuck since [Physical Activity Kickoff](physical-activity-kickoff.md).

### Review your progress record (40 min)

Each Scout brings their progress log from the last four to six weeks and reviews it one-to-one with a leader (or in small groups) — what did they do, how regularly, and what actual improvement shows up (faster, longer, more reps, better technique). Anyone whose log is thin gets a chance to talk through what got in the way, and whether a short extension or a lighter bar for "improvement" is fair given genuine circumstances.

**Materials:** each Scout's own [progress log](../../resources/physical-activity-progress-log.md) from the last several weeks.

### Final activity session (40 min)

As a group, do one more proper session of physical activity together — a genuine, log-worthy final entry that also functions as a shared celebration of the requirement's end.

**Materials:** whatever the group activity needs — cones, balls, open space.

### Share your improvement (15 min)

Quick round: each Scout shares one concrete way they've improved over the four to six weeks.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- If a Scout genuinely hasn't kept a regular record, use this session to agree a short, realistic extension rather than failing the requirement outright — the point is a genuine habit, not punctuality on a specific date.
- Leaders should use each Scout's reviewed progress log and today's improvement share as the evidence for signing off this requirement.
