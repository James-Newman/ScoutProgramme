# Life Skill: Bike Maintenance & Puncture Repair

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** delivers the badge's practical life-skill requirement using one of the badge's own suggested examples — chosen deliberately over the two-course-meal option, since Chef Activity Badge already covers cooking in Year 1, Term 3, and repeating it here wouldn't be a genuinely new skill for most Scouts.

**Badge focus:** Skills Challenge Award requirement — learning a practical life skill agreed with a leader, then putting it into practice.

> Learn a practical life skill agreed with your leader (examples include mending or customising clothing, cooking and serving a two-course meal for at least four people, fixing a puncture on a bike, washing up, using a washing machine, ironing, and changing lightbulbs), then put it into practice.

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain that today's practical life skill — bike maintenance and puncture repair — is one of the badge's own suggested examples, chosen since Chef Activity Badge already covers the cooking option for this group.

### Bike basics (20 min)

Cover the basic parts of a bike and simple checks worth doing regularly — tyre pressure, brakes, chain, and general road-worthiness (the "M-check" is a good simple routine to teach).

### Fixing a puncture: demonstration (30 min)

A leader demonstrates removing a wheel, finding the puncture, removing and repairing (or replacing) the inner tube, and refitting the wheel — talking through each step clearly enough that Scouts could repeat it unprompted afterwards.

**Materials:** a bike with a wheel that can be removed, tyre levers, a puncture repair kit or spare inner tube, a pump.

### Fixing a puncture: practice (50 min)

Each Scout gets genuine hands-on practice fixing a puncture (on a spare wheel/tube setup, or a real bike if enough are available) rather than just watching — this is the "put it into practice" half of the requirement, not optional extra time.

**Materials:** enough spare wheels/inner tubes, tyre levers, puncture repair kits, and pumps for genuinely hands-on small-group practice — borrow spare bikes/wheels from parents if the group doesn't own enough.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs enough spare wheels/tubes and repair kits for small-group hands-on practice, not just one demonstration bike — borrowing from parents ahead of the session is worth organising early.
- Leaders should note which Scouts genuinely completed a puncture repair themselves (not just watched), as the evidence for the "put it into practice" half of this requirement.
