# Physical Activity Kickoff

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** launches the term's longest-running requirement early, so there's enough real time left in the term for it to genuinely run its course before [Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md) reviews it.

**Badge focus:** Skills Challenge Award requirement — regularly taking part in physical activities over four to six weeks, keeping a progress record, and understanding warm up/warm down.

> Regularly take part in physical activities over a period of four to six weeks, keeping a record showing your improvement, understanding the importance of warm up and warm down exercises.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain the requirement: over the next four to six weeks, each Scout regularly takes part in a physical activity of their choice and keeps a simple record of it, ready to review at [Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md) later in the term.

### Why warm up, why warm down (20 min)

Teach the basics: what a warm up actually does (raises heart rate and muscle temperature, reduces injury risk) and what a warm down does (helps recovery, reduces stiffness). Demonstrate a simple warm up (light jogging on the spot, dynamic stretches) and warm down (walking pace, static stretches) that Scouts can reuse for their own chosen activity.

### Choose your activity (20 min)

Each Scout picks a physical activity to track for the next four to six weeks — running, cycling, swimming, football training, dance, or anything genuinely regular and physical. It doesn't need to be new; the requirement is about consistency and recorded improvement, not novelty.

### Design your progress record (20 min)

Each Scout sets up a simple way to record their activity — a paper log, a notes app, or a simple app/watch if they already use one. Agree what "improvement" will look like for their chosen activity (faster time, more reps, longer duration, better technique) so the record actually shows progress, not just attendance.

**Materials:** a simple progress-log template, one per Scout.

### Get moving (35 min)

As a group, do a proper warm up, then a genuine bout of physical activity together (a group run, circuit stations, ball games) as a real first log entry everyone can point to, followed by a warm down.

**Materials:** whatever the group activity needs — cones, balls, open space.

### First log entry (10 min)

Each Scout fills in their first log entry from today's group activity, so the format is bedded in before they're tracking solo activities during the week.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home. Remind Scouts their next check-in is at [Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md).

## Notes

- Encourage parents/carers to help Scouts keep the log up during the week — most of this requirement happens outside the meeting.
- Leaders should note each Scout's chosen activity and starting point today, so there's a baseline to compare against at the wrap-up session.
