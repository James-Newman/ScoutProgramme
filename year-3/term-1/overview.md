# Year 3, Term 1 (Autumn) — Skills & Self-Reliance

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 3, term 1) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: regular physical activity over four to six weeks with a progress record, researching the risks of smoking/alcohol/drugs alongside diet, hydration, and sleep, learning and practising a practical life skill, and taking part in problem-solving activities.
Badges earned: **Skills Challenge Award**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Hikes Away (+1).

This is the first term of Year 3, which had previously been entirely removed from `data/terms.yaml` — its old content was a pre-redesign leftover from the original Google Doc import that had never been revisited and had known conflicts (see the note there). Skills Challenge Award fits back into this same Year 3, Term 1 slot from that old plan, now properly verified against the real badge requirements rather than the old, incorrect content. Year 3, Terms 2 and 3 have since been replanned too — see [`year-3/term-2/overview.md`](../term-2/overview.md) and [`year-3/term-3/overview.md`](../term-3/overview.md).

Every term's 12 weeks follow the same shape: one [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md), one [Night Hike](night-hike.md), one [Camp](camp.md), one [External Activity](external-activity.md), and one [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) session — five fixed slots every term, regardless of theme. Autumn terms add two more standing slots: one [Shooting Session](shooting-session.md) and one [Little O](little-o-orienteering.md). That's 7 fixed slots this term, leaving five weeks for the term's own badge content.

**This term needs all 5 of those free weeks for Skills Challenge Award itself.** [Physical Activity Kickoff](physical-activity-kickoff.md) launches the badge's four-to-six-week physical activity requirement early in the term, so there's genuine time for it to run its course before [Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md) reviews the progress records later on — the same bookend pattern used for Adventure Challenge Award's reflective session elsewhere in this plan. [Healthy Body, Healthy Choices](healthy-body-healthy-choices.md) covers the smoking/alcohol/drugs and diet/hydration/sleep requirement in one sitting, [Problem-Solving Challenge Day](problem-solving-challenge-day.md) runs three distinct problem-solving activities back to back, and [Life Skill: Bike Maintenance & Puncture Repair](life-skill-bike-maintenance-and-puncture-repair.md) delivers the practical life-skill requirement using one of the badge's own suggested examples — chosen deliberately over the two-course-meal option, since **Chef Activity Badge already covers cooking in Year 1, Term 3**, and repeating it here wouldn't be a genuinely new skill for most Scouts. **No one-sitting filler badge is used this term** — with four requirements needing five genuine sessions (the physical activity requirement alone needs two, bookending the term), all of the term's free capacity is legitimately spoken for by the focus badge itself.

## Requirement coverage

All 4 Skills Challenge Award requirements are covered this term:

> Regularly take part in physical activities over a period of four to six weeks, keeping a record showing your improvement, understanding the importance of warm up and warm down exercises. — [Physical Activity Kickoff](physical-activity-kickoff.md), [Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md)
>
> Research and explain the dangers and harmful effects of smoking, alcohol, and drugs, and understand the importance of diet in maintaining a healthy body, including a balanced diet, staying hydrated, and a regular sleep pattern. — [Healthy Body, Healthy Choices](healthy-body-healthy-choices.md)
>
> Learn a practical life skill agreed with your leader (examples include mending or customising clothing, cooking and serving a two-course meal for at least four people, fixing a puncture on a bike, washing up, using a washing machine, ironing, and changing lightbulbs), then put it into practice. — [Life Skill: Bike Maintenance & Puncture Repair](life-skill-bike-maintenance-and-puncture-repair.md)
>
> Take part in at least three activities that require a number of problem-solving skills. — [Problem-Solving Challenge Day](problem-solving-challenge-day.md)

Source: [Scouts Skills Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/awards/skills/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

## Passive completion — other badges this term's content touches

- **Orienteer Activity Badge** — completes in full via [Little O](little-o-orienteering.md), for any Scout who hasn't already earned it in Year 1, Term 1 or Year 2, Term 1 — by this point most of the group likely already holds it.
- **Master at Arms Activity Badge** — genuine progress via [Shooting Session](shooting-session.md) (the third of at least six sessions), but remains mid-progress this term, not complete.
- **Chef Activity Badge** — checked and confirmed no overlap: this term's life-skill requirement deliberately avoids the two-course-meal option, already earned in Year 1, Term 3, in favour of bike maintenance and puncture repair instead.
- **Outdoor Challenge Award** — [Camp](camp.md) carries its usual Nights Away and environment requirements, and [Night Hike](night-hike.md) its usual Countryside Code requirement, exactly as every term — listed here for completeness, not as new incidental overlap. All of this award's dedicated filler sessions are already used up as of Year 2, Term 2 (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)), so no new filler slots in this term.
- No other session this term showed a clear, defensible incidental overlap with a real Scouts-section badge's actual requirements — this is a prioritised check, not an exhaustive sweep.

## Advance preparation

Pulled from each session's own Notes — everything a leader needs to organise ahead of running this term, not just on the day.

### Bookings & external arrangements

- **[External Activity](external-activity.md)** — book well ahead, since popular slots get booked up. Pick something different from previous terms' bookings so it counts as a genuinely new activity towards the Adventure Challenge Award tally.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** site booking — well ahead of the term.
- **[Shooting Session](shooting-session.md)** — a qualified instructor and a suitable range, either an external provider or a leader holding the relevant NGB qualification.
- **[Little O](little-o-orienteering.md)** — three pre-set courses of increasing difficulty in the local park/woods; check whether the local park already has a permanent course installed.

### Risk assessments

- **[Night Hike](night-hike.md)** — a pre-walked, risk-assessed route, plus a completed risk assessment for night activities.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — a completed camp risk assessment.
- **[Shooting Session](shooting-session.md)** — following the group's own safety policy for shooting activities.
- **[Little O](little-o-orienteering.md)** — a risk assessment for the specific venue.
- **[Life Skill: Bike Maintenance & Puncture Repair](life-skill-bike-maintenance-and-puncture-repair.md)** — check bikes/wheels used for practice are genuinely safe to handle (no sharp rim damage, tyres in reasonable condition).

### Content to source or prepare carefully

- **[Physical Activity Kickoff](physical-activity-kickoff.md)** / **[Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md)** — encourage parents/carers to help Scouts keep their progress log up during the week between these two sessions; agree upfront how to handle a Scout whose log is thin by the wrap-up.
- **[Life Skill: Bike Maintenance & Puncture Repair](life-skill-bike-maintenance-and-puncture-repair.md)** — needs enough spare wheels/tubes and repair kits for genuinely hands-on small-group practice, not just one demonstration bike; borrowing from parents ahead of the session is worth organising early.
- **[Little O](little-o-orienteering.md)** — check who's already earned Orienteer Activity Badge in Year 1, Term 1 or Year 2, Term 1, so the session's own sign-off doesn't assume everyone needs a fresh one.
- **[Shooting Session](shooting-session.md)** — carry forward each Scout's running tally towards the six-session minimum, and note whether this occurrence should be run as the one formally supervised contest.
