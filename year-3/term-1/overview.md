## Badge requirement(s)

Source: [Scouts Skills Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/awards/skills/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

> Regularly take part in physical activities over a period of four to six weeks, keeping a record showing your improvement, understanding the importance of warm up and warm down exercises.

> Research and explain the dangers and harmful effects of smoking, alcohol, and drugs, and understand the importance of diet in maintaining a healthy body, including a balanced diet, staying hydrated, and a regular sleep pattern.

> Learn a practical life skill agreed with your leader (examples include mending or customising clothing, cooking and serving a two-course meal for at least four people, fixing a puncture on a bike, washing up, using a washing machine, ironing, and changing lightbulbs), then put it into practice.

> Take part in at least three activities that require a number of problem-solving skills.
