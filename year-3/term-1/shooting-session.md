# Shooting Session

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** every Autumn term includes one Shooting Session regardless of theme — a supervised air rifle/pistol range session that's this troop's recurring contribution toward the Master at Arms Activity Badge, alongside [`filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md`](../../filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md)'s hall-based safety-rules session. Deliberately generic, like [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md) and [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) — reused unchanged (bar the term name above) as every future Autumn term's Shooting Session.

**Badge focus:** none for this term's own focus badge — but every occurrence of this session counts as one of Master at Arms Activity Badge's "at least six sessions" of training, and is the standing home for its supervised-contest requirement whenever a range visit includes one (see [`filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md`](../../filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md)). This is the third of at least six sessions across the three years, and a genuine, loggable physical activity outing for anyone tracking their Skills Challenge Award Physical Activity requirement this term.

> "Attend regular training sessions in a relevant activity like fencing, shooting or archery. Show how you've improved in technique. You should train for at least six sessions."

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with a supervised visit to an air rifle/pistol range, run by a qualified instructor (an external provider, or a suitably qualified leader). Treat the running order below as a shape to adjust to the specific range/provider, not a fixed script.

## Running order (example, ~2 hours at the range)

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Arrival & register |
| 0:10–0:25 | 15 min | Safety recap |
| 0:25–1:45 | 80 min | Supervised shooting |
| 1:45–1:55 | 10 min | Instructor feedback |
| 1:55–2:00 | 5 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Arrival & register (10 min)

Meet at the range/provider, register patrols, confirm any medical/consent requirements the provider needs.

### Safety recap (15 min)

Before any live shooting, the instructor (or a leader who's completed [`filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md`](../../filler-sessions/master-at-arms-activity-badge.md)'s safety-rules content) runs a quick recap of range commands, safe handling, and what to do if there's a malfunction.

### Supervised shooting (80 min)

Each Scout gets supervised time on the range, working on technique under instruction. Track improvement session to session — a simple scoring or grouping log per Scout works well and doubles as the evidence for "show how you've improved in technique."

### Instructor feedback (10 min)

Each Scout gets brief one-to-one feedback from the instructor on their performance and how to improve — this satisfies the supervised-contest requirement's feedback half whenever this particular session is run as a contest rather than plain practice (see Notes).

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs a qualified instructor and a suitable range — either an external provider or a leader holding the relevant NGB qualification, following the group's own safety policy for shooting activities.
- Leaders should track each Scout's running tally of sessions attended (toward the six-session minimum) and whether/when a supervised contest happened, e.g. in Online Scout Manager — this repo doesn't track individual Scout progress.
- Not every occurrence needs to be a formal contest — most should be plain supervised training; run at least one as an "officially supervised contest" across the three years to complete that separate requirement.
- Holders of the NSRA Youth Proficiency Scheme in Air Rifle or Air Pistol earn Master at Arms Activity Badge automatically and don't need to attend for that purpose (though they're welcome to, for the practice).
