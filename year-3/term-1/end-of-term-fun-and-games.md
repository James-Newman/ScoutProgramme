# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 3, Term 1 — Skills & Self-Reliance

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Physical Activity Kickoff](physical-activity-kickoff.md), [Healthy Body, Healthy Choices](healthy-body-healthy-choices.md), [Life Skill: Bike Maintenance & Puncture Repair](life-skill-bike-maintenance-and-puncture-repair.md), [Problem-Solving Challenge Day](problem-solving-challenge-day.md), and [Physical Activity Check-In & Wrap-Up](physical-activity-check-in-and-wrap-up.md).

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive every badge they've completed in full this term: **Skills Challenge Award** (this term's own focus badge) and **Orienteer Activity Badge** (from Little O, for anyone who hadn't already earned it in Year 1, Term 1 or Year 2, Term 1 — check individual records, since most of the group likely already holds it by now). **Master at Arms Activity Badge isn't ready to present yet** — this term's Shooting Session was only the third of at least six needed across the three years. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **Blindfold obstacle relay** — one Scout guided by voice alone through a simple obstacle course.
- **Human sculpture** — patrols freeze into a shape representing a given word, other patrols guess.
- **Tallest tower** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style.

**Materials:** simple obstacle-course items, blindfolds, paper and pens, tape, a colander and water.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Term 2: Science & Art.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session.
