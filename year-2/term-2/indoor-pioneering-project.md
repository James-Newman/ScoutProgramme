# Indoor Pioneering Project

**Term:** Year 2, Term 2 — Knots & Pioneering

**Fits the term because:** brings together every knot, lashing, and rope skill taught this term into the group indoor pioneering project Pioneer Activity Badge specifically asks for.

**Badge focus:** Pioneer Activity Badge requirement — as a group, plan, prepare for, and take part in an indoor pioneering project.

> "As a group, plan, prepare for, and take part in an indoor pioneering project."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Plan the build |
| 0:30–1:40 | 70 min | Build it |
| 1:40–1:55 | 15 min | Test and inspect |
| 1:55–2:00 | 5 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Announce the project: as a whole group (or as patrols working on one shared structure), build an indoor pioneering project — a simple tower, a camp gateway, or a small table or stool — using only the knots and lashings learned this term.

### Plan the build (20 min)

As a group, sketch the chosen design, list the spars and rope/cord needed, and assign roles — who's lashing which joint, who's checking each one. Planning as a group (not just leaders directing) is the actual requirement here, so let the Scouts drive the design discussion with a leader steering rather than dictating.

**Materials:** paper and pens for sketching the design.

### Build it (70 min)

Construct the project, patrols working on assigned sections or joints. Leaders check each lashing as it's completed rather than waiting until the end, so mistakes get caught and fixed while there's still time.

**Materials:** pioneering spars, lashing cord, ropes — the full kit built up across [Knots I](knots-i.md), [Knots II](knots-ii.md), [Lashings](lashings.md), and [Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md).

### Test and inspect (15 min)

As a group, test the finished structure is sound — gentle weight-bearing checks appropriate to what was built, not a stress test to destruction. Walk round it together and identify which knots and lashings hold it together.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home. A photo of the finished project is good evidence for this requirement.

## Notes

- Needs a full pioneering kit (spars, lashing cord, rope) and enough floor space to build and stand back from the finished structure.
- Leaders should note the group's planning involvement and the finished structure (a photo is good evidence) — this, together with the outdoor project at [Camp](camp.md), completes this requirement.
