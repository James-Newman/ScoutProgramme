# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 2, Term 2 — Knots & Pioneering

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Knots I](knots-i.md), [Knots II](knots-ii.md), [Lashings](lashings.md), [Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md), [Indoor Pioneering Project](indoor-pioneering-project.md), and [Camp](camp.md).

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive **Pioneer Activity Badge** — the term's own focus badge, against the six knots, three lashings, rope skills, and indoor/outdoor pioneering projects covered across the term. This is a Spring term, so there's no Shooting Session or Little O this time round — Master at Arms and Orienteer simply aren't in play this term, nothing to flag either way. The Outdoor Challenge Award has made real, tracked progress again this term (rope care, pioneering gadget, more Nights Away) but stays passive and accumulated, not a badge to present yet. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **Blindfold knot relay** — tie a named knot blindfolded, talked through by a teammate.
- **Human sculpture** — patrols freeze into a shape representing a given word, other patrols guess.
- **Tallest tower** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style.

**Materials:** rope for the blindfold relay, blindfolds, paper and pens, tape, a colander and water.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Term 3: Global Issues.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session.
