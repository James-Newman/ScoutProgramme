# Knots II

**Term:** Year 2, Term 2 — Knots & Pioneering

**Fits the term because:** the second of two dedicated knot-tying sessions, completing the six knots Pioneer Activity Badge asks for.

**Badge focus:** Pioneer Activity Badge requirement — name and tie six or more different knots (final three of six).

> "Name and tie six or more different knots, and name and tie three or more different lashings."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome & recap |
| 0:10–0:35 | 25 min | Bowline |
| 0:35–1:00 | 25 min | Sheet bend |

| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome & recap (10 min)

Notices, opening. Quick recap of last session's three knots — ask a few Scouts to demonstrate before moving on to new ones.

### Bowline (25 min)

Teach the bowline (a fixed loop that won't slip or jam) using "the rabbit comes out of the hole, round the tree, and back down the hole" as a memorable teach. Practice until confident — this one takes most Scouts a few goes.

**Materials:** one length of rope per Scout (roughly 1m).

### Sheet bend (25 min)

Teach the sheet bend (joining two ropes of different thickness, where a reef knot would fail) — a good moment to demonstrate why: try (and fail) to join a thick rope to thin string with a reef knot first, then show the sheet bend holding.

**Materials:** ropes of two different thicknesses.

### Timber hitch (30 min)

Teach the timber hitch (attaching a rope to a log or pole for dragging or hoisting) — practice by actually dragging a log or pioneering spar a short distance using the knot, so the Scouts feel it hold under real load.

**Materials:** a log or spar to drag; ropes from above.

### Six-knot circuit (20 min)

Set up six stations, one per knot taught across this session and [Knots I](knots-i.md). Patrols rotate in small groups, tying the named knot at each station against the clock, checked off by a leader. This is the natural point to sign off each Scout against all six knots.

**Materials:** [knot reference cards](../../resources/knot-reference-card.md), one per station.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs a log or spar sturdy enough to drag without snapping, plus the rope/pole kit from [Knots I](knots-i.md).
- Leaders should use the six-knot circuit to sign off each Scout against the full "six or more different knots" requirement — this is the natural completion point.
