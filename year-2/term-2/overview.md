# Year 2, Term 2 (Spring) — Knots & Pioneering

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 2, term 2) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: a group indoor and outdoor pioneering project, whipping and sealing rope ends, splicing, coiling and storing rope, using levers, pioneering safety, and naming and tying six or more knots and three or more lashings.
Badges earned: **Pioneer Activity Badge**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Hikes Away (+1).

This term resolves a TBD flag left open at the top of `data/terms.yaml`: Pioneer Activity Badge was displaced from Year 1, Term 3 when that term became Fire & Cooking, and hadn't been re-slotted anywhere until now.

Every term's 12 weeks follow the same shape: one [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md), one [Night Hike](night-hike.md), one [Camp](camp.md), one [External Activity](external-activity.md), and one [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) session — five fixed slots every term, regardless of theme. This is a Spring term, so there's no Shooting Session or Little O this time round — those only recur in each year's Autumn term. That leaves seven weeks for the term's own badge content.

**This term needs all 7 of those core-content weeks for Pioneer Activity Badge itself.** [Knots I](knots-i.md) and [Knots II](knots-ii.md) teach the six required knots (reef knot, clove hitch, round turn and two half hitches, bowline, sheet bend, timber hitch); [Lashings](lashings.md) teaches the three required lashings (square, diagonal, sheer), building directly on the clove hitch; [Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md) covers whipping/sealing, splicing, coiling/storing rope, using a lever, and pioneering safety; and [Indoor Pioneering Project](indoor-pioneering-project.md) brings it all together as the badge's required group indoor project. The badge's outdoor project resolves at [Camp](camp.md), which also doubles — deliberately — as the natural home for the Outdoor Challenge Award's pioneering-gadget requirement, since building an outdoor camp gadget with the term's own knots and lashings satisfies both in a single build. **No one-sitting filler badge is used this term**, but two Outdoor Challenge Award filler sessions ([Outdoor Safety & Risk Awareness](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/outdoor-safety-and-risk-awareness.md), [Camp Kit & Personal Equipment](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md)) are woven in rather than one-sitting badges, since neither of the three remaining one-sitting fillers (Entertainer, Media Relations & Marketing, Local Knowledge) fits this term thematically, and both chosen fillers genuinely tie in — safety ahead of a pioneering-heavy term, and kit ahead of Camp.

## Requirement coverage

All 3 Pioneer Activity Badge requirements are covered this term:

> "As a group, plan, prepare for, and take part in an indoor and an outdoor pioneering project." — [Indoor Pioneering Project](indoor-pioneering-project.md), [Camp](camp.md)
>
> "Show how to do the following: whip or seal the end of a rope; make a suitable splice in a rope; correctly coil and store a rope; use a lever; explain how to keep yourself and others safe while taking part in pioneering activities." — [Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md)
>
> "Name and tie six or more different knots, and name and tie three or more different lashings." — [Knots I](knots-i.md), [Knots II](knots-ii.md), [Lashings](lashings.md)

Source: [Scouts Pioneer Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/pioneer/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

## Advance preparation

Pulled from each session's own Notes — everything a leader needs to organise ahead of running this term, not just on the day.

### Bookings & external arrangements

- **[External Activity](external-activity.md)** — book well ahead, since popular slots get booked up. Pick something different from previous terms' bookings so it counts as a genuinely new activity towards the Adventure Challenge Award tally.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** site booking — well ahead of the term.

### Risk assessments

- **[Night Hike](night-hike.md)** — a pre-walked, risk-assessed route, plus a completed risk assessment for night activities.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — a completed camp risk assessment, plus enough pioneering spars and cord for an outdoor-scale build.
- **[Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md)** — heat-sealing rope ends needs close leader supervision; Scouts shouldn't handle the flame themselves.

### Content to source or prepare carefully

- **[Knots I](knots-i.md)** / **[Knots II](knots-ii.md)** / **[Lashings](lashings.md)** / **[Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md)** / **[Indoor Pioneering Project](indoor-pioneering-project.md)** — this term needs a genuinely specialist kit inventory (spars, ropes of varying length and thickness, lashing cord, mallets) built up and kept together across the whole term — see the [material inventory](#material-inventory) below.
- **[Outdoor Safety & Risk Awareness](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/outdoor-safety-and-risk-awareness.md)** — best slotted in shortly before [Night Hike](night-hike.md) so the buddy system and STOP mnemonic are fresh.

### Equipment & materials to source

See the [material inventory](#material-inventory) below for the full list — the standouts needing advance sourcing are enough pioneering spars/broom handles and sisal lashing cord to last five sessions plus Camp, a log or spar sturdy enough to drag, and rucksacks/borrowed camp kit for the kit-packing filler.
