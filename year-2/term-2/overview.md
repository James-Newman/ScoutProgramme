# Year 2, Term 2 (Spring) — Knots & Pioneering

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 2, term 2) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: a group indoor and outdoor pioneering project, whipping and sealing rope ends, splicing, coiling and storing rope, using levers, pioneering safety, and naming and tying six or more knots and three or more lashings.
Badges earned: **Pioneer Activity Badge**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Hikes Away (+1).

## Badge requirement(s)

Source: [Scouts Pioneer Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/pioneer/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

> "As a group, plan, prepare for, and take part in an indoor and an outdoor pioneering project."

> "Show how to do the following: whip or seal the end of a rope; make a suitable splice in a rope; correctly coil and store a rope; use a lever; explain how to keep yourself and others safe while taking part in pioneering activities."

> "Name and tie six or more different knots, and name and tie three or more different lashings."
