# Camp

**Term:** Year 2, Term 2 — Knots & Pioneering

**Fits the term because:** every term includes one Camp regardless of theme — it's this term's Nights Away opportunity, and (per the standing convention for every term's Camp) the home for two Outdoor Challenge Award requirements that accumulate passively regardless of the term's own focus badge. This term, Camp also hosts Pioneer Activity Badge's outdoor pioneering project, following on from [Indoor Pioneering Project](indoor-pioneering-project.md), and doubles as a natural home for the Outdoor Challenge Award's pioneering-gadget requirement.

**Badge focus:** Pioneer Activity Badge requirement — as a group, plan, prepare for, and take part in an outdoor pioneering project. This session is also the standing home for two passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirements (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)), this term's Nights Away (+2) staged-progression opportunity, and — via the same build — the Outdoor Challenge Award's pioneering-gadget requirement.

> "As a group, plan, prepare for, and take part in an indoor and an outdoor pioneering project."

> "Using knots that you have learned, build a simple pioneering project, object or camp gadget." (Outdoor Challenge Award)

> "Take an active part in at least eight nights away as a Scout. Four of the nights should be camping." (Outdoor Challenge Award)

> "Explore the environment of your camp and make sure you know where everything is. Respect the environment you are in and, at the end of the camp, leave the site as you found it." (Outdoor Challenge Award)

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with a residential weekend (typically Friday evening to Sunday afternoon).

## Running order (example, Friday evening – Sunday)

| When | Block |
| --- | --- |
| Friday, arrival | Arrive, patrols pitch and lay out camp largely independently |
| Friday evening | Informal games, campfire chat |
| Saturday morning | Plan and build the outdoor pioneering project/camp gadget in patrols |
| Saturday afternoon | Finish and test the build; free time |
| Saturday evening | Cook and serve dinner as a patrol; campfire |
| Sunday morning | Strike camp (and the pioneering build), patrol chores, pack away |
| Sunday, before departure | Debrief circle |

## Session detail

### Friday: arrival and settle-in

Patrols pitch and lay out camp with minimal leader input at this point. Walk each patrol round the whole site so they know where everything is (the "know where everything is" half of the passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirement).

### Saturday: outdoor pioneering project

Each patrol plans, then builds, an outdoor pioneering project or camp gadget using the knots and lashings learned this term — a camp gateway, a washing-up stand, a bench, a flagpole rig, or similar. Being outdoors and on a real site gives patrols a bigger scale and rougher materials than [Indoor Pioneering Project](indoor-pioneering-project.md) allowed, and satisfies both the Pioneer Activity Badge's outdoor-project requirement and the Outdoor Challenge Award's pioneering-gadget requirement in one build.

**Materials:** pioneering spars, lashing cord, ropes — reuse the term's kit built up across [Knots I](knots-i.md), [Knots II](knots-ii.md), [Lashings](lashings.md), and [Rope Care & Using a Lever](rope-care-and-using-a-lever.md).

### Saturday evening: cook, serve, campfire

Patrols cook and serve their evening meal, then a campfire to finish the day.

### Sunday: strike camp and debrief

Patrols dismantle their pioneering build alongside striking and packing away camp, then walk their pitch before leaving — litter picked, ground checked, nothing left that wasn't there Friday (the "leave the site as you found it" half of the passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirement). Finish with a short debrief circle before parents arrive.

## Notes

- Needs a completed camp risk assessment and site booking well ahead of the term, plus enough pioneering spars and cord for an outdoor-scale build.
- Counts towards this term's Nights Away (+2) staged progression, and — combined with every other term's Camp — towards the Outdoor Challenge Award's Nights Away requirement.
- A photo of the finished outdoor pioneering project is good evidence for both the Pioneer Activity Badge requirement and the Outdoor Challenge Award's pioneering-gadget requirement.
