# Lashings

**Term:** Year 2, Term 2 — Knots & Pioneering

**Fits the term because:** Pioneer Activity Badge's lashings requirement builds directly on the clove hitch from [Knots I](knots-i.md), which starts and finishes every lashing taught here.

**Badge focus:** Pioneer Activity Badge requirement — name and tie three or more different lashings.

> "Name and tie six or more different knots, and name and tie three or more different lashings."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome & clove hitch recap |
| 0:10–0:40 | 30 min | Square lashing |
| 0:40–1:10 | 30 min | Diagonal lashing |
| 1:10–1:40 | 30 min | Sheer lashing |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Lashing strength test |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome & clove hitch recap (10 min)

Notices, opening. Every lashing today starts and finishes with a clove hitch, so a quick recap of it (from [Knots I](knots-i.md)) is worth the ten minutes.

### Square lashing (30 min)

Teach the square lashing (joining two spars at right angles, the workhorse of most pioneering builds) — frapping turns and all. Practice in pairs joining two short spars.

**Materials:** pioneering spars or broom handles, sisal or lashing cord.

### Diagonal lashing (30 min)

Teach the diagonal lashing (joining two spars that cross but don't quite touch, or that need pulling together first) — a good moment to contrast with the square lashing so Scouts know when to use which.

**Materials:** spars and cord from above.

### Sheer lashing (30 min)

Teach the sheer lashing (binding two or more spars side by side, e.g. to extend a pole's length or make an A-frame) — practice making a simple A-frame using two spars and a sheer lashing.

**Materials:** spars and cord from above.

### Lashing strength test (10 min)

Each patrol picks their best lashing from the session and puts it under gentle load (hanging weight, or two Scouts pulling) to see if it holds — a fun, physical way to check the work was done properly, not just for show.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Needs a decent supply of pioneering spars (or broom handles/canes as a cheaper substitute) and sisal or purpose-made lashing cord — more of both than a single knots session, since lashings use much more cord per join.
- Leaders should sign off each Scout against all three lashings during today's practice and the strength test, completing the "three or more different lashings" requirement.
