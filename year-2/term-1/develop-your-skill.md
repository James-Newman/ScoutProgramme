# Develop Your Skill

**Term:** Year 2, Term 1 — Creative Challenge

**Fits the term because:** follows directly from [Creative Taster Carousel](creative-taster-carousel.md) — now that every Scout has tried four creative activities, this session is where each one goes deeper on whichever they chose.

**Badge focus:** Creative Challenge Award requirement — developing skill further in one chosen creative activity.

> "Choose one of your creative activities and develop your skill further."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Regroup by activity |
| 0:20–1:40 | 80 min | Go deeper |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Share |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### Regroup by activity (10 min)

Scouts move into informal groups based on which of the four Creative Taster Carousel activities they chose (art, music, drama, or collage/recycled materials) — groups don't need to be even, some activities may attract more Scouts than others.

### Go deeper (80 min)

Each group spends real time properly developing their chosen skill, well beyond the taster-station level: the art group works on a full piece with proper technique instruction; the music group learns a real short piece or a more complex rhythm pattern; the drama group develops and rehearses a longer scene; the collage/recycled-materials group builds something more ambitious than the taster version. A leader with relevant knowledge in each area helps where possible — if no leader has a particular skill, bring in a helper, parent, or older Scout/Explorer who does, or lean on online tutorials as a fallback.

**Materials:** whatever the chosen activity needs at a deeper level — proper art materials, instruments, a bigger prop/costume box, more substantial recycled materials.

### Share (10 min)

Each group briefly shows or performs what they developed to the rest of the room — not a full performance (that's a later session), just a quick showcase of progress.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- "Develop your skill further" is a genuine step up from the taster session, not a repeat of it — the evidence should show real progression, not just more time spent on the same level of activity.
- Leaders should note each Scout's chosen activity and the progression shown as the evidence for this requirement.
