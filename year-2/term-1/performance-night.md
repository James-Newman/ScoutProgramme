# Performance Night

**Term:** Year 2, Term 1 — Creative Challenge

**Fits the term because:** the last of the Creative Challenge Award's six requirements, and a natural culmination of the term — Scouts perform using whatever art, drama, dance, or music skills they've built up this term.

**Badge focus:** Creative Challenge Award requirement — taking part in a performance.

> "Take part in a performance, using your art, drama, dance, or music skills."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:50 | 40 min | Prepare your piece |
| 0:50–1:40 | 50 min | Performance showcase |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Round of applause & reflect |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain the format: everyone performs something, in whatever art, drama, dance, or music form suits them.

### Prepare your piece (40 min)

Final prep time — Scouts who developed drama or music in [Develop Your Skill](develop-your-skill.md) polish that piece; others put together something new (a quick dance routine, a short musical piece, a dramatic reading, displaying and talking through an art piece as a form of performance/presentation). Patrols can perform individually or as a group act.

### Performance showcase (50 min)

Each Scout or group performs for the room. Keep the tone supportive and low-pressure — this is a Scout meeting showcase, not a competition. A simple compere role (a Scout volunteer) introducing each act works well and gives an extra Scout something to do.

### Round of applause & reflect (10 min)

Quick round: one thing each Scout enjoyed about performing or watching. Note this also marks the end of the six Creative Challenge Award requirements for anyone who's completed all of them across the term.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- "Performance" is deliberately broad here — displaying and talking through a piece of art counts, as does a short dance, a musical piece, or a dramatic reading, per the badge's own "art, drama, dance, or music" wording.
- Leaders should note who performed and in what form, as the evidence for this requirement — and use this session to do a final check that each Scout has now completed all six Creative Challenge Award requirements.
