# Build a Model

**Term:** Year 2, Term 1 — Creative Challenge

**Fits the term because:** the Creative Challenge Award's model-building requirement is distinct from the earlier collage taster station — this session is a fuller, standalone build, not a repeat of it.

**Badge focus:** Creative Challenge Award requirement — constructing a model from a kit or recycled/recyclable materials.

> "Construct a model using a kit or recycled/recyclable materials."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Pick your build |
| 0:20–1:40 | 80 min | Build it |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Show and tell |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### Pick your build (10 min)

Each Scout (or pair, if materials are limited) chooses between a kit-based model (a model kit the group already owns, or a simple one sourced for the session) or a recycled-materials build to a prompt (build something that moves, a model of a building, a creature, a vehicle). Either route satisfies the requirement — the badge doesn't require one over the other.

### Build it (80 min)

Scouts work on their chosen build. For kit builds, this is mostly independent assembly with a leader on hand for anyone stuck. For recycled builds, provide a decent pile of raw material (cardboard, plastic bottles, tubes, bottle tops, tape, glue) and let Scouts problem-solve their own construction.

**Materials:** model kits (simple, inexpensive ones — plastic construction kits, model vehicle kits) for those who choose that route; a substantial pile of clean recyclable materials (cardboard, bottles, tubes, tops), tape, glue, scissors for the other route.

### Show and tell (10 min)

Each Scout shows their finished (or in-progress, if it's a bigger build) model and briefly explains what they made and how.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- A build doesn't need to be finished perfectly in one session — a genuine, substantial attempt that's clearly a real model (not a half-hearted five-minute effort) satisfies the requirement.
- Leaders should note each Scout's finished or in-progress model as the evidence for this requirement.
