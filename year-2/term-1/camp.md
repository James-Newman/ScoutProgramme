# Camp

**Term:** Year 2, Term 1 — Creative Challenge

**Fits the term because:** every term includes one Camp regardless of theme — it's this term's Nights Away opportunity, and (per the standing convention for every term's Camp) the home for two Outdoor Challenge Award requirements that accumulate passively regardless of the term's own focus badge. By now patrols have had two terms' worth of campcraft sessions ([Tent Pitching](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/tent-pitching.md), [Campsite Selection](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/campsite-selection.md), [Site Layout & Design](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/site-layout-design.md), [Fire Safety & Camp Cooking Prep](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md)), so this camp should be close to fully patrol-led.

**Badge focus:** none for Creative Challenge Award — but this session is the standing home for two passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirements (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)) and this term's Nights Away (+2) staged-progression opportunity. A campfire evening is also a natural, low-pressure place to reuse a piece from this term's [Performance Night](performance-night.md), if any patrol wants to.

> "Take an active part in at least eight nights away as a Scout. Four of the nights should be camping."

> "Explore the environment of your camp and make sure you know where everything is. Respect the environment you are in and, at the end of the camp, leave the site as you found it."

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with a residential weekend (typically Friday evening to Sunday afternoon).

## Running order (example, Friday evening – Sunday)

| When | Block |
| --- | --- |
| Friday, arrival | Arrive, patrols pitch and lay out camp largely independently |
| Friday evening | Informal games, campfire chat |
| Saturday | Patrol-led activities, games, free time |
| Saturday evening | Cook and serve dinner as a patrol; campfire, optionally reusing a Performance Night piece |
| Sunday morning | Strike camp, patrol chores, pack away |
| Sunday, before departure | Debrief circle |

## Session detail

### Friday: arrival and settle-in

Patrols pitch and lay out camp with minimal leader input at this point. Walk each patrol round the whole site so they know where everything is (the "know where everything is" half of the passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirement).

### Saturday: camp in full swing

Patrol-led activities, games, chores rota.

### Saturday evening: cook, serve, campfire

Patrols cook and serve their evening meal, then a campfire to finish the day — a good, informal spot for anyone keen to reprise a Performance Night piece, though this is entirely optional and not required for the badge.

### Sunday: strike camp and debrief

Patrols strike and pack away camp independently, then walk their pitch before leaving — litter picked, ground checked, nothing left that wasn't there Friday (the "leave the site as you found it" half of the passive Outdoor Challenge Award requirement). Finish with a short debrief circle before parents arrive.

## Notes

- Needs a completed camp risk assessment and site booking well ahead of the term.
- Counts towards this term's Nights Away (+2) staged progression, and — combined with every other term's Camp — towards the Outdoor Challenge Award's Nights Away requirement.
