# Creative Taster Carousel

**Term:** Year 2, Term 1 — Creative Challenge

**Fits the term because:** the Creative Challenge Award's own wording asks for "four or more creative activities" — this session gives every Scout a genuine, hands-on taste of four distinct ones in a single sitting, setting up which one each Scout will go deeper on next session.

**Badge focus:** Creative Challenge Award requirement — taking part in four or more creative activities.

> "Take part in four or more creative activities. These could be linked to Scouts activity badges, but they don't have to be."

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Station A: Sketch & paint |
| 0:30–0:50 | 20 min | Station B: Rhythm & music |
| 0:50–1:10 | 20 min | Station C: Drama improv |
| 1:10–1:30 | 20 min | Station D: Recycled collage |
| 1:30–1:50 | 20 min | Which one's yours? |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain the carousel format and that next session, each Scout picks one of today's four to develop further.

### Station A: Sketch & paint (20 min)

Quick sketching or painting exercise — a still life, a portrait of a patrol member, or an abstract response to a piece of music played in the background. No pressure on finishing something polished; the point is trying the medium.

**Materials:** paper, pencils/paints, something to sketch or a music player.

### Station B: Rhythm & music (20 min)

Simple percussion using found objects (upturned buckets, pens on tables, body percussion) — teach a basic rhythm, then let patrols build their own short pattern and perform it back.

**Materials:** upturned buckets or similar improvised percussion, or real percussion instruments if available.

### Station C: Drama improv (20 min)

Short improv games — "yes, and" scenes, a one-word-at-a-time story acted out, or a quick scenario challenge ("you're stuck in a lift with a celebrity"). Keep it light and low-stakes; the aim is trying performance, not producing a polished scene.

### Station D: Recycled collage (20 min)

Using scrap paper, magazine cuttings, and other recyclable materials, each Scout builds a small collage or 3D piece around a simple prompt ("something from nature," "a machine," "your patrol"). This is a light taste of working with recycled materials, distinct from the fuller model-building session later in the term.

**Materials:** scrap paper, magazine cuttings, glue, scissors, other recyclable odds and ends.

### Which one's yours? (20 min)

Each Scout reflects on the four stations and picks the one they'd like to go deeper on next session — write it down or tell a leader, so sessions can be grouped sensibly next time. Discuss briefly as a group what each station involved, so everyone's clear on the options before committing.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Run stations as genuinely short and low-pressure — the requirement is "take part in," not "produce something impressive."
- Leaders should note which four activities each Scout genuinely took part in, and which one they chose to develop further, as the evidence for this requirement and the set-up for [Develop Your Skill](develop-your-skill.md).
