# Night Hike

**Term:** Year 2, Term 1 — Creative Challenge

**Fits the term because:** every term includes one Night Hike regardless of theme — it's this term's Hikes Away opportunity, and (per the standing convention for every term's Night Hike) the home for the Outdoor Challenge Award's Countryside Code requirement, which accumulates passively regardless of the term's own focus badge.

**Badge focus:** none for Creative Challenge Award — but this session is the standing home for the passive Outdoor Challenge Award Countryside Code requirement (see [`filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md`](../../filler-sessions/outdoor-challenge-award/README.md)) and this term's optional Hikes Away (+1) staged-progression opportunity. It's also the standing home for Navigator Staged Activity Badge's real navigated routes (see [`filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md`](../../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-3.md)/[`navigator-stage-4.md`](../../filler-sessions/navigator-stage-4.md)) — but since neither of those has been slotted into a term yet, no Scout should be expected to have the map-reading knowledge for a genuine navigating turn this early. If any Scout already does, give them the lead for part of the route.

> "Understand the three points of the Countryside Code. Show what action you can take to follow the code."

This isn't a standard 2-hour hall session — it replaces one week's meeting with an evening/early-night walk. Treat the running order below as a shape to adjust to local daylight and route length, not a fixed script.

## Running order (example, ~2.5 hours)

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Muster & kit check |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Route & safety brief |
| 0:30–1:30 | 60 min | The hike out |
| 1:30–1:50 | 20 min | Halfway stop |
| 1:50–2:30 | 40 min | The hike back |
| 2:30–2:45 | 15 min | Debrief & collection |

## Session detail

### Muster & kit check (10 min)

Meet at the hall (or the walk's start point), register patrols, and run a quick kit check per Scout — torch with spare batteries, footwear and coat suitable for the forecast, whistle, water. Confirm buddy pairs.

### Route & safety brief (20 min)

Walk the route on a map with the whole group before setting off. Cover the buddy system, what to do if separated, and the Countryside Code: leave gates as you find them, stick to paths, take litter home, keep dogs under control, be considerate of other people out at night. Official leaflets/posters are free to download from [gov.uk/countryside-code](https://www.gov.uk/countryside-code).

**Materials:** route map, torches, whistles.

### The hike out (60 min)

Walk the outbound leg in patrols, leaders spread front, middle, and back.

### Halfway stop (20 min)

A short break at a pre-scouted spot — hot chocolate from a flask, a quiet torch-lit game, a head-count.

### The hike back (40 min)

Return leg, ideally a different route or direction. Rotate who's leading at the front of each patrol.

### Debrief & collection (15 min)

Head-count, a quick "what went well" round while parents arrive, and a reminder that this counts towards Hikes Away progression.

## Notes

- Needs a pre-walked, risk-assessed route and a completed risk assessment for night activities per group policy.
- Adult:young person ratios, hi-vis, and torch requirements should follow the group's own safety policy for night activities.
- Leaders should note engagement with the Countryside Code scenarios and whether any Scout got a genuine navigating turn, as the evidence for those two passive requirements.
