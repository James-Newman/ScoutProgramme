# Year 2, Term 1 (Autumn) — Creative Challenge

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 2, term 1) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: trying out four or more creative activities over time, developing a real skill in one of them, building a model from a kit or recycled materials, using social media/the internet creatively and safely, and taking part in a performance.
Badges earned: **Creative Challenge Award**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Hikes Away (+1).

**Status: framework only.** This term hasn't been built out session-by-session yet — this file just records the theme and focus badge so the rest of the 4-year plan stays consistent. See `data/terms.yaml` for the source of truth.

## Badge requirement(s)

Source: [Scouts Creative Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/challenge-awards/creative-challenge/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

> "Take part in four or more creative activities. These could be linked to Scouts activity badges, but they don't have to be."

> "Choose one of your creative activities and develop your skill further."

> "Use your creative ability to help promote a Scouting activity or event."

> "Construct a model using a kit or recycled/recyclable materials."

> "Use social media or the internet to be creative, and know how to stay safe when doing this."

> "Take part in a performance, using your art, drama, dance, or music skills."
