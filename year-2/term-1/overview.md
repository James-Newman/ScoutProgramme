# Year 2, Term 1 (Autumn) — Creative Challenge

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 2, term 1) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: trying out four or more creative activities, developing a real skill in one of them, using creative ability to promote a Scouting activity or event, using social media/the internet creatively and safely, building a model from a kit or recycled materials, and taking part in a performance.
Badges earned: **Creative Challenge Award**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Hikes Away (+1).

Every term's 12 weeks follow the same shape: one [Welcome Session](welcome-session.md), one [Night Hike](night-hike.md), one [Camp](camp.md), one [External Activity](external-activity.md), and one [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md) session — five fixed slots every term, regardless of theme. Autumn terms add two more standing slots: one [Shooting Session](shooting-session.md) and one [Little O](little-o-orienteering.md). That's 7 fixed slots this term, leaving five weeks for the term's own badge content.

**This term needs all 5 of those free weeks for Creative Challenge Award itself.** The Award's six requirements split naturally across five sessions: [Creative Taster Carousel](creative-taster-carousel.md) covers "four or more creative activities" in one sitting via a four-station carousel, [Develop Your Skill](develop-your-skill.md) takes each Scout deeper into whichever station they chose, [Promote Online, Safely](promote-online-safely.md) combines the promote-an-event and stay-safe-online requirements (they're naturally the same task), [Build a Model](build-a-model.md) is a fuller standalone build distinct from the carousel's lighter collage station, and [Performance Night](performance-night.md) closes out the sixth requirement and doubles as a final check that every Scout has completed all six. **No one-sitting filler badge is used this term** — with six requirements needing five genuine sessions, all of the term's free capacity is legitimately spoken for by the focus badge itself, unlike Year 1's terms which had spare weeks to spend on Naturalist, Air Spotter, Writer, or Communicator.

## Requirement coverage

All 6 Creative Challenge Award requirements are covered this term:

> "Take part in four or more creative activities. These could be linked to Scouts activity badges, but they don't have to be." — [Creative Taster Carousel](creative-taster-carousel.md)
>
> "Choose one of your creative activities and develop your skill further." — [Develop Your Skill](develop-your-skill.md)
>
> "Use your creative ability to help promote a Scouting activity or event." — [Promote Online, Safely](promote-online-safely.md)
>
> "Use social media or the internet to be creative, and know how to stay safe when doing this." — [Promote Online, Safely](promote-online-safely.md)
>
> "Construct a model using a kit or recycled/recyclable materials." — [Build a Model](build-a-model.md)
>
> "Take part in a performance, using your art, drama, dance, or music skills." — [Performance Night](performance-night.md)

Source: [Scouts Creative Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/challenge-awards/creative-challenge/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

## Advance preparation

Pulled from each session's own Notes — everything a leader needs to organise ahead of running this term, not just on the day.

### Bookings & external arrangements

- **[External Activity](external-activity.md)** — book well ahead, since popular slots get booked up. Pick something different from previous terms' bookings so it counts as a genuinely new activity towards the Adventure Challenge Award tally.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** site booking — well ahead of the term.
- **[Shooting Session](shooting-session.md)** — a qualified instructor and a suitable range, either an external provider or a leader holding the relevant NGB qualification.
- **[Little O](little-o-orienteering.md)** — three pre-set courses of increasing difficulty in the local park/woods; check whether the local park already has a permanent course installed.

### Risk assessments

- **[Night Hike](night-hike.md)** — a pre-walked, risk-assessed route, plus a completed risk assessment for night activities.
- **[Camp](camp.md)** — a completed camp risk assessment.
- **[Shooting Session](shooting-session.md)** — following the group's own safety policy for shooting activities.
- **[Little O](little-o-orienteering.md)** — a risk assessment for the specific venue.

### Content to source or prepare carefully

- **[Promote Online, Safely](promote-online-safely.md)** — the "staying safe online" block needs a leader confident on the Group's own social media policy (official channels, image consent, no identifying details of young people) before running it.
- **[Little O](little-o-orienteering.md)** — check which Scouts already earned Orienteer Activity Badge in Year 1, Term 1, so the session's own sign-off doesn't assume everyone needs a fresh one.
- **[Shooting Session](shooting-session.md)** — carry forward each Scout's running tally from Year 1, Term 1 towards the six-session minimum, and note whether this occurrence should be run as the one formally supervised contest.

### Equipment & materials to source

See the [material inventory](#material-inventory) below for the full list — the standouts needing advance sourcing are model kits and a substantial pile of clean recyclable materials (Build a Model), and phones/tablets for the digital half of Promote Online, Safely.

Two badges complete in full this term — order stock for both from the [Scout Store](https://shop.scouts.org.uk/) ahead of [End of Term Fun & Games](end-of-term-fun-and-games.md):

- **Orienteer Activity Badge** — completed via [Little O](little-o-orienteering.md), for any Scout who hadn't already earned it in Year 1, Term 1. Check individual records before ordering, since not every Scout will need a fresh badge.

**Not this term:** Master at Arms Activity Badge remains mid-progress (two of at least six Shooting Sessions so far) — not a badge to stock yet.
