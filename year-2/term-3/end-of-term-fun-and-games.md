# End of Term Fun & Games

**Term:** Year 2, Term 3 — Global Issues

**Fits the term because:** closes the term with a celebration rather than more assessment — the badge work is already done and logged from [Clothing & Belongings: Made Where?](clothing-and-belongings-made-where.md), [Natural Disaster Investigation](natural-disaster-investigation.md), [International Sport & Peace](international-sport-and-peace.md), [International Health Issue: Creative Share](international-health-issue-creative-share.md), and [Rights Around the World](rights-around-the-world.md).

**Badge focus:** none — badge sign-off happened earlier in the term; this session just presents what's already been earned.

Like every term's closing session, this one is deliberately light on new content — a quick badge presentation, then a run of daft, low-prep Taskmaster-style challenges.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Badge presentation |
| 0:20–1:50 | 90 min | Taskmaster challenges |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, patrol flags, opening.

### Badge presentation (10 min)

Roll call by patrol, each Scout called up individually to receive every badge they've completed in full this term: **Global Issues Activity Badge** (this term's own focus badge) and **Media Relations & Marketing Activity Badge** (via [Media Relations & Marketing](../../filler-sessions/media-relations-and-marketing.md)). This is a Summer term, so there's no Shooting Session or Little O this time round — Master at Arms and Orienteer simply aren't in play this term. Patrol applause, a quick photo, then move straight on.

**Materials:** badges/certificates.

### Taskmaster challenges (90 min)

Run five or six short, silly, low-prep challenges in patrols, Taskmaster-TV-show style — score each out of five, keep a running scoreboard on the wall. A mix of pure silliness and a light nod back to the term's themes works well:

- **World flag relay** — patrols race to correctly match a shuffled set of flag cards to their countries.
- **[Human sculpture](https://www.icebreakerspot.com/activities/human-sculptures)** — patrols freeze into a shape representing a given word, other patrols guess.
- **[Tallest tower](https://www.icebreakerspot.com/activities/newspaper-tower-challenge)** — build the tallest free-standing tower out of paper and tape in three minutes.
- **Water carry** — carry the most water across the room in a colander in one trip.
- **Patrol anthem** — three minutes to write and perform a one-verse patrol song about the term.

Leaders judge, deadpan, TV-host style.

**Materials:** [flag cards](../../resources/flag-cards.md), paper and pens, tape, a colander and water.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home, and a teaser for Year 3, Term 1: Skills & Self-Reliance.

## Notes

- Swap any challenge for a local favourite — the format matters more than the specific tasks.
- This session's shape is designed to be reused unchanged as every future term's closing session.
