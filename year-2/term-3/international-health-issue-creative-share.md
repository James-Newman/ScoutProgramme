# International Health Issue: Creative Share

**Term:** Year 2, Term 3 — Global Issues

**Fits the term because:** the badge's own wording explicitly asks for a creative way of sharing what's learned, giving this session a different shape from the term's more discussion-based sessions.

**Badge focus:** Global Issues Activity Badge requirement — learning about an international health issue and creatively sharing it with the Troop.

> "In small groups, learn about an international health issue and use a creative way to share what you have learnt with your Troop" (examples include Malaria, Ebola, HIV and Aids).

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Choosing an issue |
| 0:20–0:50 | 30 min | Learn about it |
| 0:50–1:35 | 45 min | Prepare the creative share |
| 1:35–1:55 | 20 min | Share with the Troop |
| 1:55–2:00 | 5 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Explain that today's requirement is specifically about sharing creatively, not just researching — leave real time for the second half.

### Choosing an issue (10 min)

Each small group picks one international health issue — the badge's own examples (Malaria, Ebola, HIV and Aids) are good starting points, but any genuine international health issue works. Encourage variety across groups.

### Learn about it (30 min)

Groups research their chosen issue: what it is, where it's most prevalent, and what's being done to address it (vaccination campaigns, awareness programmes, medical research).

**Materials:** internet access (phones/tablets/laptops), or pre-printed briefing notes as a fallback.

### Prepare the creative share (45 min)

Each group prepares a creative way of sharing what they've learned — a short skit, a poster or infographic, a rap or song, a quiz for the room, or a simple game that teaches the key facts. The creativity is the actual requirement here, not just the research.

**Materials:** poster paper/card, pens, and whatever else each group's chosen format needs — props for a skit, a laptop/tablet for a slideshow.

### Share with the Troop (20 min)

Each group presents their creative share to the rest of the room.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Push groups towards a genuinely creative format rather than a straight verbal report — a skit, quiz, or poster session all satisfy the requirement far better than reading facts off a slide.
- Leaders should note each group's chosen issue and the format of their creative share, as the evidence for this requirement.
