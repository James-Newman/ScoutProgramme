# Troop Action Day

**Term:** Year 2, Term 3 — Global Issues

**Fits the term because:** follows through on whichever help proposal the Troop picked in [Natural Disaster Investigation](natural-disaster-investigation.md) — turning a discussion into a genuine, if modest, action.

**Badge focus:** none required by Global Issues Activity Badge itself — this is a deliberate enrichment session, following through on the badge's "determine how your Troop can assist" wording with a real (if small-scale) action, rather than leaving it as a discussion exercise.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:30 | 20 min | Plan the action |
| 0:30–1:40 | 70 min | Run the action |
| 1:40–1:55 | 15 min | Reflect |
| 1:55–2:00 | 5 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening. Recap which proposal the group picked at the end of Natural Disaster Investigation.

### Plan the action (20 min)

In patrols, plan the practical details of the action for today — this varies entirely based on what got picked, but examples that work within a single 2-hour session and don't need external logistics include: a sponsored silence or challenge during the session with donations collected from parents at pickup, making and decorating donation collection boxes to take home and use over the coming weeks, writing a batch of letters or a short awareness campaign (posters, a social post draft) about the chosen disaster, or packing a small batch of donated items (tinned food, warm clothing) already gathered by patrols in the run-up to this session.

### Run the action (70 min)

Carry out the planned action as a group or in patrols.

**Materials:** varies entirely by chosen action — craft materials for collection boxes/posters, or donated items if a collection was organised in advance.

### Reflect (15 min)

Quick round: what did the group actually do, and what real difference (even a small one) might it make. Note down the practical follow-up if the action needs one (banking a collection, sending letters, posting content) — someone needs to own it after today.

### Close (5 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Keep the chosen action realistic for a single meeting — anything needing external bookings or weeks of lead time belongs in [External Activity](external-activity.md) territory, not here.
- If the action needs a follow-up after the session (posting collection boxes out, banking money raised), assign it to a specific leader before closing, so it doesn't quietly drop.
- Leaders should note what action the Troop actually took, as evidence that the "determine how your Troop can assist" requirement led to something real, not just a discussion.
