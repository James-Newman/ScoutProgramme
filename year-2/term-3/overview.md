## Badge requirement(s)

Source: [Scouts Global Issues Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/global-issues/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

> "Identify where your clothing or other belongings have been made" and discuss with other Scouts why companies choose overseas production and its advantages/disadvantages.

> "Investigate a recent natural disaster by looking at photos, news articles or videos online" and decide in small groups what five things were needed to help, then determine how your Troop can assist.

> "Find out about an international sporting event and discuss with your Troop how sporting events can contribute to international peace and understanding."

> "In small groups, learn about an international health issue and use a creative way to share what you have learnt with your Troop" (examples include Malaria, Ebola, HIV and Aids).

> "Think about the rights that we have in the UK (like the right to vote, the right to go to school and equal gender rights)" and compare these with rights a Scout in another country might have.
