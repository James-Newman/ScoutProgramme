# Year 2, Term 3 (Summer) — On the Water

Term summary: [`data/terms.yaml`](../../data/terms.yaml) (year 2, term 3) · [`docs/timeline.md`](../../docs/timeline.md)

Core goals: identifying different paddle craft, naming safe local canoeing/kayaking locations, a warm-up activity, dressing appropriately with a buoyancy aid, and a taster session covering equipment, getting in and out safely, balancing, and manoeuvring.
Badges earned: **Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge (Stage 1)**.
Optional staged progression: Nights Away (+2), Time on the Water.

**Status: framework only.** This term hasn't been built out session-by-session yet — this file just records the theme and focus badge so the rest of the 3-year plan stays consistent. See `data/terms.yaml` for the source of truth.

This term resolves a TBD flag left open at the top of `data/terms.yaml`: this badge was displaced from Year 1, Term 3 when that term became Fire & Cooking, and hadn't been re-slotted anywhere until now. It was previously mis-recorded as a flat "Paddle Sports Activity Badge" — research for this term found that badge doesn't exist; it's actually a staged badge, so it's recorded here (and in `data/awards.yaml`) as Stage 1, the natural starting stage for Scouts with no prior paddle sports experience.

## Badge requirement(s)

Source: [Scouts Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge, Stage 1](https://www.scouts.org.uk/staged-badges/paddlesports/stage-1) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated by the Scout Association.

There are two options for reaching Stage 1; the group is expected to use Option 1 (Option 2 is an automatic award to anyone who already holds British Canoeing's Paddle Start Award).

> "Identify the different types of craft used in canoeing and kayaking."

> "Name three places where it's safe to go canoeing or kayaking near you."

> "Take part in a warm up activity before you get on the water."

> "Show you know how to dress appropriately for canoeing or kayaking, including wearing a buoyancy aid."

> "Take part in a taster session that covers: naming the equipment you're using; getting in and out of your craft safely; balancing while on the water; moving your craft around (manoeuvring)."
