# Scout Programme

A 3-year plan for a UK Scout Troop (ages 10–14). Scouts work towards real Scout Association badges during regular weekly meetings, with camps, hikes, and other activities layered on top.

## How this is organised

Each term runs for 12 weeks. Every term opens with a Welcome Session and closes with an End of Term Fun & Games session, and always includes one Night Hike, one Camp, and one fun External Activity (plus, in Autumn terms, a Little O orienteering session) — the remaining weeks cover that term's own badge content, aiming to complete one badge in full within the term. Several badges are never given a dedicated term at all, each covered a different way: Outdoor Challenge Award, Adventure Challenge Award, Teamwork Challenge Award, Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge (Stage 1), Master at Arms Activity Badge, and Navigator Staged Activity Badge (Stage 3/4) are earned passively through the standing Camp/Night Hike/Welcome/External Activity slots every term already has plus a handful of standalone sessions in [Filler sessions](filler-sessions/README.md); Expedition Challenge Award is covered by the standing summer Camp and/or an additional annual hike, with each Scout writing their own report afterward; and Team Leader Challenge Award and Personal Challenge Award are reviewed with Scouts and leaders each term instead of via any dedicated session. All 9 real Scout Challenge Awards now have a home this way, with no outstanding gaps. Separately, a term's regular content sometimes also happens to advance requirements of other badges without any dedicated time for them; where that's a genuine match, it's called out as "passive completion" in that term's overview so it's not double-counted or overstated. [Year 1, Term 1](year-1/term-1/overview.md) is built out in full so far; the rest of the 3-year plan currently exists at a summary level in the [timeline](docs/timeline.md), and will be filled in with the same level of detail over time.
