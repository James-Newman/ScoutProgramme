# Scout Programme

A 4-year plan for a UK Scout Troop (ages 10–14). Scouts work towards real Scout Association badges during regular weekly meetings, with camps, hikes, and other activities layered on top.

## Where to start

- **[Timeline](docs/timeline.md)** — the whole 4-year, 12-term plan at a glance: what each term is about, and which badges get earned when.
- **[Awards summary](docs/awards-summary.md)** — every badge and award a Scout earns by sticking with the programme, in one place.
- **[Year 1, Term 1](year-1/term-1/overview.md)** — the first term planned in full detail, session by session, including what to bring to each one.
- **[Resources](resources/README.md)** — printable cards, checklists, and templates for running the sessions.
- **[Filler sessions](filler-sessions/README.md)** — standalone sessions to drop into a term that runs a week or two longer than planned.

## How this is organised

Each term runs for 12 weeks. Every term opens with a Welcome Session and closes with an End of Term Fun & Games session, and always includes one Night Hike, one Camp, and one fun External Activity — the other seven weeks cover that term's own badge content. [Year 1, Term 1](year-1/term-1/overview.md) is the first term built out this way; the rest of the 4-year plan currently exists at a summary level in the [timeline](docs/timeline.md), and will be filled in with the same level of detail over time.
