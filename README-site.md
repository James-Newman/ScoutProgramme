# Scout Programme

A 4-year plan for a UK Scout Troop (ages 10–14). Scouts work towards real Scout Association badges during regular weekly meetings, with camps, hikes, and other activities layered on top.

## Where to start

- **[Timeline](docs/timeline.md)** — the whole 4-year, 12-term plan at a glance: what each term is about, and which badges get earned when.
- **[Awards summary](docs/awards-summary.md)** — every badge and award a Scout earns by sticking with the programme, in one place.
- **[Year 1, Term 1](year-1/term-1/overview.md)** — World Challenge Award, the first term planned in full detail, session by session, including what to bring to each one.
- **[Year 1, Term 2](year-1/term-2/overview.md)** and **[Year 1, Term 3](year-1/term-3/overview.md)** — First Aid (Emergency Aid Stage 3) and Fire & Cooking (Chef Activity Badge). Framework only so far — theme and focus badge decided, session-by-session detail still to come.
- **[Resources](resources/README.md)** — printable cards, checklists, and templates for running the sessions.
- **[Filler sessions](filler-sessions/README.md)** — standalone sessions that sit outside the 12-term plan: some complete a full badge in one sitting, others (Outdoor, Adventure, and Teamwork Challenge Awards) are earned passively across many terms instead of ever getting a dedicated term.

## How this is organised

Each term runs for 12 weeks. Every term opens with a Welcome Session and closes with an End of Term Fun & Games session, and always includes one Night Hike, one Camp, and one fun External Activity (plus, in Autumn terms, a Little O orienteering session) — the remaining weeks cover that term's own badge content, aiming to complete one badge in full within the term. Three badges — Outdoor Challenge Award, Adventure Challenge Award, and Teamwork Challenge Award — are never given a dedicated term at all; they're earned passively through the standing Camp/Night Hike/Welcome/External Activity slots every term already has, plus a handful of standalone sessions in [Filler sessions](filler-sessions/README.md). Separately, a term's regular content sometimes also happens to advance requirements of other badges without any dedicated time for them; where that's a genuine match, it's called out as "passive completion" in that term's overview so it's not double-counted or overstated. [Year 1, Term 1](year-1/term-1/overview.md) is built out in full so far; the rest of the 4-year plan currently exists at a summary level in the [timeline](docs/timeline.md), and will be filled in with the same level of detail over time.
