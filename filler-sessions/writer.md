# Writer Activity Badge

**Badge:** [Scouts Writer Activity Badge](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/activity-badges/writer/)

**Why it's a good filler:** the badge is "complete four of these [eight] activities" — picking the four below needs no audience, no external person, and no take-home time. It sits entirely outside any term's theme, which is exactly what a filler slot needs.

**Adaptation needed:** none. All four tasks are chosen from the badge's own published list, including its own explicit "real or imaginary" option for the pen pal.

**Requirements completed this session:**

> "Compose a poem of at least eight lines and discuss its meaning and construction"
>
> "Write a play or dramatic sketch lasting at least 10 minutes"
>
> "Write a descriptive passage of around 600 words on a subject, agreed with an appropriate adult beforehand, or write a 600-word review of a favourite book, play or other work of literature"
>
> "Write a letter to a pen pal (real or imaginary) of at least 600 words"

The badge also requires: "talk about your choices with an appropriate adult" — built into the closing block below.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | The writer's menu |
| 0:20–0:35 | 15 min | Poem |
| 0:35–1:00 | 25 min | Descriptive passage or review |
| 1:00–1:20 | 20 min | Play or dramatic sketch |
| 1:20–1:40 | 20 min | Letter to a pen pal |
| 1:40–1:50 | 10 min | Talk about your choices |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### The writer's menu (10 min)

Explain the four pieces coming up and roughly how long each should take. Scouts write individually throughout — there's no need for group work here. Set the expectation early that "around 600 words" is a guide, not a strict cut-off; a good, complete piece a bit short of 600 words beats a padded one that hits the number.

### Poem (15 min)

At least eight lines, any subject, any style (rhyming or not). When time's up, ask a few Scouts to read theirs and briefly discuss what it's about and how it's built (rhyme scheme, rhythm, why they chose the words they did) — that discussion is part of the requirement, not optional extra.

### Descriptive passage or review (25 min)

Scouts choose one: a descriptive passage on a subject they agree with a leader in the first minute or two (a place, a person, an object), or a review of a favourite book, play, film, or game. Around 600 words.

### Play or dramatic sketch (20 min)

A written play or dramatic sketch lasting at least 10 minutes when performed — the requirement is the writing, not a performance, so there's no need to actually act it out this session. A two-hander or monologue scene works well solo; encourage stage directions as well as dialogue.

### Letter to a pen pal (20 min)

At least 600 words, to a real or imaginary pen pal — the badge explicitly allows imaginary, so nobody needs an actual pen pal lined up. Suggest a hook if anyone's stuck: "write to a pen pal in another country describing what Scouts is like."

### Talk about your choices (10 min)

In small groups or one-to-one with a leader, each Scout talks briefly through their four pieces — what they wrote, why, what they'd change. This is the "talk about your choices with an appropriate adult" requirement, and it's also where a leader confirms all four pieces are genuinely complete.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Materials

Paper and pens (or laptops/tablets if the group has them), a few subject prompts on hand for Scouts who get stuck on the descriptive passage or the pen-pal letter.

## Notes

- Requirements can be adapted to suit each young person's abilities, per the Scout Association's own stated policy — a Scout who struggles with handwriting can dictate to a leader who scribes, for example.
- Keep the four written pieces (or photos of them) as the evidence for sign-off.
