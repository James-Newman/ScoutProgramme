# Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge (Stage 1)

**Badge:** [Scouts Paddle Sports Staged Activity Badge, Stage 1](https://www.scouts.org.uk/staged-badges/paddlesports/stage-1) — mostly passive, with one hall session covering the non-water requirements.

**Why this works as a filler:** every summer term already includes water-based content via the standing "Time on the Water" optional-progression slot and/or that term's External Activity booking, so the two requirements that genuinely need real water access get covered whenever that recurring opportunity happens to be a paddle sports session — no dedicated term is needed to make that happen, the group runs it every summer regardless. This session delivers the three requirements that don't need water access at all.

**Requirements this session covers:**

> "Identify the different types of craft used in canoeing and kayaking."

> "Name three places where it's safe to go canoeing or kayaking near you."

> "Show you know how to dress appropriately for canoeing or kayaking, including wearing a buoyancy aid."

**Requirements covered passively elsewhere:**

> "Take part in a warm up activity before you get on the water." — happens immediately before whichever summer term's Time on the Water/External Activity slot is a paddle sports session.

> "Take part in a taster session that covers: naming the equipment you're using; getting in and out of your craft safely; balancing while on the water; moving your craft around (manoeuvring)." — covered by that same recurring water session.

## Running order

| Time | Duration | Block |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:10 | 10 min | Welcome |
| 0:10–0:20 | 10 min | Tally check |
| 0:20–0:50 | 30 min | Craft & venues |
| 0:50–1:30 | 40 min | Dress & buoyancy aid |
| 1:30–1:50 | 20 min | Look ahead to the water |
| 1:50–2:00 | 10 min | Close |

## Session detail

### Welcome (10 min)

Notices, opening.

### Tally check (10 min)

Quick round: has each Scout already had a Time on the Water paddle sports session this term, or is one booked? This session works best run beforehand, so Scouts arrive at the real water session already knowing their kit and venues — but it still covers real requirements either way if run afterward instead.

### Craft & venues (30 min)

Using pictures or real kit if available, Scouts learn to tell apart the different craft types used in canoeing and kayaking (open canoe, sit-in kayak, sit-on-top kayak, and so on). Then, using local knowledge or a bit of research, each Scout names three places nearby where it's safe to go canoeing or kayaking — this can reuse whatever venue the group's own summer paddling session actually happens at, plus two others.

### Dress & buoyancy aid (40 min)

Hands-on with real buoyancy aids if the group has them (borrow from whoever's running the summer water session if not): correct fitting, checking buckles and straps, why cotton/jeans are a bad idea on the water, and what else to wear for canoeing or kayaking (footwear, layers, sun/cold protection as appropriate). Every Scout should be able to properly fit and check a buoyancy aid by the end of this block, not just describe one.

### Look ahead to the water (20 min)

Preview what the actual paddling session will involve — naming the equipment they'll use, how to get in and out of a craft safely, balancing, and manoeuvring — so Scouts arrive primed. This block doesn't itself satisfy the taster-session or warm-up requirements (those need real water), it just sets expectations.

### Close (10 min)

Notices, thought for the week, home.

## Notes

- Leaders should note which Scouts have completed this hall session and which have had their on-water taster/warm-up as the evidence for the five requirements combined — this repo doesn't track individual Scout progress, only where each requirement gets covered.
- If a summer term's Time on the Water slot ends up being something other than canoeing/kayaking (e.g. paddleboarding) in a given year, the two water-dependent requirements above won't be covered that term — flag it back to whoever's booking External Activities rather than trying to fix it in this session.
