# Outdoor Challenge Award — Filler Sessions

Unlike the badges in the parent [`filler-sessions/`](../README.md) directory, the Outdoor Challenge Award can't be completed in one sitting — it has 10 fixed requirements plus "complete at least four" optional extras, and the group has decided to earn it **passively**, accumulated across many terms via camps, hikes, and a handful of standalone skill sessions, rather than dedicating a whole term to it. Slot the sessions below into any term that has room, in any order, over as many terms as it takes.

Source: [Scouts Outdoor Challenge Award](https://www.scouts.org.uk/scouts/awards/outdoor/) — check the live page before formally signing anything off, since requirement wording is occasionally updated.

## Requirement map

| # | Requirement | Where it gets covered |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | "Take an active part in at least eight nights away as a Scout. Four of the nights should be camping." | **Passive** — every term's standing Camp contributes 2 nights; accumulates over ~4 terms regardless of that term's own focus badge. |
| 2 | "With others, pitch and strike your tent." | [`tent-pitching.md`](tent-pitching.md) |
| 3 | "Lead, or help to lead, a group of Scouts to set up a well-organised site..." | [`site-layout-design.md`](site-layout-design.md) (prep: [`campsite-selection.md`](campsite-selection.md)) |
| 4 | "Prepare and light an open fire or set up a suitable stove. Use it to prepare, cook and serve a meal safely." | [`fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md`](fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md) — also a strong passive match if it lands during a Chef Activity Badge term |
| 5 | "Understand the three points of the Countryside Code..." | **Passive** — every term's standing Night Hike touches on this (see the Night Hike convention in `AGENTS.md`). |
| 6 | "Find out why personal and campsite hygiene is important..." | [`camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md`](camp-hygiene-and-food-safety.md) |
| 7 | "Using knots that you have learned, build a simple pioneering project, object or camp gadget." | **Not yet covered.** No dedicated session exists; the natural home is a future term that already teaches knots (e.g. a Pioneer Activity Badge term), or a short dedicated filler if one never comes up. |
| 8 | "Explore the environment of your camp... leave the site as you found it." | **Passive** — every term's standing Camp includes a site walk on arrival and a leave-no-trace check before departure (see the Camp convention in `AGENTS.md`). |
| 9 | "Find out what accidents and incidents can happen outdoors or during your camp..." | [`outdoor-safety-and-risk-awareness.md`](outdoor-safety-and-risk-awareness.md) |
| 10 | "Show how to use an axe, saw or knife safely..." | [`fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md`](fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md) |
| 11 | "Complete at least four of these tasks:" (optional extras) | Rucksack packing — [`camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md`](camp-kit-and-personal-equipment.md); menu planning — at whichever camp comes next; campfire entertainment and a backwoods meal — both in [`fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md`](fire-safety-and-camp-cooking.md). That's 4 — the requirement is met once all four sessions/camps have happened. |

## Notes

- These 6 session files were originally built as a dedicated Year 1, Term 2 term before the group decided to make this badge passive instead — the content itself didn't need to change, only the framing (no more "Term:"/"Fits the term because:" fields, no hard links to a specific term's Camp/Night Hike/Welcome Session).
- Leaders should track individual Scout progress against the table above themselves (e.g. in OSM) — this repo doesn't track per-Scout completion state, only what each session covers.
- Requirement 7 (pioneering gadget) is the one genuine gap — flag it if a full term goes by without a natural opportunity to pick it up.
